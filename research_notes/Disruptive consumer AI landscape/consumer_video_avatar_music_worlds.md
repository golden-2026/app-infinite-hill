# Consumer AI: video, avatars and celebrity likeness, music, games and living worlds, AR/spatial (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: about 18 web searches and fetches. Many figures come from secondary trackers (Sacra, getlatka, morphed, blogs) and are marked as such. Primary or near-primary sources: TechCrunch, Billboard, MBW, Deadline, Bloomberg, Kuaishou filings, Google, Snap, and the 9to5Google/Gizmodo product reporting.

## AI video/film for consumers: Sora, Veo/Flow, Runway, Kling, Pika, Hedra, Captions, CapCut, micro-dramas, cameo features

### Takeaway
The biggest 2025 consumer "jaw-drop" was OpenAI's Sora app, where you put yourself in the video through cameos. It went viral and then died. OpenAI announced the shutdown on Mar 24 2026 and switched it off on Apr 26 2026, after downloads fell by about two thirds and in-app revenue reached only about $2.1M. The money in AI video is in creator and prosumer tools (Kling about $500M ARR, Higgsfield, Runway, HeyGen) and in AI-fed micro-drama apps, not in AI-only social feeds. The cameo idea proved the "wow", but a feed made only of AI clips did not keep people coming back.

### Cited Findings
**Sora (OpenAI)**
- Sora app: the iOS app had over 1M installs in its first week, and Android had about 470K downloads on day one (Appfigures) — [Yahoo/AT&T](https://currently.att.yahoo.com/att/android-users-downloaded-openais-sora-223027571.html); [CO/AI](https://getcoai.com/news/openais-sora-app-hits-1m-downloads-in-5-days-despite-copyright-concerns/)
- Downloads peaked at about 3.33M a month in Nov 2025 (iOS+Play) and fell to about 1.13M by Feb 2026. Lifetime in-app purchase revenue was only about $2.1M (Appfigures) — [TechCrunch, Mar 24 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/)
- OpenAI announced the shutdown on Mar 24 2026 and gave no reason. The Sora 2 model stays available only behind the ChatGPT paywall. Disney's planned $1B investment and character-licensing deal (Disney/Marvel/Pixar/Star Wars characters in Sora) ended with the app, and no money appears to have changed hands — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/)
- The app and website were switched off on Apr 26 2026 — [ForkOff summary](https://forkoff.xyz/blog/viral-launch/sora-2-viral-launch-and-shutdown-2026); [FelloAI](https://felloai.com/how-to-download-sora-2-app-use-cameo-outside-us-25-prompts-ready-to-try/) (secondary)
- "Cameos" let users put their own likeness into AI clips and could be made public so that others could use your face. TechCrunch called it "the creepiest app on your phone". After a trademark dispute with the company Cameo, the feature was renamed "characters" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/); [search summary of TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3065682)

**Meta Vibes**
- Meta Vibes, an AI-only short-video feed in the Meta AI app, launched Sep 25 2025. It is powered partly by Black Forest Labs, offers remix and share-to-Reels, expanded to Europe, and was widely mocked as "AI slop". No official user numbers were found — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/25/meta-launches-vibes-a-short-form-video-feed-of-ai-slop); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/meta-vibes-expands-to-europe-ai-app/)

**Google Veo/Flow**
- Google Flow (the AI filmmaking app on Veo, Imagen and Gemini) launched May 20 2025. Veo 3.1 is in Flow and in the Gemini app, with "Ingredients to Video" and portrait mode — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/veo-3-1-ingredients-to-video/); [Google blog: Flow updates](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/veo-updates-flow/); [Wikipedia: Veo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veo_(text-to-video_model))
- Unverified secondary claim: the Gemini app now uses a newer "Gemini Omni" video model, and Veo 3/Veo 2 were retired on Jun 30 2026 — [camclo3d](https://camclo3d.com/blog/veo-3-pricing). No figures were found for how many videos consumers generate.

**Kling (Kuaishou)**
- Kling, part of Kuaishou, is the clearest revenue proof. Revenue was over RMB650M in Q1 2026 (more than 300% up year on year) and over RMB850M in Q2 2026 (more than 200% up). ARR was about $500M in March 2026, roughly 4x a year earlier, up from $240M in Dec 2025. There were 60M+ registered creators and 600M+ videos by Dec 2025. Kuaishou is weighing a restructuring with outside financing (announced May 12 2026) — [iTiger/Kuaishou results](https://www.itiger.com/news/1160238640); [Kuaishou Q1 release](https://www.daytraders.com/news/2026/05/27/kuaishou-technology-announces-first-quarter-2026-unaudited-financial-results); [KrASIA](https://amp.kr-asia.com/kuaishous-kling-ai-seeks-fresh-funding-ahead-of-planned-2027-ipo)
- About 75% of Kling's revenue reportedly comes from outside China (secondary) — [iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1119092134)

**Creator and prosumer tools**
- Runway: valued at about $5.3B after a $315M Series E led by General Atlantic (Feb 10 2026). ARR reports conflict: about $300M annualized by Oct 2025 versus "$200M ARR in Sep 2026" — [morphed](https://morphed.app/stats/runway-statistics); [Quantumrun](https://www.quantumrun.com/consulting/runway-ml-revenue/) (secondary, conflicting)
- Higgsfield, a consumer and creator video app known for viral camera-motion presets: reported $400M Series B at $5.4B, and the company says annualized revenue was $700M in Aug 2026. Older Jan 2026 data put it at a $1.3B valuation — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgsfield_AI); [getlatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/higgsfield.ai) (secondary)
- HeyGen (avatar video): announced ARR above $200M on Jun 25 2026 after doubling in 8 months. Its last priced round was the 2024 Benchmark Series A at $500M — [clinkbill](https://clinkbill.com/arr-leaderboard/heygen); [getlatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/heygen) (secondary)

**AI micro-dramas**
- ReelShort is projected by Media Partners Asia to reach $1.05B revenue in 2026 (+34%), with its first profit ($63M EBITDA), from $785M in 2025 — [Deadline, Aug 2026](https://deadline.com/2026/08/microdrama-reelshort-revenue-profit-1237027492/)
- ReelShort uses AI to add to its live-action slate, not to replace it. In China, about 430K micro-dramas went live in the first 8 months of 2026, and over 90% were AI-generated. Only 1.3% break even — [clip-short](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/ai-short-dramas); [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/ad721dc7-6c9c-4cd0-b49f-4de6daee49c3) (secondary)
- DramaBox and ReelShort were each about $140M in in-app revenue in Q1 2026 (Sensor Tower, via secondary) — [clip-short](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/ai-short-dramas)

### Inferences
- Hype vs real. Real: paid creator tools (Kling, Higgsfield, Runway, HeyGen) and AI used as a supply engine for micro-dramas. Hype: AI-only social feeds (Sora app dead, Vibes no proof). The jaw-drop moment ("me in the movie") wears off fast unless there is a story or a reason to come back.
- The Sora/Disney collapse shows that licensed-IP cameo experiences are wanted by big rights holders. Disney said it will keep working with AI platforms. The slot is open, but no consumer product has made it work yet.
- In China the AI micro-drama flood (90%+ AI, 1.3% break even) points to fast commoditisation of AI-made stories. Quality and IP set titles apart, not the fact that AI made them.

### Gaps
- No 2026 user or revenue data was found for Pika, Hedra, Captions (now "Mirage"), or CapCut's AI features. They were not searched in depth because of the tool budget.
- No official numbers were found for Gemini/Flow video volume or Meta Vibes users.
- The Runway ARR figures conflict, and the Higgsfield $700M ARR is a company claim reported by secondary sites.

## Avatars, digital humans and celebrity likeness (licensed voices, Meta celebrity AIs, digital twins, legacy and spiritual-teacher avatars, historical figures)

### Takeaway
Licensed likeness has moved from novelty to a consent-plus-revenue-share business. Examples: ElevenLabs Iconic Marketplace (Nov 2025), Genies x UTA (Oct 2026) and the Khaby Lame $975M digital-twin deal (Jan 2026). Consumer-facing celebrity chatbots have a poor track record: Meta dropped its 2023 celebrity AIs within a year. In the faith space, "chat with Jesus" apps are a real but small niche with store-scale downloads (100K+). The large faith AI player, Bible Chat, says it has 40M users.

### Cited Findings
- ElevenLabs Iconic Marketplace launched Nov 2025. It licenses AI voices of celebrities and historical figures (about 25-28 at launch, e.g. Michael Caine, Liza Minnelli, Mark Twain, Thomas Edison, Alan Turing). Every use needs the rights holder's approval, and ElevenLabs takes a revenue share. Matthew McConaughey is an investor, but his voice is not in the marketplace — [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/iconic-marketplace); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/elevenlabs-ai-voice-marketplace-matthew-mcconaughey/); [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/elevenlabs-just-solved-ais-celebrity-voice-problem-and-michael-caine-approves/)
- Unverified, single source: Michael Caine AI narration of The Odyssey (Jun 2026), and a Gene Wilder/Wonka posthumous voice for Netflix — [aifilms.ai](https://studio.aifilms.ai/blog/elevenlabs-voice-hollywood-ai)
- Genies x United Talent Agency (Oct 12 2026): UTA clients (reportedly Will Ferrell, Kylie Minogue, Bad Bunny) can create AI personas built on their likeness, personality and experiences that talk with fans. The talent moderates and controls them — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/genies-signs-deal-to-turn-uta-s-hollywood-actors-into-ai-avatars); [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/hollywood_stars_are_getting_ai_doppelgngers_as_genies_signs_major_deal_with_united_talent_agency/s1_17785_44421827)
- Khaby Lame (Jan 2026): a $975M all-stock deal with Rich Sparkle Holdings that includes rights to an AI digital twin (image, voice, behaviour) for multilingual content and commerce. Lawyers flag the risk that the likeness keeps being used after the deal ends — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/01/29/tiktok-influencer-khaby-lame-ai-avatar-deported-trump-creator-economy-975-million-deal); [HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/ip/2026-02/selling-your-ai-digital-twin-the-brave-new-world-of-identity-led-ip-transactions-the-khaby-lame-deal-analysed)
- Meta's 2023 celebrity AI chatbots (Tom Brady, Kendall Jenner and others) were scrapped in under a year for lack of user interest. Background item — [Michael Parekh summary](https://michaelparekh.substack.com/p/ai-about-those-digital-twins); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/01/29/tiktok-influencer-khaby-lame-ai-avatar-deported-trump-creator-economy-975-million-deal)
- 2wai (Calum Worthy) is a "own your AI self" digital-twin app (background, 2025) — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/25/calum-worthy-russell-geyser-2wai-digital-twin)
- Character.AI: about 20M MAU, about 10% under 18. Open-ended chat for under-18s ended by Nov 25 2025, and teens were moved to "Stories" (choice-driven interactive fiction, Dec 2025). A community feed for characters, scenes and video launched Aug 2025 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/29/character-ai-is-killing-the-chatbot-experience-for-minors/?rand=23331); [Sherwood](https://sherwood.news/tech/teens-are-upset-that-they-cant-speak-to-character-ai-chatbots-anymore/)
- Text With Jesus (Catloaf Software, LA): 100K+ Google Play downloads (US listing), rated 4.5. Users can chat with Jesus, Mary, the apostles and even Satan; premium unlocks all figures. RNS reported the founder did not consult theologians — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.textwith.jesus&hl=en); [All That's Interesting](https://allthatsinteresting.com/text-with-jesus)
- Bible Chat says over 40M users (vendor claim). A free tier includes limited AI conversations — [Bible Chat](https://thebiblechat.com/)

### Inferences
- Licensed likeness now runs on consent per project plus a revenue share (ElevenLabs) or the talent moderating its own twin (Genies/UTA). Any celebrity or Keeper likeness product would be expected to follow this model.
- Fans talking to a celebrity AI only works when the celebrity is in control and the fan gets something personal. Meta's attempt failed because the celebrity bots were generic characters.
- Faith chatbots that "play" holy figures are controversial (the Satan character, no theologians consulted). Reviewed, sourced AI with a clear voice is a gap competitors are leaving open.

### Gaps
- No 2026 sources were gathered on StoryFile, the Deepak Chopra AI (DeepakChopra.ai), AI versions of other spiritual teachers, "Hello History"-style historical figure apps, Synthesia consumer use, or 2026 Meta AI celebrity voices (the 2024 Meta AI voices by Judi Dench, John Cena, Awkwafina and others are background from memory and not sourced here).

## AI music: Suno, Udio, personalized songs, AI music in social apps

### Takeaway
Suno is the breakout consumer AI music product. It had 2M paid subscribers and $300M ARR (Feb 2026), raised a $400M Series D at $5.4B led by OpenAI and Bond, settled with Warner, and launched licensed v6 models on Sep 9 2026 trained on WMG, BMG and Believe material. Udio settled with UMG and moved into a walled garden with no downloads, which upset users. The labels have turned lawsuits into licensing.

### Cited Findings
- Suno had 2M paid subscribers and $300M ARR, per CEO Mikey Shulman (Feb 27 2026) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/ai-music-generator-suno-hits-2-million-paid-subscribers-and-300m-in-annual-recurring-revenue/)
- Suno raised over $400M at a $5.4B valuation, up from about $500M in 2024 — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-raises-over-400-million-pushing-valuation-to-5-4-billion/); [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/suno-raised-400m-new-ai-model-made-with-music-industry/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/suno-raises-400m-series-d-134608801.html)
- Under the Warner settlement, free users can no longer download songs and Suno acquired Songkick. The licensed v6 launched Sep 9 2026, trained on WMG/BMG/Believe material with a revenue share. Warner's CEO called it "a smart win". The UMG and Sony suits against Suno continue — [Billboard WMG memo](https://www.billboard.com/pro/warner-music-ceo-memo-suno-partnership-ai-music-model/); [ecoustics](https://www.ecoustics.com/news/suno-v6-licensed-ai-music-warner-bmg-believe/)
- Total Suno users estimated at 100M+ (estimate) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/suno/)
- Udio and UMG settled in Oct 2025 and planned a licensed creation-and-streaming platform for 2026. Existing creations are kept in a walled garden, downloads are disabled (users complained), artists opt in, and users can change vocals or swap in the voices of participating UMG artists. Irving Azoff and the Music Artists Coalition questioned how the money is split — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/universal-music-settles-udio-lawsuit-strikes-deal-for-licensed-ai-music-platform); [Hollywood Reporter](https://hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-industry-news/universal-music-group-announces-settlement-with-udio-1236414023); [MBW on Azoff](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/umgs-udio-deal-prompts-questions-from-irving-azoff-and-the-music-artists-coalition)

### Inferences
- AI music is the strongest example of consumer AI people actually pay for: a song in seconds with real vocals is the jaw-drop, and it repeats every time. Licensed artist voices and styles (Udio/UMG, Suno v6) are the next consumer feature.
- Custom songs (birthday, faith songs, a "your song" moment) look like a proven behaviour on Suno, though no usage breakdown was found.

### Gaps
- No confirmation was found on whether Udio's licensed UMG platform launched in 2026.
- No data on personalized-song apps (e.g. Songfinch AI) or on AI music inside TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.

## AI games and living worlds: Genie, Inworld, AI NPCs, Roblox AI, AI-native mobile games

### Takeaway
Google's Project Genie (Genie 3) is the world-model "jaw-drop" for consumers. Since Jan 29 2026 you can type or sketch a world and walk around inside it. It is gated to $250/mo AI Ultra, capped at 60-second sessions at 720p, and is a demo, not a game. AI-NPC games exist (inZOI, Legend of Zhaoyang, Skyrim's Mantella mod), but no AI-native game breakout with traction comparable to Suno or Kling was found.

### Cited Findings
- Project Genie launched Jan 29 2026 as a Google Labs prototype on Genie 3. Users can sketch, explore and remix interactive worlds from text and images. At launch it was limited to US AI Ultra subscribers aged 18+, with 60-second sessions at about 720p and 20-24 fps — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/01/29/google-project-genie/); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/01/30/google-launches-project-genie-create-interactive-worlds-with-ai/)
- Gizmodo: "Project Genie is not for you". The price is about $250/mo — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/googles-project-genie-is-not-for-you-2000716070)
- Unverified (Wikipedia only): worldwide AI Ultra rollout and Street View integration in May 2026, and Genie 4 announced Oct 27 2026 — [Wikipedia: Project Genie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Genie_(website)); [Wikipedia: Genie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie_(world_model))
- inZOI (KRAFTON, Steam Early Access since Mar 2025) uses NVIDIA ACE "Smart Zoi" NPCs that act on their own personalities. Legend of Zhaoyang (China, Steam) gives every NPC an AI agent with personality and memory, and is reported at 89% positive. The Mantella Skyrim mod is cited as the most playable use of generative NPCs (listicles, low reliability) — [nexworld](https://nexworld.wiki/en/ai-games/ai-games-panorama-2026/); [aivexify](https://aivexify.com/best-ai-npc-games-in-2026/); [SolidAITech](https://www.solidaitech.com/2026/06/ai-video-games.html)
- Character.AI "Stories" (interactive fiction) is a consumer step from chat toward game-like narrative — [Sherwood](https://sherwood.news/tech/teens-are-upset-that-they-cant-speak-to-character-ai-chatbots-anymore/)

### Inferences
- Hype vs real: world models give the strongest demo "wow", but they are not yet consumer products (cost, 60-second sessions, no goals). AI NPCs add depth to existing genres; they do not yet make breakout hits on their own.
- For a lesson app, a guided AI character inside a designed world, with game loops written by people, is closer to what actually works today than a fully generated world.

### Gaps
- Roblox AI tools (Cube 3D/4D, Assistant), Inworld's consumer products, AI Dungeon/Latitude successors, and AI-native mobile hits were not found with sourced 2026 traction. These need a follow-up search.

## AR/spatial and wearables: Meta Ray-Ban AI, Vision Pro, Snap generative lenses, immersive experiences

### Takeaway
AI glasses are the real wearable story. EssilorLuxottica sold over 7M Ray-Ban/Oakley Meta glasses in 2025, about 3x 2023-24 combined, and AI-glasses revenue nearly doubled again in Q2 2026. Supply limits for the $799 Ray-Ban Display delayed its international launch. Snap's generative lenses (Imagine Lens, now free in the US with limits) run at Snapchat's huge lens scale. Snap Specs were promised for 2026, but no launch was confirmed in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- EssilorLuxottica sold over 7M Ray-Ban and Oakley Meta glasses in full-year 2025, versus about 2M across 2023-24 — [mezha.ua](https://mezha.ua/en/news/essilorluxottica-prodala-ponad-7-mln-smartokulyariv-meta-308538/); [Wikipedia: Ray-Ban Meta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray-Ban_Meta)
- AI-glasses revenue nearly doubled in Q2 2026, and EssilorLuxottica is moving into waveguide manufacturing — [TechTimes, Jul 28 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321887/20260728/ai-glasses-revenue-nearly-doubled-q2-essilorluxottica-targets-waveguide-manufacturing.htm)
- Production capacity: the target was 10M a year by end-2026, with talks of 20M+ and possibly over 30M (Bloomberg, via secondary) — [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/meta-aims-to-double-possibly-even-triple-smart-glasses-production-this-year/); [technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/01/14/meta-and-essilorluxottica-eye-massive-production-boost-for-ray-ban-smart-glasses/)
- The Meta Ray-Ban Display ($799, US launch Sep 2025) had its international launch delayed because of "unprecedented" US interest and waveguide supply limits — [Wikipedia: Ray-Ban Meta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray-Ban_Meta); [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/meta-aims-to-double-possibly-even-triple-smart-glasses-production-this-year/)
- Snap Imagine Lens is an open-prompt generative AI lens (images). It started on paid tiers and was made free in the US with a limited number of generations (Oct 2025). AI image-to-video lenses were marked "coming soon" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/22/snapchat-makes-its-first-open-prompt-ai-lens-available-for-free-in-the-us); [Snapchat Help](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/41681335401108-What-is-the-Snapchat-Imagine-Lens)
- Snap announced consumer Specs AR glasses for 2026 and says AR lenses are used 8B times a day (2025 figure) — [Snap newsroom](https://newsroom.snap.com/launch-specs-2026)

### Inferences
- Glasses with an AI assistant are the wearable form consumers are actually buying. A guide that sees what you see and talks in your ear is a near-future way for any companion or guide product to reach people.
- Generative camera lenses ("turn me into...") are the mass-market version of the cameo idea, and they live inside existing social apps.

### Gaps
- No sourced 2026 data was found on Apple Vision Pro AI experiences or sales, on whether Snap Specs actually shipped, on Meta's own 2026 unit counts for the Display, or on teamLab-style immersive venue numbers (e.g. teamLab attendance, Cosm, Sphere). These need a follow-up search.
