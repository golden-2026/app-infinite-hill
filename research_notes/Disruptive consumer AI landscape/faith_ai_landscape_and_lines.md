# Faith-tech AI landscape and the religious/ethical lines (2024 - Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-10. About 24 searches; snippets read via search summaries (few full-page fetches), so exact quotes should be checked against the primary source before publishing. App metrics are third-party estimates (Appfigures, Sensor Tower), not company-reported.

## Which faith/spiritual AI products exist and how are they doing

### Takeaway
The money and retention in faith apps still sit with human-voiced, curated content (Hallow, about $40M in 2025 net revenue, no prominent AI chat). The AI-native chat apps (Bible Chat) win on downloads but earn far less, and "chat with a holy figure" apps (Text With Jesus, GitaGPT) grow fast while drawing blasphemy and accuracy criticism. The biggest Bible platform, YouVersion, is deliberately holding back on open-ended AI because of accuracy.

### Cited Findings
**Hallow (Catholic prayer, human-narrated, celebrity voices)**
- Appfigures estimates roughly $40M net revenue in 2025; peaks are seasonal (about $10M in March 2024, $9.7M in April 2025, around Lent); about 280K downloads per month through most of 2025. — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/hallow-lent-surge-prayer-app-revenue)
- Fast Company: Hallow "leads the pack" of Christian tech with 22M+ downloads. — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91249914/as-bible-sales-boom-so-does-christian-tech)
- A directory listing mentions a "Hallow AI" feature, but I found no reliable detail on what it does. (Low confidence.) — [faith.tools](https://faith.tools/bible)

**Bible Chat (AI-native, Romanian startup Apps Momentum)**
- Downloads doubled in 2025 and nearly 10x since early 2024; best month March 2025 with 1.5M downloads; earned about $750K net revenue that month. — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250418/5-this-is-how-to-drive-demand-for-the-oldest-book-in-the-world)
- About 5M monthly active users just over a year after launch; about 70% of users in the US. — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/06/20/how-romanian-startup-bible-chat-turned-ai-and-faith-into-a-global-phenomenon/)
- Developer claims 25M+ downloads (developer claim, via a review site). — [LearnOfChrist review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/bible-chat)
- A Chinese analysis says Bible Chat's downloads beat Hallow's over six months while revenue stayed well below, though September 2025 subscription revenue (about $1.1M to $2.2M depending on source) briefly passed Hallow's. Sources conflict. — [Baijing](https://www.baijing.cn/article/54281)

**Glorify / Abide**
- Appfigures puts Glorify and Abide downloads "in the tens of thousands" during the Lent period, far behind Hallow. I found no firm 2025 revenue figure. — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/hallow-lent-surge-prayer-app-revenue)

**YouVersion (largest Bible app)**
- Founder/CEO Bobby Gruenewald says top AI models misquote the Bible at least 15% of the time, some up to 60%. YouVersion will only add open-ended AI if it meets high safety and accuracy standards. It uses AI internally for coding and workflow. — [Christian Post](https://www.christianpost.com/news/ais-bible-misquotes-range-from-15-to-60-youversion-ceo.html); [Christian Today](https://www.christiantoday.com/news/ai-still-too-inaccurate-when-it-comes-to-scripture-says-youversion-founder)
- YouVersion launched "Platform AI Skills" for coding agents at Missional AI 2026 and has a ChatGPT app listing with features "coming soon." — [faith.tools](https://faith.tools/app/10230-youversion-platform-services)

**"Chat with a holy figure" apps**
- Text With Jesus (Catloaf Software, LA, built on OpenAI's models) lets users text Jesus, Moses, Mary, apostles; the paid tier ($2.99) lets you turn on "Satan." 4.7/5 App Store rating; "thousands of paying subscribers." Founder Stéphane Peter calls it "another way to explore Scripture." — [Fox/LiveNOW](https://www.fox9.com/news/text-jesus-ai-chatbot-app-grows-rapidly-despite-criticism); [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/14/2025/new-chatbot-lets-users-text-with-ai-jesus)
- Critics: James Spencer (Moody Center) said the AI "Jesus" seemed more concerned with pleasing modern ears than with the Law and Prophets; the bot dodges hard topics (on gender transition: "The Scriptures do not speak directly..."); characters don't admit to being AI when asked in character. — [Crosswalk](https://www.crosswalk.com/headlines/contributors/michael-foust/text-with-jesus-app-gains-popularity-as-critics-call-it-blasphemous.html); [CBN](https://www2.cbn.com/news/us/what-would-you-ask-jesus-new-ai-jesus-app-knows-gospel-well-critics-are-alarmed)
- Note: one search summary claimed Pope Leo called the Satan feature "chilling"; a follow-up search found NO direct Pope Leo quote about Text With Jesus. Treat that claim as unverified.

**Hindu: GitaGPT and Gita bots**
- At least five Gita chatbots appeared in India Jan to Mar 2023; some answered up to 50,000 questions a day. GitaGPT answered as if it were Krishna. The Quint, after 120+ prompts, found bots giving answers that condone violence; one called Gandhi "not competent enough to lead the country." — [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/world/india-religious-chatbots-1.6896628); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/explainers/chatgpt-gitagpt-bhagavad-gita-chatbot-india-violence)

**Jewish AI tools**
- AISH launched "Rabbi AI" (ai.aish.com) for Jewish practice questions in 2025. Rebbe.io and RavGPT carry disclaimers ("educational tool; consult a real rabbi"). JewPT presents itself as a study partner (chevruta), not a rabbi. Kosher.Chat built as a "chatbot with a Jewish filter" after the Skver Beth Din restricted AI. — [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/06/ai-rabbis-jewish-thinkers-moral-guardrails-judaism-torah/); [i24](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/middle-east/technology-science/1683536734-check-out-kosher-chat-chat-gpt-s-jewish-competitor); [Rabbi Jason blog on JewPT](https://blog.rabbijason.com/2026/02/jewpt-look-at-future-of-jewish-learning.html)

**Catholic: "Father Justin" (Catholic Answers)**
- Launched April 23, 2024 as a 3D priest avatar; it called users "my child," suggested it could absolve and witness marriage, and reportedly okayed baptism with Gatorade. Within about a day it was "laicized" into "Justin" in a button-down shirt. Catholic Answers said it stays "no less resolved" to use AI. — [America Magazine](https://americamagazine.org/faith/2024/04/25/ai-priest-catholic-answers-laicized-father-justin-247799); [Christian Post](https://christianpost.com/news/catholic-answers-shelves-ai-priest-father-justin-amid-backlash.html); [Catholic Virginian](https://catholicvirginian.org/?p=35329)

**"Deus in Machina" AI Jesus (Lucerne, Switzerland)**
- Aug to Oct 2024, St. Peter's Chapel put an AI Jesus avatar (trained on the New Testament, about 100 languages) in a confessional booth with Lucerne University's lab; framed as an art experiment, not a sacrament; 1,000+ visitors; drew criticism for using a real confessional. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/world/deus-machina-swiss-church-installs-ai-jesus-connect-digital-divine-rcna182973); [EWTN fact check](https://ewtnnews.com/world/fact-check-ai-jesus-isn-t-actually-hearing-confessions)

**Buddhist: Mindar (Kodaiji, Kyoto)**
- $1M android of Kannon (2019, Hiroshi Ishiguro). It gives a pre-scripted Heart Sutra sermon and was not AI-driven at launch. Temple says Western visitors were most upset; Japanese feedback mostly positive. Beijing's Longquan Monastery has robot monk Xian'er. — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/east-asia/article/3022716/meet-mindar-humanoid-robot-preaches-sermons-buddhist-temple); [Lion's Roar](https://lionsroar.com/what-can-we-learn-from-an-android-buddhist-preacher/)

### Inferences
- Paid retention favors trusted human voices plus curation (Hallow's celebrity narrators); AI chat drives cheap downloads but weaker monetization. For Infinite Hill this supports the plan: celebrity/human-narrated films as the core, AI as a helper, not the headline.
- The category leader holding back on open AI (YouVersion) means "accurate, sourced, reviewed" is a real way to stand out.
- Every "AI speaks AS the holy figure / clergy" product hit backlash; "AI as a study partner" framing (JewPT, Rebbe.io) did not.

### Gaps
- No reliable data found on Quran AI apps (e.g., usage, scholar endorsements), Deepak Chopra's "DigitalDeepak"/AI Chopra traction, or Hallow's AI features.
- No 2026 Sensor Tower faith-category report found; all metrics above are 2025 estimates.
- No recent (2025-26) info on whether Mindar now uses generative AI, or on newer Buddhist chatbots.
- Could not verify Bible Chat's 2026 download total (30M figure not supported).

## What went viral or wowed, and what backfired

### Takeaway
AI Bible videos with a modern twist ("If Moses had an iPhone," Bible figures as vloggers) pulled tens of millions of views in 2025 and split Christians between "creative evangelism" and "entertainment that cheapens scripture." The backlashes have a clear pattern: AI posing as clergy or a deity, AI inventing rituals or history, and AI images of figures a tradition says must not be shown.

### Cited Findings
**Wowed / viral**
- @holyvlogsz-style AI "Bible influencer" videos: a Daniel-in-the-lion's-den vlog passed 6.1M views in three days (other reports say 6.8M to 9M); "If Moses had an iPhone" reported at 30M views; a Dec 2023 AI angels TikTok passed 33M. The accounts have millions of followers across platforms. — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ai-bible-influencer-videos-ai-bible-stories); [Baptist Times](https://baptisttimes.co.uk/Articles/723700/Millions_are_watching.aspx); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/06/03/viral-ai-videos-bring-bible-figures-to-life-as-influencers)
- Filmmaker PJ Accetturo's Veo 3 Bible compilation reportedly got 1.3M views in a day for about $100 in credits. — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ai-bible-influencer-videos-ai-bible-stories)
- A 2026 Journal of Religious Education paper studied 25 high-visibility @holyvlogsz videos (50K+ views each), showing academic attention. — [Chrostowski 2026](https://edoc.ku.de/id/eprint/36817/1/Chrostowski-2026-Journal_of_Religious_Education.pdf)
- Reception is split: some call it creative evangelism, others say it crosses a line; Baptist Times argues trusted church institutions should step into the space. (Opinion.) — [Crosswalk](https://www.crosswalk.com/headlines/contributors/michael-foust/christians-split-over-viral-ai-videos-that-put-bible-characters-on-tiktok.html); [Premier Christianity](https://www.premierchristianity.com/opinion/millions-are-watching-these-bizarre-ai-bible-videos-on-tiktok-should-you-be-worried-or-encouraged/19850.article)

**Mixed: Pray.com AI Bible films**
- Pray.com makes cinematic AI Bible videos (Old Testament, Revelation); an 8-minute Revelation video got 750K+ views in two months. Theologians Brad East (Abilene Christian) said they reduce the Bible to an action movie; Jeffrey Bilbro (Grove City) said they frame scripture as entertainment; Paul Hoffman (Samford) questioned animating hard texts like Revelation. Pray.com CTO Ryan Beck: "this isn't AI slop," citing real actors' voices and original music. — [NPR](https://npr.org/2025/09/07/nx-s1-5518263/ai-bible-christianity-content)

**Backfired**
- Father Justin (2024): priestly persona plus wrong sacramental claims; renamed within about a day. — [America Magazine](https://americamagazine.org/faith/2024/04/25/ai-priest-catholic-answers-laicized-father-justin-247799)
- Sikh: YouTuber Dhruv Rathee's May 2025 video with AI images of Guru Gobind Singh and the Sahibzade drew demands from the SGPC and Akal Takht for takedown and action; he removed it the next day. SGPC says the Gurus and their families cannot be shown in human form or in films/animation. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/ai-visuals-of-sikh-gurus-land-dhruv-rathee-in-soup-sgpc-sad-demand-immediate-takedown/2630875); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/akal-takht-sgpc-slam-youtuber-seek-strict-action); [OECD AI incident](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-05-19-983f)
- Hindu: Jan 2026, Puri's Shree Jagannath Temple administration filed a police complaint over AI videos of Lord Jagannath (e.g., a fake crane pouring milk over the deity, a ritual that does not exist); the temple committee moved to make such content punishable; by July 2026 police removed 97 AI images (e.g., deities with thermometers before Rath Yatra). — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/odisha/fake-ai-generated-videos-of-lord-jagannath-surface-police-complaint-filed-3867751); [OdishaBytes](https://odishabytes.com/97-ai-generated-misleading-images-of-puri-jagannath-temple-deities-removed-after-outrage/)
- Hindu: cricketer Rinku Singh faced a police complaint from Karni Sena over an AI "God in Car" trend video with a Hindu deity. — [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/sports/cricket/karni-sena-files-police-complaint-against-rinku-singh-over-ai-generated-hindu-deity-video-7281789.html)
- GitaGPT bots condoning violence and giving political opinions in a god's voice (2023). — [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/world/india-religious-chatbots-1.6896628)
- Deepfakes of clergy: Al-Azhar warned of fake AI videos of the Grand Imam promoting medical products. — [Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/575728.aspx)
- Text With Jesus's "Satan" option is the most-criticized feature. — [The Loop via preview](https://preview.zeale.co/news/articles/text-with-jesus-ai-chatbot-app-draws-criticism-including-for-option-to-message-with-satan)

### Inferences
- What wows: modern framing of ancient stories, spectacle, humor, cheap fast production. What backfires: impersonation of sacred authority, fabricated rituals or history, depicting figures a tradition forbids, and "entertainment over meaning."
- Backlash is strongest and most organized (police complaints, takedowns) in Sikh and Hindu temple contexts in India, and in Islam on prophets; Christian backlash is mostly commentary, not legal.
- For a Christian-first audience, AI Bible spectacle reaches millions but scholars attack it as cheapening; pairing spectacle with real teaching and named human reviewers is the defense.

### Gaps
- No good data on "He Gets Us" (not AI; did not research its reach here).
- No found case of a faith app losing users or revenue measurably because of an AI backlash.

## What religious authorities say about AI

### Takeaway
The Catholic Church has the most developed position (Antiqua et Nova 2025; Pope Leo XIV's first encyclical Magnifica Humanitas, May 2026): AI is a tool, must not pose as a person or replace human relationship, and the Pope refused an AI avatar of himself. Evangelicals say AI may complement, never replace, ministry. Muslim, Sikh and many Jewish authorities draw firm lines on depicting holy figures and on AI giving rulings.

### Cited Findings
**Catholic**
- Antiqua et Nova (Jan 28, 2025, Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith plus Dicastery for Culture and Education): calling AI "intelligence" can mislead; AI is a product of human intelligence; warns that anthropomorphizing AI harms children's development and that AI can replace real friendship and isolate people. — [America Magazine](https://www.americamagazine.org/politics-society/2025/01/28/vatican-artificial-intelligence-document-249797); [Relevant Radio](https://relevantradio.com/?p=94445)
- Pope Leo XIV (interview, July 30, 2025, published Sept 2025) refused to authorize an "AI pope" avatar for virtual audiences; said if anyone shouldn't be represented by an avatar, the pope is high on the list. — [USCCB/CNS](https://www.usccb.org/news/2025/pope-nixes-virtual-pope-idea-explains-concerns-about-ai)
- Magnifica Humanitas (first encyclical, May 25, 2026): AI must be "disarmed"; calls for strong regulation and development for the common good, not profit; no lethal decisions to AI. — [USCCB](https://www.usccb.org/news/2026/first-encyclical-pope-leo-urges-world-disarm-ai-amid-increased-reliance); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/5/25/pope-says-ai-must-be-disarmed-to-prevent-domination-exclusion-and-death)
- Commentary quotes the encyclical: simulated words "do not build genuine relationships, but only their appearance," and AI may "simulate empathy and understanding" without understanding. (Quoted by secondary sources; verify against vatican.va.) — [New Polity](https://newpolity.com/blog//on-the-papal-endorsement-of-chatbots); [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/tRaBFKqmiQ5sPP85w/cultivated-not-built-an-ai-s-reply-to-pope-leo-xiv-s)
- Catholic scholar: an AI Jesus can "listen" but cannot absolve sins. — [The Conversation via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/ai-jesus-might-listen-confession-140645558.html)

**Evangelical / Southern Baptist**
- ERLC "Artificial Intelligence: An Evangelical Statement of Principles" (2019); SBC resolution on AI (2023); ERLC 39-page guide "The Work of Our Hands" (Sept 2025): AI should complement, not replace, human ministry. ERLC warned against AI-written sermons. — [ERLC](https://erlc.com/research/the-work-of-our-hands-christian-ministry-in-the-age-of-artificial-intelligence/); [Christian Post](https://christianpost.com/news/erlc-warns-against-use-of-ai-to-write-sermons.html)
- Lifeway "Brentwood Statement" on AI in ministry (June 8, 2026). Lifeway Research (fielded Sept 2025): 61% of Protestant churchgoers concerned about AI's effect on faith (67% of evangelicals); 10% of pastors regular AI users, 32% experimenting. — [Lifeway](https://news.lifeway.com/2026/06/08/lifeway-sbc-leaders-respond-to-research-findings-with-ai-in-christian-ministry-statement/); [Religion Unplugged](https://religionunplugged.com/news/2026/4/21/pastors-churchgoers-see-ai-as-concerning-and-confusing)

**Islamic**
- Egypt's Dar al-Ifta: impermissible to depict prophets and messengers in films. — [Dar al-Ifta](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/10769/depicting-the-prophets-and-messengers-in-movies)
- Al-Azhar rejects any production depicting prophets, messengers or the Prophet's companions (2014 statement on "Noah"); in Ramadan 2025 its scholars issued a fatwa against watching the Saudi series "Muawiya" for portraying companions. — [Christian Post (2014)](https://www.christianpost.com/news/top-islamic-institution-in-egypt-calls-for-noah-movie-ban-says-it-violates-islamic-law-115729/); [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/al-azhar-bans-viewing-inappropriate-ramadan-series-muawiya)
- Dar al-Ifta AI-specific fatwas (Dec 2025): using AI to depict God is forbidden; separate fatwa on depicting the Day of Judgment with AI. Egypt's Mufti warned against AI depictions of the Divine (Jan 2026). (Arabic sources; full text not read.) — [Dar al-Ifta, AI and depicting God](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/22369/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-ai-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A9); [Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/Islameyat/others-islamic_ppl_news/details/2026/1/6/2920778/)
- Dar al-Ifta: AI animation of deceased people depends on purpose; putting false words or actions on real people is prohibited. — [Dar al-Ifta](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/24168/creating-an-ai-generated-video-of-a-deceased-person)
- On AI images of humans generally, scholars differ: strict view says images of humans are haram, including AI; others say it depends on use. — [IslamWeb](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/528754/ruling-on-ai-generated-images-using-music-and-earning-from-youtube); [IslamiCity](https://www.islamicity.org/dearhadi/624?utm_id=643)
- AI issuing fatwas: Jordan's Ifta and Egypt's Dar al-Ifta treat AI as an assistant, not a mufti. — [Jordan Ifta](https://aliftaa.jo/article-en/5929/Artificial-Intelligence-An-Assistant-or-a-Mufti-); [Dar al-Ifta](https://dar-alifta.org/en/article/details/12325/resolving-family-issues-the-dilemma-of-ai-generated-fatwas)

**Jewish**
- No formal cross-denominational ruling found. Chabad.org: "a chatbot does not a rabbi make"; YU's Rabbi Netanel Wiederblank argued AI shouldn't resolve rabbinic debates; Rabbi Mordechai Lightstone warned that treating AI as having a soul risks idolatry; the Skver Hasidic court restricted AI use. — [Chabad.org](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/6797062/jewish/Can-AI-Replace-Rabbis.htm); [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/06/ai-rabbis-jewish-thinkers-moral-guardrails-judaism-torah/)
- Opinion: AI's probability-based answers sit poorly with Judaism's culture of debate (multiple views kept alive). — [J. Weekly](https://jweekly.com/2025/12/18/ais-systems-of-probability-are-far-removed-from-the-jewish-culture-of-debate/)

**Sikh**
- SGPC objected to AI misinformation on Gurbani, Sikh history and Gurmat and wrote to ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Meta, Google, Midjourney and others; SGPC says Gurus, Sahibzade and their families cannot be shown in human form or films. — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/amritsar/sgpc-writes-to-ai-platforms-raises-concern-over-wrong-information-on-gurbani-sikh-history); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/sgpc-raises-objection-to-ai-generated-misinformation-on-sikhism)

**Hindu**
- No central authority; reactions come from temple bodies and devotee groups (Puri complaint, police removals) and from groups like Karni Sena. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/odisha/fake-ai-generated-videos-of-lord-jagannath-surface-police-complaint-filed-3867751)

### Inferences
- Across faiths the shared line is: AI as a helper or study partner is tolerated; AI as the authority, the clergy, or the holy figure is not.
- Depiction rules differ sharply by faith: Christianity broadly tolerates images of Jesus; Islam (prophets, companions), Sikhism (Gurus and families) forbid; Hinduism allows deity images but objects to invented rituals and disrespect.

### Gaps
- No direct Buddhist institutional statement on AI found.
- Hindu institutional positions beyond individual temples not found.
- Full text of Magnifica Humanitas on religious chatbots not read; no passage found that addresses faith chatbots directly.

## Practical lines for an app like Infinite Hill

### Takeaway
The lines most cited by authorities and backlash cases are: do not show the Prophet Muhammad or other prophets/companions in Islam, or the Sikh Gurus and their families; do not let AI speak as God, Jesus, a deity, or as clergy; do not let AI give religious rulings; do not invent rituals or history; do not deepfake real clergy. Jesus can be shown in Christian content, but the AI-Jesus-voice products draw criticism.

### Cited Findings
- Prophets and companions (Islam): impermissible to depict in film per Dar al-Ifta and Al-Azhar; AI depiction of God forbidden (Dar al-Ifta, Dec 2025). — [Dar al-Ifta](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/10769/depicting-the-prophets-and-messengers-in-movies); [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/al-azhar-bans-viewing-inappropriate-ramadan-series-muawiya)
- Sikh Gurus: no human-form depiction in films or animation (SGPC); a takedown followed within a day in the Rathee case. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/ai-visuals-of-sikh-gurus-land-dhruv-rathee-in-soup-sgpc-sad-demand-immediate-takedown/2630875)
- AI in a holy figure's voice: GitaGPT spoke as Krishna and condoned violence; Text With Jesus characters don't admit to being AI when asked in character. — [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/world/india-religious-chatbots-1.6896628); [CBN](https://www2.cbn.com/news/us/what-would-you-ask-jesus-new-ai-jesus-app-knows-gospel-well-critics-are-alarmed)
- AI as clergy / sacraments: Father Justin claimed it could absolve; renamed within a day. — [America Magazine](https://americamagazine.org/faith/2024/04/25/ai-priest-catholic-answers-laicized-father-justin-247799)
- AI rulings: Jordan Ifta, Dar al-Ifta (AI is an assistant, not a mufti); Jewish tools add "consult a real rabbi" disclaimers. — [Jordan Ifta](https://aliftaa.jo/article-en/5929/Artificial-Intelligence-An-Assistant-or-a-Mufti-); [Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/06/ai-rabbis-jewish-thinkers-moral-guardrails-judaism-torah/)

- Fabricated ritual: Puri complaint over invented milk-pouring ritual. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/odisha/fake-ai-generated-videos-of-lord-jagannath-surface-police-complaint-filed-3867751)
