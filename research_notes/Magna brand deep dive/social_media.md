# Magna (drinkmagna) social media teardown, 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-11-10. Method: logged-out fetch of instagram.com/drinkmagna (profile header and 12 thumbnails/alt text worked; individual post pages and TikTok returned empty shells); Particl (a brand-tracking service that archives Instagram captions and homepage snapshots); Runneth (formerly Motion) Meta ad library; drinkmagna.com; press. Picuki redirected to a site that returned 403. No Social Blade or engagement data was reachable, so likes/views are not available for any post.

**Source-quality warning:** Particl pages show the account header as "magna, 1.2M followers, 892 following". The live Instagram profile shows **70.7K followers, 179 following**. The Particl figure appears to be a placeholder or mock-up on their page template (it also appears beside a sample SMS "15% off" mock-up) and should NOT be used. Particl titles like "Splits59 Giveaway Announcement" are Particl's auto-labels, not Magna's words; captions in quotes below are what Particl captured.

## 1. What recurring post templates and series exist? (content pillars, formats, grid look)

### Takeaway
Magna's feed runs on a few repeating moves: (a) flavor "drops" teased days ahead with a guessing-game or countdown post, then launched with a pun or culture-riff headline; (b) the "Play the Long Game" tagline used as a sign-off and as a highlight/series of athlete and community profiles; (c) LA community events (run clubs, cafés, marathon weekend) shown as recaps; (d) high-production fashion-style campaign shoots, with full credits in the caption. Fall 2026 was almost entirely the Shirley Temple flavor.

## 6. Compared with Liquid Death, Olipop, Poppi, Cann

### Takeaway
Magna is much smaller and plays a different game: local, IRL, taste-led scene marketing plus heavy direct-response ads, rather than Liquid Death's comedy persona at mass scale or Poppi/Olipop's huge creator volume.

### Cited Findings
- Liquid Death: a fictional over-the-top brand persona; about 7M followers on TikTok and 7.2M on Instagram; shifting some paid social to connected TV — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2026/01/12/liquid-death-social-marketing-strategy); [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/tiktok-as-brand-building-surface-stanley-liquid-death-poppi-case)
- Poppi: founder-story TikTok ("My story", 2021) and creator volume; Modash counted ~6.5K sponsored TikTok posts in 12 months vs ~2.5K Olipop and ~436 Liquid Death — [Modash](https://www.modash.io/breakdowns/poppi-influencer-marketing-strategy)
- Olipop vs Poppi described as the first social-media-driven arms race in beverages — [Food Institute](https://foodinstitute.com/?p=559757); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/olipop-social-media-walmart-target-whole-foods-soda/)
- Shared playbook: creator-led launch, TikTok momentum, then retail (Target/Whole Foods) — [5W PR playbook](https://www.5wpr.com/research/food-beverage-digital-to-retail-playbook-2026/) (PR-firm source; treat as indicative)
- Shirley Temple as a trend: Poppi and 7UP and Slice also launched Shirley Temple flavors around late 2025 — [Beverage Industry](https://www.bevindustry.com/articles/97928-slice-soda-shirley-temple)

### Inferences
- Magna's distinct moves: (1) every flavor is a "drop" with a tease, subscriber early access and a pun; (2) IRL scene partners (run clubs, cafés) rather than a fictional persona; (3) fashion-editorial production with credits; (4) organic stays claim-free while ads do hard selling. Like Poppi/Olipop, it uses creators and moved into Target.
- Shirley Temple was a crowded trend; Magna's edge was the "your body is a temple" pun, not the flavor itself.

### Gaps
- No Cann social data gathered (out of call budget). No engagement-rate benchmarks for Magna vs peers.
