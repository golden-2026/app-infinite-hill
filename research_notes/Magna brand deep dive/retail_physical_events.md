# Magna (drinkmagna.com): physical-world branding, retail, events (2025-2026)

Scope note: Magna is the magnesium-electrolyte drink mix from Everlane founder Michael Preysman, launched July 2024 as direct-to-consumer ([The New Consumer](https://newconsumer.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-magnesium-michael-preysman/); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91157124/the-founder-of-everlane-is-launching-a-new-supplement-company-to-tap-into-gen-zs-health-obsession)). Very little trade press covers Magna's physical marketing. Most of what follows comes from Magna's own site and from Particl, a service that keeps copies of Magna's Instagram captions. I did not view the images themselves, so I can't describe the visuals (signage, cups, banners) in detail. Visual descriptions are limited to what captions and page text say.

## Where is Magna sold, and how does it look on shelf?

### Takeaway
Magna's site says it is in "every Target," nationwide, in the hydration aisle. A real Target product page exists, a 10-count Lemon Lime stick pack. But that listing is marked clearance, and one third-party page says Target doesn't carry it, so how wide the Target rollout really is remains unclear. I found no evidence of Erewhon, Whole Foods or gym stocking. Cafés and studios come in through a small wholesale program.

### Cited Findings
- The homepage says: "Magna is now stocked in Target stores **nationwide** - in the hydration aisle, wherever you are." A "Find Us In Every Target" line and a "Find Your Nearest Store" button sit in the header and footer. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- The store-locator page names only Target. It runs a "Buy One Get One Free... Exclusively At Target" offer for "any two Magna products at a Target store near you," in-store purchases only, with the line "Find Us In The Hydration Aisle." — [Magna store locator](https://www.drinkmagna.com/pages/store-locator)
- Target listing: "Magna Electrolyte Mix Stick Pack... Lemon Lime," 10 stick packs, $14.44 (regular $16.99), marked **Clearance**, 15% off. Reviews are dated Feb–Oct 2026, so it was on shelf during that time. — [Target A-94931978](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-94931978)
- Conflicting signal: a 2026 shopping guide asks "why doesn't Target carry Magna" and suggests alternatives. — [ShopSmartLivingFinds](https://shopsmartlivingfinds.com/2026/08/18/best-magna-alternatives-at-target/). This is a low-quality affiliate page. Weigh it lightly against the live Target listing.
- Shipt carries Magna items (Shipt delivers for Target and other stores), which supports some store presence. — [Shipt listing](https://www.shipt.com/shop/products/e2ed1c4a-3109-11f1-807f-c72397ca2a46?zip=78239)
- Wholesale program: an apply-to-partner form, reviewed within 7 days. The minimum order is "6 30-count boxes total at a time," shipped free and paid by card. It is "for in store retail only," which fits cafés, studios and shops. No partner names are listed. — [Magna wholesale](https://www.drinkmagna.com/pages/wholesale)
- Magna is on onbrand, a sponsorship marketplace where events and creators apply for product. Its page has an "Apply for partnership" button and lists 2-count sample packs (Watermelon Lime + Lemon Lime) as offerings. — [onbrand Magna](https://www.onbrand.com/magna)
- The product line uses single-serve stick packs, a 2-count "sampler" at $4, 28/30-count boxes ($55, or $45 on subscription) and a $9 branded Magna water bottle. — [Particl company page](https://www.particl.com/company/magna); [letsliveitup review](https://letsliveitup.com/blogs/electrolytes/magna-electrolytes-review)
- Brand lines on the site: "Tastes Great. Works Greater.", "Not Just Hydration. A Full Body Upgrade.", "The Hydration Molecule", "The nostalgic icon, reimagined for what your body needs now." The logo is black. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- I found no Erewhon listing: searching Erewhon and Magna together turned up only other brands. — [search context: Erewhon ship site](https://ship.erewhon.com/products/magic-magnesium)

### Inferences
- Retail is Target-first and Target-only at the chain level. Wholesale handles small independent accounts (cafés, studios), with a low minimum (6 boxes).
- The "Clearance" tag on the Lemon Lime 10-count may mean that size or flavor is being swapped out, not that Magna is leaving Target. I can't confirm either way.
- The cheap 2-count sampler is the main sampling tool. It works as the free gift at café collabs (see below) and as the event-sponsorship unit on onbrand.

### Gaps
- No photos or descriptions found of Magna's Target shelf placement, endcaps, shelf talkers or display units.
- No confirmation of Whole Foods, Erewhon, Sprouts, gyms (Equinox and the like) or convenience stores.
- No launch date or press coverage for the Target rollout (I checked BevNET, Modern Retail and Food Navigator through search, with no hits).

## Events, pop-ups, run clubs, café collabs and partnerships

### Takeaway
Magna's physical playbook is small-scale, LA- and NYC-based, and built on partners: run clubs (Renegade Running, Soft Bar), cafés serving a limited-time Magna drink (Sua Superette, Cafe Tondo), a hotel (The LINE LA), fitness-apparel brands (adidas through Renegade, Splits59) and themed nights ("Shirley Under the Stars"). Each event is tied to a flavor launch and sold like a sneaker drop: short windows, "self destruct," early access.

### Cited Findings
- Magna's homepage "community" strip lists these partners: **Renegade Running x Adidas (LA 2026), Softbar x Track Club (Brooklyn 2026), Cafe Tondo (LA 2026), Session Training x PNTB Movement (NYC 2025), The LINE Hotel (LA 2026)**. The copy says Magna is for athletes and wellness people "at running clubs and fitness events," with a wholesale link for partners. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- **Sua Superette café collab (Oct 2026):** "SHIRLEY TEMPLE MATCHA at @sua_superette 🍒 Available now until 10/25." Each purchase came with a free Shirley sample pack. Posted Oct 22, 2026, so the run was about 4 days. — [Particl asset feed](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)
- **Cafe Tondo flavor preview (Mar 2026):** "New flavor going live soon. Serving tomorrow at @cafetondo. This post will self destruct in 24 hours." Posted Mar 5, 2026, making the in-café serving Mar 6. — [Particl post](https://www.particl.com/magna/new-flavor-announcement-9501397-8b28e0c4)
- **Run club / night event (Oct 2026):** "SHIRLEY UNDER THE STARS @starpartysilverlake x @renegade_running ⭐️", posted Oct 21, 2026. It paired the Silver Lake "Star Party" with the Renegade Running club. — [Particl asset feed](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)
- **LA hike + giveaway with Splits59 (Oct 2026):** "Celebrating our LA hike with @drinkmagna with a giveaway for one lucky winner." The prize was a $250 Splits59 gift card plus Shirley Temple product, with entries closing 10/28. — [Particl asset feed](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)
- **Teaser for a dinner-style event (Oct 13, 2026):** "Table's set / Guest of honor arrives soon / Don't be late." It sits in the run-up to the Shirley Temple launch, where the "guest of honor" is the flavor. — [Particl asset feed](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)
- **Shirley Temple launch campaign lines (Oct 2026):** "YOUR BODY IS A __ __ Give us your best guess. Coming this week." (Oct 12), then "Your body is a Shirley Temple / The drink you grew up on, the version you grew into / Out now 🍒" (Oct 14), "Pretty pretty please with a cherry on top" (Oct 17), "The Classic Shirley You know it, you love it. Show us what you got 🍒" (Oct 19), "Your workout has never tasted so good" (Oct 20), and a Shirley shoot behind-the-scenes post (Nov 4). — [Particl asset feed](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)
- **Drop mechanics (Jul 2026):** a product drop "the next day at 11am PST, with early access for subscribers at 9am PST" (Jul 7), followed by Raspberry and Peach Iced Tea flavors with "buy before the batch sells out" (Jul 8) and a #MagnaPartner post (Jul 14). — [Particl company page](https://www.particl.com/company/magna)
- **Soft Bar (Brooklyn) context:** Soft Bar at 200 Banker St, Williamsburg, runs a free run club with Carl Radke. Runs go to McCarren Park and back to Soft Bar "for recovery drinks and café beverages." This is the venue behind Magna's "Softbar x Track Club" listing. — [Eventbrite Soft Bar](https://www.eventbrite.com.au/o/soft-bar-105369220341); [NYC for Free](https://www.nycforfree.co/events/soft-strides-anniversary-run-club)
- onbrand lists Magna-sponsored events, including a "Carb Loader LA" item with no date or venue given. — [onbrand Magna](https://www.onbrand.com/magna) (from a search snippet. The full page fetch showed no event details.)

### Inferences
- The repeating format: (1) an Instagram teaser with a blank to fill in or a "self destruct" line, (2) a 1–4 day serving at a cool independent café as a menu special, (3) a free sample pack with each purchase, (4) a run club or night event with a partner, (5) a giveaway with a fitness-apparel brand. Magna's own cost stays low because partners supply the venue and the crowd.
- Nostalgia drives the flavor story: Shirley Temple, Apple Juice, Iced Tea Lemonade, "the nostalgic icon, reimagined." The physical events carry that theme (cherry 🍒, "under the stars").
- Café collabs build the flavor into the café's own signature drink (matcha), rather than just handing out sachets, so the café's regulars become the reach.

### Gaps
- I couldn't view the event photos, so the branding at these events is undescribed: banners, cups, sleeves, flyers, booth design, staff uniforms. The image URLs below are the place to check.
- No Partiful, Luma or Eventbrite listings found for Magna events. No DJ nights confirmed.
- Details on the LINE Hotel LA and Session Training x PNTB Movement activations weren't found beyond the homepage listing.
- Particl lists "1.2M followers" for the account. That looks doubtful for a brand this size and may come from mixed-up data. I couldn't verify it.

I found no evidence of any out-of-home ads (billboards, wild postings, wraps) or print zines for Magna in 2025–2026. The only confirmed merch is a $9 branded Magna water bottle.

### Cited Findings
- Magna Water Bottle, $9, first seen Dec 20, 2024. — [Particl company page](https://www.particl.com/company/magna)
- Searches for Magna together with billboard, pop-up or Expo West 2026 found nothing. The one ad mention is a reviewer saying Magna had been "targetting me with their ads," which points to digital ads. — [letsliveitup review](https://letsliveitup.com/blogs/electrolytes/magna-electrolytes-review)

### Inferences

### Gaps

## Creatine billboard campaign (added scope): who made it, where, when, headlines, idea, press

### Takeaway
I could not confirm the LA Outfront creatine billboard campaign from any indexed source. Searches for each exact headline, "speak it into existence" and Magna together with Outfront all came back empty, and Particl's caption copies only go back to Oct 12, 2026. What I can confirm: Magna+Creatine launched around August 2026, and its product page carries manifesting copy ("Manifest your future in 30 days or less"). That copy matches the affirmation-style headlines the coordinator reported.

### Cited Findings
- Magna+Creatine is Magna's first product beyond hydration. It is the hydration formula plus 5g of NURA creatine monohydrate (magnesium malate/gluconate/glycinate, 300mg sodium, 150mg potassium, vitamins D/B6/B12), sold as 28 stick packs per box at $59, in Raspberry Lemonade, Passion Fruit, Iced Tea Lemonade and a variety pack. Article dated August 2026. — [Stack3d](https://www.stack3d.com/2026/08/magna-creatine/)
- Creatine product page headline (from a search snippet; the page itself returned a rate-limit error): "Manifest your future in 30 days or less," with the point that creatine builds up over weeks, not hours. — [Magna creatine page](https://www.drinkmagna.com/products/creatine)
- I found no press coverage of the billboards: nothing in ad/marketing trade press, BevNET or LA press through search. The only 2026 creatine out-of-home campaign that showed up belongs to a different brand: Thorne x Ciara (agency Frosty, director Jack Begert), with digital billboards in LA, NYC, Atlanta and Miami from Sep 8 to Nov 1, 2026. — [MM+M](https://www.mmm-online.com/news/ciara-and-thorne-creatine-marketing/); [Thorne press release](https://www.thorne.com/press-releases/thorne-shines-spotlight-on-creatine-with-new-campaign-featuring-grammy-award-winning-musical-artist-ciara). This is useful context: Magna's billboards would have run in the same city and season as a celebrity creatine campaign.
- Related LA context: California's AB2030 will require ID to buy muscle-building supplements, which may include creatine, starting Jan 1, 2027. — [Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/11/09/california-new-supplement-law-regulations/)

### Inferences
- The headlines the coordinator gave ("STRENGTH FINDS ME IN EVERY TIMELINE", "PLEASE BE PATIENT! CREATINE ISN'T FINISHED WITH ME YET", "I AM PURE OF HEART AND I DESERVE TO BE BEAUTIFUL", "THE CREATINE YOU FEAR TO DRINK", "I CAN HAVE THE POWER") read as one idea. Each one takes a viral first-person affirmation or internet meme line ("I can have the power" echoes the "I have the power" He-Man line; "pure of heart..." and "please be patient" echo meme captions) and turns it into a creatine claim. That matches the site's "Manifest your future in 30 days or less" and the slow-build "isn't finished with me yet" message. Magna's Shirley Temple posts follow the same playbook: nostalgia or meme language, all caps, first person ("YOUR BODY IS A Shirley Temple").
- This is interpretation, not a stated creative brief. I found nothing from the brand explaining the idea.

### Gaps
- Agency, photographer, exact Outfront placements, dates, the full headline list, the Mango billboard and public reaction: none found. Instagram itself isn't indexed, and Particl's free view only shows the latest ~11 posts. Next steps: open @drinkmagna on Instagram (posts around Aug–Sep 2026 with the captions "Speak it into existence..." and "creatine campaign for..."), check the credits in those captions, and look on LinkedIn for staff or agency posts.
