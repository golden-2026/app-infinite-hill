# Magna (drinkmagna.com) brand strategy: who, why, how, and what transfers

Research date: 2026-11-10. Many primary sources (The New Consumer, Fast Company, Dieline full text, Taste Radio, Trend Hunter) are paywalled or blocked (403). What is below comes from the readable portions plus secondary coverage. Treat anything under "Inferences" as my reasoning, not reported fact.

## 1. Who founded Magna, when, why; funding and investors

### Takeaway
Michael Preysman, who founded Everlane in 2011, launched Magna in July 2024, selling direct to consumers first. Its pitch is magnesium-first hydration, built on the search demand for magnesium. Forerunner Ventures, an early Everlane backer, lists Magna in its portfolio. No round size or other investors are public.

### Cited Findings
- Founder: Michael Preysman, who founded the DTC clothing brand Everlane in 2011 and left the CEO role "a couple of years" before launching Magna. The New Consumer described it as "an electrolyte beverage brand with an initial emphasis on magnesium." — [The New Consumer, Jul 2024](https://newconsumer.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-magnesium-michael-preysman/)
- Why magnesium: Preysman argued magnesium is the most-searched nutrient on Google ("Above everything"). Interest in it is tied to relaxation, sleep and mood. — [The New Consumer](https://newconsumer.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-magnesium-michael-preysman/)
- Fast Company framed the gap as putting magnesium "front and center," a mineral present in very low amounts in drinks like Gatorade. — [Fast Company, Jul 2024 (via search summary; page 403)](https://www.fastcompany.com/91157124/the-founder-of-everlane-is-launching-a-new-supplement-company-to-tap-into-gen-zs-health-obsession)
- The New Consumer's subheadline calls the approach a "post-Nike ideology." The free excerpt does not explain the phrase. Dan Frommer adds that beverages, like fashion, run on "brand vibes" and calls Preysman a skilled storyteller and brand builder. — [The New Consumer](https://newconsumer.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-magnesium-michael-preysman/)
- Development: nine months of formulation with doctors, sports nutritionists and athletes. A "founding team" of five athletes includes pro skier and surfer Malou Peterson. — [Wellworthy, Jul 16 2024](https://wellworthy.com/magna-magnesium-powered-hydration/)
- Investors: Forerunner Ventures lists Magna (founded 2024, founder Preysman) in its portfolio. The page also reportedly shows an "Exited" tag, which is unexplained and unverified. — [Forerunner Ventures](https://www.forerunnerventures.com/investments/magna)
- No funding amount, round or other investors surfaced in search, including the CB Insights and Preqin results. — [search summary of Forerunner page and databases](https://www.forerunnerventures.com/investments/magna)

### Inferences
- Forerunner (Kirsten Green) was a known early Everlane investor. If so, Magna's backing looks like a repeat-founder relationship bet, not a classic seed round. This is my inference and is not confirmed for Magna.
- The "most searched mineral" line shows Magna was built from existing demand: it picked a molecule people already wanted and made it the hero. It did not try to create a new need.

### Gaps
- Round size, valuation, co-founders and team size: none found.
- The meaning of Forerunner's "Exited" tag: not verified.

## 2. Who designed the identity and packaging

### Takeaway
Some Days, a creative studio founded in 2017 by Steve Reinmuth in Berkeley (now also New York and Amsterdam), made the launch identity. The studio's long-time client is Nike, and Dieline covered the work as "a very modern take on sports drinks." Preysman has said the package borrows from a cigarette brand.

### Cited Findings
- Dieline article "Some Days' Magna Design is a Very Modern Take On Sports Drinks" (Rudy Sanchez, Sep 11 2024). The readable intro frames the category as refocusing on "younger Millennials and Generation Z." The full text is paywalled. — [Dieline](https://thedieline.com/some-days-magna-design-is-a-very-modern-take-on-sports-drinks/)
- Design description from secondary coverage: a stylish yellow design with a blocky lowercase wordmark. — [search summary citing Dieline / Trend Hunter](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/magna)
- Some Days: founded 2017 in Berkeley by Steve Reinmuth, now Berkeley, New York and Amsterdam. Work spans campaigns, visual systems, custom typefaces, limited packaging and event visuals. Clients include long-time collaborator Nike, plus Converse and Jordan. Reinmuth on on-trend vs timeless: "Both have their purpose as long as it fits the context." — [It's Nice That, 2023](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/some-days-graphic-design-discover-050623)
- Taste Radio Ep. 646 ("How Everlane's Founder Is Fashioning Success In CPG") covers differentiation, packaging, and why Magna's pack draws on a cigarette brand. The page was 403, so only the listing summary was seen. — [Taste Radio](https://www.tasteradio.com/episodes/how-everlanes-founder-is-fashioning-success-in-cpg)
- Context: Dieline's own trend column reports cigarette-inspired packaging is spreading because of smoking's cultural cachet (Pinterest "cigarette aesthetic" searches rising). — [Dieline Shelf Life 079](https://thedieline.com/shelf-life-079-cigarette-inspired-packaging-is-trending-thanks-to-smokings-cultural-cache/)
- The site's footer links "Partners" to onbrand.com. Its role (agency, platform or partner) is unclear. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)

### Inferences
- "Post-Nike ideology" plus a Nike-trained studio suggests the brand wanted to read as fashion and culture, not supplement or pharmacy. It is a sports drink that does not look like Gatorade or Pedialyte.
- The cigarette-pack cue is about the object as a status prop: something you carry and show, like a pack of smokes, not something medical.

### Gaps
- No Some Days case study page or Behance entry could be read (some-days.com failed with a certificate error). No Brand New review was found.
- The naming rationale is not stated in any source found. The obvious reading is MAGnesium plus "magna" (Latin for great), which ties to the site's "Live life, greater" and "Works Greater." That reading is inference.
- Whether the identity was updated in 2025-26 (new flavors such as "Blue Ice" and "Apple Juice," the "nostalgic icon, reimagined" copy) is unconfirmed.

## 3. Positioning versus Liquid IV, LMNT and Pedialyte

### Takeaway
Magna's wedge is "not salty": magnesium-led, sugar-free daily hydration for regular exercisers. That sets it against salt-maximalist LMNT, sugary Gatorade and medicinal Pedialyte. The brand frames itself as a daily habit, not a sports-only or sick-day product.

### Cited Findings
- The brand runs dedicated "not salty electrolytes" and "5 reasons to ditch salty electrolytes" pages, positioning against high-sodium mixes. — [drinkmagna.com/pages/not-salty-electrolytes](https://www.drinkmagna.com/pages/not-salty-electrolytes); [5 reasons page](https://drinkmagna.com/pages/5-reasons-to-ditch-salty-electrolytes)
- Competitive context: The New Consumer names Gatorade, Pedialyte and LMNT. It describes LMNT as built on podcast sponsorships and the contrarian claim that salt is good for you. — [The New Consumer](https://newconsumer.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-magnesium-michael-preysman/)
- Comparative claim: "3x More Magnesium Than Our Competitors" (210mg vs 26-60mg). Other site lines: "Tastes Great. Works Greater.", "Not Just Hydration. A Full Body Upgrade.", "The Hydration Molecule". — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- Framed as a "daily hydration formula," not a traditional sports drink. Target audience is people who exercise regularly but are not pros. Core target is 25-50 year olds, even though the press framed it as Gen Z. — [YPulse, Jul 18 2024](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2024/07/18/everlane-founder-michael-preysman-is-launching-a-sports-beverage-for-health-and-wellness-obsessed-gen-z/)
- Three magnesiums, each mapped to one benefit: malate for energy, glycinate for recovery, L-threonate (Magtein) for focus. Zero sugar, stevia. — [The New Consumer](https://newconsumer.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-magnesium-michael-preysman/); [Stack3d](https://www.stack3d.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-supplement/)
- The 2026 Target listing and homepage list gluconate, malate and glycinate. A competitor's review of the label calls the magnesium listing confusing. — [Target.com listing via search](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-94931978); [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com); [Live it Up review, Jul 2026 (competitor, biased)](https://letsliveitup.com/blogs/electrolytes/magna-electrolytes-review)

### Inferences
- The disruptive angle is reframing the category's hero ingredient, salt to magnesium, and riding a trend people already search for (sleep, calm, focus), so hydration becomes a wellness ritual. One benefit per form of magnesium makes the science easy to explain.
- The formula appears to have changed between 2024 and 2026 (threonate to gluconate on the main label). That is unconfirmed and should not be stated as fact.

### Gaps
- No head-to-head analysis of Magna vs Liquid IV from an independent analyst was found.

## 4. The brand platform: "Play the Long Game," values and tone

### Takeaway
"Play the long game" appeared as a tagline on a Magna landing page captured in Aug 2025, next to "Live life, greater." No published manifesto explaining it was found. The live 2026 homepage leads with product and benefit claims instead.

### Cited Findings
- A cached drinkmagna.com landing page (captured Aug 6-16 2025) carries "Play the long game" and "Live life, greater," and calls Magna "the world's first magnesium powered hydration formula." — [Pagedeck capture](https://lps.pagedeck.com/brand/drinkmagna.com/pages-try)
- No manifesto or "long game" copy appears on the live homepage (Nov 2026). Community copy: "built for athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and anyone passionate about living well." Tone is science-led (claims "500+ peer-reviewed studies"), confident and comparative. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- Community and IRL partners (2025-26): Renegade Running x Adidas (LA), Softbar x Track Club (Brooklyn), Cafe Tondo (LA), Session Training x PNTB Movement (NYC), The LINE Hotel (LA), plus an ambassador program. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- Launch copy paired each flavor with a moment, e.g. Peach Mango as "paradise on a push day." — [Wellworthy](https://wellworthy.com/magna-magnesium-powered-hydration/)

### Inferences
- "Play the long game" reframes hydration from a fix after a workout to a daily investment in your future self, with compounding benefits. That fits a daily habit and a subscription model. It works as an attitude line, not a product line.
- The IRL strategy (run clubs, cafes, design hotels in LA and Brooklyn) builds culture through scenes. That likely carries more of the brand's coolness than any manifesto does.

### Gaps
- Who wrote the platform, whether Some Days did the verbal identity, and any official tone rules were not found.

## 6. Public results: growth, retail, sales

### Takeaway
Magna moved from DTC only (Jul 2024) to Target nationwide by 2025-26, and expanded from 4 to about 8 flavors. No revenue or growth numbers are public.

### Cited Findings
- Launch: DTC at drinkmagna.com. The 30-stick box cost $55 one-time or $45 on subscription, in 4 flavors (Lemon Lime, Tea Lemonade, Watermelon Lime, Peach Mango). — [Wellworthy](https://wellworthy.com/magna-magnesium-powered-hydration/); [Stack3d](https://www.stack3d.com/2024/07/magna-hydration-supplement/)
- Retail: the homepage says "Find Us In Every Target" and "In Stock Nationwide," hydration aisle. — [drinkmagna.com](https://drinkmagna.com)
- Target.com sells the 10-count at $16.99. Shipt lists Blood Orange, Watermelon Lime and Apple Juice at Target. — [Target](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-94931978); [Shipt](https://www.shipt.com/shop/products/e2ec155c-3109-11f1-a1fe-0b390a6c5730)
- 2026 lineup (per a competitor review): Pure (unflavored), Apple Juice, Iced Tea Lemonade, Island Punch, Blood Orange, Lemon Lime, Mint Lemonade, Watermelon Lime. Subscription price is $39 for 28 servings. — [Live it Up (competitor)](https://letsliveitup.com/blogs/electrolytes/magna-electrolytes-review)
- Particl first tracked Magna listings between Dec 2024 and Apr 2025. These are listing dates, not stocking dates. — [Particl](https://www.particl.com/company/magna)

### Inferences
- Getting into every Target within about 1-2 years of launch is a strong signal for a young CPG brand, but there is no public sales data to size it.
- The flavor shift toward nostalgic, kid-like names (Apple Juice, Blue Ice, Island Punch, "the nostalgic icon, reimagined") suggests a move from cool minimalism to nostalgic, mass-appeal fun once it reached mass retail. This is inference.

### Gaps
- No Target launch date or door count was found, and no revenue, growth rate or Bevnet/Modern Retail coverage turned up.

## Transferable principles for Infinite Hill (all inference)
1. Pick a hero people already search for. Magna led with magnesium because demand existed. IH could lead with the "want" people already search for (calm after a blowup, meaning, belonging) and keep "religion" as the how.
2. Make the product look like a culture object, not its category. Magna does not look like Gatorade (Nike-trained studio, cigarette-pack cue). IH should not look like a faith app; it should look like something worth carrying and showing.
3. Name a clear enemy that is a product trait, not a person or rival. Magna's enemy is "salty" electrolytes. IH can roast "stale, preachy" faith apps the same way, without naming competitors.
4. Give each element one plain benefit. Magna maps one magnesium to one benefit. IH lessons and features can each carry a single plain promise.
5. Use an attitude line that turns a habit into an investment. "Play the long game" turns a daily sachet into compounding self-care. It fits IH's daily practice and streaks.
6. Build the scene in real life. Run clubs, cafes and design hotels in LA and Brooklyn give the brand its coolness. IH's community gap could borrow this (real stamps, real places, partner venues).
7. Mind the drift risk. Magna's 2026 copy reads more mass-market and claim-heavy than the 2024 launch. Keep the original edge when scaling.
