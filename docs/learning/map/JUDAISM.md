# Five-year map: Judaism (2026-10-06)

Step 1 only — a plan for the owner to review. Nothing in `docs/curriculum/judaism` is edited here.

## 1. What the research says this audience needs

1. **Belonging doesn't require belief or proof.** Pew: 18–29-year-olds attend services and believe in God at rates
   close to 65+ (22% vs 22% monthly; 76% vs 72%). "Can you be Jewish and not believe in God?" and "am I Jewish
   enough?" are the two most-repeated Reddit topics found (10+ threads each). *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md §2,3;
   REDDIT.md §4)*
2. **Patrilineal and "not Jewish enough" pain is large and specific.** Reddit threads on being told "you're not
   really Jewish" because of a non-Jewish mother recur constantly; our earlier research never used the word
   "patrilineal." *(REDDIT.md §4, items 17–21)*
3. **"First time back since my bar/bat mitzvah" is its own re-entry moment.** Many stopped at 13; this is a natural
   placement signal, not a failure. *(REDDIT.md §4, items 22–23)*
4. **Hebrew school is remembered as dread, not content** — people can sing the prayers but can't say what the words
   mean. "Where do I start as an adult?" is a direct, common question. *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md §3, §4, items
   24–27)*
5. **The hook is the object or moment, not the law.** Best creators (Jew in the City, BimBam) open with "why do we
   kiss the door," never with halacha categories. *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md, "How the best creators hook
   people")*
6. **Yiddish words people already say** (mazel tov, oy vey, chutzpah, kvetch, schlep, mensch) are a near-universal,
   low-stakes delight hook. *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md items 28–30, Top 20 #6)*
7. **Kosher is widely misunderstood as "just no pork," and "I keep it kinda" is a real, common identity, not a
   failure.** *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md items 8–11, Top 20 #7)*
8. **Fasting (Yom Kippur) has a health-exemption misconception.** Reddit disagreement specifically flagged: mainstream
   law says life and health override the fast; pregnant, nursing or sick people should ask a doctor and a rabbi. Never
   imply fasting is required at risk to health. *(REDDIT_ROUND2.md §5, "flag")*
9. **Not all Jews are of European descent.** Discovering Indian, Ethiopian, Mizrahi and Sephardi Jewish communities
   was the single top-voted reply (280) in the Reddit research — a delight, not just a correction. *(REDDIT_ROUND2.md
   §5)*
10. **Denominational differences ("Reform vs Conservative vs Orthodox") should wait** until some rituals already have
    personal meaning — but when it comes, frame it as more than "how strict." *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md, "What to
    avoid early"; item 19)*
11. **Interfaith families and non-Jewish partners/guests** are a large and growing share of this exact audience, asking
    "what do I need to know before a seder/bar mitzvah/wedding I'm invited to?" *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md items
    17, 40; 18Doors sources)*
12. **Shiva and Jewish mourning basics are a real, repeatedly-adjacent gap** — what to do or say, what shiva actually
    involves. *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md Top 20 #20; APP_REVIEWS.md item 18)*
13. **Dense text can feel like gossip, not homework.** Miriam Anzovin's Talmud-as-story model ("Shalom, friends!") is
    the clearest example of making Torah/Talmud argument feel alive, not academic. *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md,
    "How the best creators hook people" #2; SOCIAL.md)*
14. **Rituals told with "families tell this a few different ways," not one official answer** — the glass-breaking
    story alone has four competing explanations in circulation. *(AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md, "What to avoid early")*
15. **Israel/Palestine drives the most comments and the most real-world danger (antisemitic attacks) but is explicitly
    out of scope for lesson content** — note it exists, never take a side, never build a lesson around it.
    *(REDDIT_TRENDING.md §4; AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md "What to avoid early")*
16. **No app currently teaches the "why" in three minutes.** Sefaria is a library with no path; Aleph Beta is deep
    paid video. The gap is a short daily "why do we do this" for people who already know the blessing but not the
    meaning. *(APP_REVIEWS.md, "Judaism" gap row)*

## 3. Proposed changes, ranked by impact

1. **Add an explicit "you belong, no test required" moment to Week 1 or very early Camp 2.** What: a short card
   saying Jewish identity is people, family and practice, not a belief or ancestry test — one parent, no parent,
   patrilineal, secular, questioning, all welcome; no one has to prove anything here. Where: new day inserted at
   Week 1 (days 1–7 currently fixed; add as day 8, the Camp 2 opener) or as the very first day of Camp 2 (day 22).
   Why: the two most-repeated real questions in the research (REDDIT.md #1, #2) and named directly as the Y4-day-1237
   gap. Size: small edit (one new day; borrow language already drafted for day 1237 and move a trimmed version
   earlier, keeping the fuller "Who is a Jew?" denominational mechanics at Y4).
2. **Fix the Yom Kippur fasting day (Y1 day 125) to state the health exemption plainly.** What: add one sentence —
   pregnancy, illness, or doctor's orders come first; ask a doctor and a rabbi. Why: this is a flagged safety
   misconception (REDDIT_ROUND2.md), and the current line ("like angels for a day") could read as fasting-at-all-costs
   without the edit. Where: Y1 day 125, in place. Size: small edit, same day.
3. **Move "not all Jews are of European descent" earlier, as its own delight-forward day.** What: pull the Beta
   Israel / India material (Y3 days 342–344) into a standalone early card, around Camp 2 or Camp 3 in Year 1 (e.g.
   near day 100–150), kept short and visual (synagogues, foods, names); leave the fuller Beta Israel/Sigd and India
   history material in Y3 for depth. Why: it was the single top-voted reply (280) in the Reddit research — a hook,
   not just a correction, and currently arrives 2.7 years in. Size: small edit — duplicate/relocate a light version;
   no need to remove the deeper Y3 treatment.
4. **Build a short "life questions" strand for shiva and interfaith-guest basics, pulled forward from Year 4 to
   Year 1 or 2.** What: 2–3 light, practical days — "what to do/say if you're invited to a shiva," "what to know as a
   non-Jewish guest at a seder or bar mitzvah" — distinct from the full Block F life-cycle treatment, which stays in
   Y4 for depth. Where: new short strand, Year 1 Camp 3 (practices) or early Year 2, days TBD by the owner. Why: both
   are named as live, frequent needs (a friend's loss or a wedding invite doesn't wait 3+ years) and both are flagged
   gaps in the research (APP_REVIEWS.md, AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md Top 20 #18, #20). Size: rewrite roughly half a
   week (new short strand), no change to the existing Y4 life-cycle block.
5. **Expand the Yiddish-words day (Week 1 day 3) or add a short companion day.** What: beyond "mensch," touch oy vey,
   mazel tov, chutzpah, kvetch, schlep in one light, funny roundup — "you've been speaking a little Jewish all along."
   Where: Week 1 day 3, or a new day right after it. Why: AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_JUDAISM.md calls this one of the most
   reliable, universal hooks, and the proposed first week already names it as the intended pattern. Size: small edit.

**Recurring patterns to apply across the outline (not single days):**
- **"You belong, no test required"** should recur a few times in Year 1–2, not just once at the open — e.g. near
  the denominations block (Y4) and near the "Who is a Jew?" day, reassuring again rather than treating it as settled
  after day one.
- **"Families tell this a few different ways"** (modeled once at the glass-breaking day) should become a named,
  repeatable framing — flag it explicitly in the script-writing workflow so writers reuse it whenever a ritual has
  competing origin stories (Hanukkah's miracle vs. rebellion framing, the red string, Tisha B'Av's many sorrows).
- **A hard-questions strand**, lightly touching "is it okay to be just culturally Jewish," "why argue instead of
  being told the answer," and antisemitism/safety in the gentlest possible terms (never the news cycle, never
  Israel/Palestine specifics) could sit in Year 2–3, after rituals already carry personal meaning.

## 4. Sensitive ground for this path

- **Israel and Palestine.** Out of scope for lesson content; the existing Y3 treatment (days 1025, 1034–1035) is
  already careful and should not expand. Never let a lesson become advocacy either direction; note the topic exists
  and move on.
- **Patrilineal descent and "who is a Jew."** Movements genuinely disagree (matrilineal-only vs. also-patrilineal
  with upbringing). State both plainly, as the outline already does at Y4 day 1237, and never imply one is more
  correct — this needs **Keeper (scholar) review** before it goes live, given how often it surfaces as a personal
  wound in the research.
- **Conversion recognition.** Different movements don't all recognize each other's conversions (Y4 day 1238 already
  notes this). Keep neutral; a convert reading this should never feel like an asterisk.
- **Antisemitism and safety.** Visible Jewish identity (kippah, Star of David, mezuzah) can carry real safety
  calculus for some users. If this is ever touched, keep it to "use your own judgment, many people do X" — never
  instructive about risk, and never tied to current events. Needs Keeper review before any safety-adjacent line ships.
- **Fasting and health.** Any lesson mentioning Yom Kippur, Tisha B'Av or other fast days needs the health-exemption
  line; treat as a standing rule for script review, not a one-time fix.
- **Interfaith families and intermarriage.** Keep descriptive and warm (several communities respond differently);
  avoid any line that reads as "whose fault" or "which side wins."

## 5. What stays

Week 1 (days 1–7) is a strong, well-targeted "start from a moment in their own life" build — the mezuzah, candles,
mensch, seder, the glass, bar/bat mitzvah, and machloket already match the research's top moments almost exactly.
The Talmud-as-living-argument treatment (machloket, the taste-of-Talmud block, Hillel/Shammai) is a genuine strength
and mirrors the single best model found (Miriam Anzovin) without copying her voice. The Israel/Palestine handling is
already careful and age-appropriate — don't touch it beyond small wording passes.

## 6. Estimated size of the revision

Small overall. Roughly: one new or relocated day near the Week 1/Camp 2 seam (belonging), one in-place sentence fix
(Y1 day 125), one relocated/duplicated day (Beta Israel/India delight hook), a new half-week strand (shiva and
interfaith-guest basics, 2–3 days) somewhere in Year 1–2, and one expanded day (Yiddish words). That's roughly 6–8
days touched or added out of 1,826 — under half a percent of the curriculum — plus a standing script-review rule
(fasting health exemption) and a reusable framing note ("families tell this a few ways") for future lesson-writing
workflows. No camp needs to be rebuilt; Year 1's structure holds.
