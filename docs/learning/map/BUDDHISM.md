# Five-year map: Buddhism

Step 1 only — a plan to review. Nothing in `docs/curriculum/buddhism` is edited here.

## 1. What the research says this audience needs

1. **"Do I have to believe in rebirth?"** is the single loudest worry across every source — five of six saved r/Buddhism threads, repeated in REDDIT_ROUND2. People like the teaching but can't accept rebirth and fear that disqualifies them. (`research-extra/REDDIT_REPLIES.md` §2, `REDDIT.md` §7)
2. **"Karma is not cosmic payback or punishment"** is the most-corrected idea in both the audience research and Reddit. "That's karma" (revenge meme) needs direct, early correction to "intention, and what it grows." (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` Top 20 #1, `REDDIT_REPLIES.md` §2)
3. **"Is meditation Buddhist, or did the apps make it secular?"** — the mindfulness-path audience's core confusion; they already meditate via Headspace/Calm and don't know the techniques are renamed vipassana/shamatha. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` §2 items 4, 30–33, §3)
4. **"Non-attachment isn't coldness"** — fear that practice means giving up loving family, partners, hobbies. Reassurance that lay life and love are expected; detachment ≠ non-attachment. (`REDDIT_REPLIES.md` §2, items 6–9)
5. **Heritage identity: "I grew up going to temple but never understood it — is my family's practice culture or real Buddhism?"** A recurring frame in diaspora sources; not one clean Reddit thread opened, but strong across multiple cites. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` §2 items 34–36, §3)
6. **Monk etiquette and the alms round** — shaved heads, robes, not eating after noon, "isn't feeding monks begging?" Very frequently asked, concrete, visual. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` Top 20 #6–7)
7. **Incense, bowing, the altar — "not to please a god"** — the single most common "explain my childhood" moment; three independent explainer sites exist just for the incense question. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` §4, Top 20 #2, #4)
8. **Buddha statue as home decor / in a bar, on a tattoo — is it disrespectful?** A viral 2026 Reddit thread (749 points) split between "customs differ by country" and "it's fine, he meets people where they are." Needs the "customs differ, don't police" frame. (`research-extra/REDDIT_ROUND2.md` §2, `REDDIT_TRENDING.md` §7)
9. **Vesak and festival variation** — birth, awakening or death on one day, and it genuinely differs by country/school; say "in many traditions…", never pick one as correct. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` Top 20 #8)
10. **Appropriation worry (mindfulness-path side)** — "can I use these ideas without 'becoming Buddhist'?" Needs a non-shaming, inviting answer, not gatekeeping. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` §2 item 31, §4)
11. **Buddhism vs. Stoicism** — a real, currently-trending comparison question (177–230-comment threads) and a good bridge for the Simply Spiritual door too. Needs an honest answer: similar practices, different reasons (no cosmic order vs. seeing impermanence/not-self). (`research-extra/REDDIT_ROUND2.md` §2)
12. **Trust in where teachings come from** — "AI content is an insult to the Dharma" and a viral fake-Buddha-quote meme show this audience is primed to distrust unsourced "Buddhist" content online. Matters for how the app itself is perceived, not just lesson content. (`research-extra/REDDIT_ROUND2.md` §2)
13. **Chanting in a language you don't understand** (Pali, Sanskrit, Vietnamese, Japanese) — the heritage-side mirror of the Hindu "chant without meaning" gap; strongly inferred, not independently sourced. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` §2 item 39)
14. **Mala beads and mudras** — ritual objects seen constantly, rarely explained; game-friendly, visual topics. (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_BUDDHISM.md` Top 20 #9–10)
15. **"How do I actually practice, daily?"** — a repeated, plain Reddit question; people want a simple routine, not more theory. (`research-extra/REDDIT.md` §7 items 16–19)

## 3. Proposed changes, ranked by impact

1. **Add "it's okay not to believe in rebirth yet" right where rebirth is first taught, not 300+ days later.** What: insert the *ehipassiko* reassurance (needs 1) into week 1 day 7 itself, and again as a short standalone day in camp 1 or early camp 2 (around day 22–30). Where: week 1 day 7 rewrite; new day near 23–30. Why: this is the #1 blocker to people even starting ("I like it but can't believe X disqualifies me"); currently the app states the idea beautifully but never gives the permission-to-doubt line until day 325. Size: small edit (day 7 wording) + one new day.
2. **Add a dedicated "is meditation Buddhist?" day early (camp 1 or camp 2).** What: directly address Headspace/Calm renaming vipassana → "insight meditation," honestly: yes in origin, something was left out, here's what. Where: new day, ideally days 8–15 (right after the week-1 hook, before camp 2's story arc starts). Why: this is the mindfulness-path audience's single biggest "where do I fit" question (need 3) and currently has zero coverage. Size: new day, small.
3. **Add a "loving your family isn't against the practice" day.** What: non-attachment vs. detachment, lay life, "if a concept doesn't make you more compassionate, you haven't understood it yet." Where: near camp 2's palace/Yasodhara days (29–35) where the theme already brushes against it — widen day 30 or add a day right after it. Why: a repeated, anxious Reddit question (need 4); the hook (Yasodhara) already exists, it just doesn't land the lesson. Size: small edit or one new day.
4. **Add a short "culture or religion?" day for heritage users.** What: name the tension directly — temple attendance, language classes, family ritual all taught as one package in heritage communities (GDPT, BCA) — and say both can be true, culture and the real teaching, without one cancelling the other. Where: camp 1 or early camp 2 (days 10–25), near the "explain my childhood" cluster. Why: a recurring diaspora-essay theme (need 5) with zero current coverage, and the parallel Hindu research flagged the identical gap — likely worth the same fix pattern across doors. Size: new day, small.
5. **Add "is it okay to display/wear a Buddha image?" day.** What: the viral billboard question — customs differ by country (never near feet/toilets in Buddhist countries; relaxed in the West), answer with "customs differ, we don't police," not a scolding tone. Where: pair with the existing incense/altar week (y1 d138–144) or earlier, since the research itself recommended year 1. Why: currently missing (need 8); it's a question heritage kids get asked by friends and non-Buddhist users ask themselves. Size: new day, small.
6. **Add "Buddhism and Stoicism" as a bridge day.** What: honest comparison — similar calm, different reasons (no cosmic Logos vs. seeing impermanence/not-self); call out that pop "mindfulness" versions of both look more alike than they are. Where: year 2, after the four-noble-truths block closes (around day 352–400), matching the research's own "Year 2" placement. Why: a currently trending, repeatable comparison question (need 11) and a stated cross-door bridge to Simply Spiritual users. Size: new day, small.
7. **Add a mala beads / mudras day.** What: the 108 beads, how they're used, is it like a rosary; the Buddha's hand positions and what each means — visual, game-friendly. Where: y1 camp 3 (weeks 15–20, days 97–144), alongside the shrine/chant week. Why: named explicitly in the research's top-20 list (needs 14) and absent from the actual outline despite camp 3 being exactly the "practices" camp where it belongs. Size: new day or two, small.
8. **Add a "chanting in a language you don't fully understand" day.** What: name the pattern honestly (Pali, Sanskrit, Vietnamese, Japanese chants memorized before meaning), same gap the Hindu research found; give the meaning behind one common chant (the three refuges, or *Buddham saranam gacchami*, already in week 1's say-it list). Where: y1 camp 3, near the refuges/chant days (123, 138). Why: strongly inferred heritage gap (need 13); low-risk since week 1 already has the phonetic chant — this just adds the "why unclear chants are normal" framing. Size: small edit to an existing day.
9. **Appropriation reassurance for the mindfulness-path audience.** What: a short, non-shaming answer — you can learn and practice without "becoming Buddhist"; the tradition invites rather than gatekeeps (*ehipassiko* again). Where: pair with the "is meditation Buddhist" day (change #2) rather than a separate day, to avoid over-weighting one audience. Why: real worry (need 10), but lower volume than #1–#3; folding it into #2 is cheaper and keeps the week-one pacing light. Size: fold into an existing new day, no extra size.

## 4. Sensitive ground

- **Rebirth and karma**: never present physics ("energy conservation") or past-life-hypnosis material as proof — Reddit itself flagged both as weak/contradictory to Buddhist no-self teaching. Teach the shared shape (action has consequence; something continues without a fixed soul) and say traditions describe the mechanism differently.
- **Rankings**: never rank Theravada vs. Mahayana vs. Vajrayana, or claim one Vesak date/story is the only one. The outline already does this well (y4 Block J "never ranked"); keep it.
- **Nuns' ordination**: the Theravada bhikkhuni lineage question (y1 d302) is a live, two-sided issue within the tradition — keep the "both views held in good faith" framing, Keeper review before any rewrite.
- **Appropriation vs. gatekeeping**: don't shame the mindfulness-path audience; also don't pretend meditation has no Buddhist roots. Keeper review especially for the "is meditation Buddhist" and "appropriation" days — this is the place most likely to upset either heritage or convert users if handled clumsily.
- **AI/sourcing trust**: given the "AI content insults the Dharma" and fake-quote-meme findings, every quoted sutta line needs a real citation (the outline already cites MN/SN/DN/AN numbers — good) and the app should never imply a quote is the Buddha's own words without a source. This is a production discipline, not a lesson topic.
- **Heritage specificity**: don't collapse Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese, Tibetan, Cambodian, Sri Lankan, Chinese and Korean practice into one "Asian Buddhism." Joss-paper burning is Taoist/folk as much as Buddhist; name mixes honestly.

## 5. What stays

The outline's spine is strong and shouldn't move: karma correction on day 1, the Buddha's whole life told as a story (camp 2), the cumulative practice build (camp 3), reading the whole Dhammapada (camp 4), and the never-ranked elective pairing of Theravada and Vajrayana (y1 camp 5, y4 Block J) all match what the research says works. Week 1's "in many traditions…" framing for Vesak and its honest, plain-English four-truths day are exactly right and need no change.

## 6. Estimated size of the revision

Small. Roughly 6–8 new or rewritten days total, concentrated in camp 1/early camp 2 (days ~7–30, for needs 1–5) and y1 camp 3 (days ~97–144, for needs 7–8), plus one new day in year 2 (~352–400, need 6). No camp needs restructuring, no week needs to move, and nothing needs cutting — this is additive, on the order of one week's worth of content spread across two years.
