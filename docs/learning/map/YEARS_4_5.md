# Years 4–5 after the five-year map (2026-10-06)

Years 4–5 are planned in outline (`docs/curriculum/<door>/y4.md`, `y5.md`); year 4 lessons are partly written
outside `scripts/` (see HANDOFF, the `write-lessons-new-process` workflow). The map's "too late" findings were handled
in job 1 by writing an **early, light answer in years 1–3 and keeping the deeper later lesson**. So the year 4–5 plan
needs no reordering. What it does need when those years are written or reviewed:

- **Point back.** Where a year 4–5 lesson goes deep on a topic that now has an early answer, open with one line that
  points back (e.g. Christianity's prayer dryness, Islam's hijab and mental health, Hindu caste, Jewish belonging,
  Sikh cut hair). Don't repeat the early lesson.
- **Christianity, year 4 (left for later on purpose):** women pastors and sexuality and the Bible (days around
  1098, 1108, 1218, 1316, 1383). Handle with "traditions differ", never a ruling; Keeper and pastor review together.
- **Islam, year 4:** days 1376, 1378, 1391 and 1400 stay the fuller versions of hijab, music and tattoos, mental
  health and converts; they can now assume day 113, 115, 131, 139, 144, 148 and 151.
- **Hinduism, year 5:** the caste and diaspora material (~day 1450+) stays deep; it can assume days 95 and 222.
- **Every path:** apply the job 2 style (`JOB2_BRIEF.md`) and add `games.guess` as each year 4–5 lesson is written.
- **Crisis lines** belong in any year 4–5 lesson on despair, grief, abuse or "what's the point".
