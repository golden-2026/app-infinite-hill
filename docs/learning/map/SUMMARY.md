# The five-year map: summary (2026-10-06)

Eight readers each compared all the research (Reddit old and new, YouTube comments, Instagram/TikTok, rival app
reviews, the audience reports) with one path's five-year outline. Full maps: `docs/learning/map/<PATH>.md`.

## The headline
The outlines are mostly sound. The research doesn't say "throw it out"; it says **the questions people actually carry
show up too late, or not at all**. Most changes land in **year 1**: answer the real worry early, warmly, then go deep later.

## Patterns for every path
1. **"You're not behind / you belong"** early: cut hair, secular Jew, missed prayers, cradle Catholic who never learned.
2. **The hard question each path raises, answered in year 1**, not years 3–5.
3. **"Traditions differ on this"** framing wherever sects or families disagree.
4. **Celebrate firsts** (first Shabbat, first turban, first Mass back, first full prayer).
5. **Crisis care near grief and despair lessons** (988), and in the Guide.
6. **Exact facts, cited quotes, human-made art for holy figures** (people catch errors and fakes, and dislike AI art).

## Top changes by path (about 6–12 days per path, mostly year 1)
- **Hinduism:** "is my offering enough?" (Gita 9.26); a week of "things you grew up with, explained" (tilak, red thread,
  nazar); a wedding and life-rituals week; Krishna's dark skin and colorism; caste and diaspora identity moved from
  year 5 to year 1.
- **Buddhism:** "it's okay not to believe in rebirth yet" moved from day 325 to week 1; "is meditation Buddhist?" (vs
  Headspace/Calm); non-attachment as better love; displaying a Buddha statue respectfully; culture vs religion.
- **Christianity:** "prayer feels empty" moved from year 4 to year 1; "church hurt me, not Christ" moved earlier; an
  honest "is it a sin?" day; pastors and money; light Genesis puzzles (Cain's wife).
- **Catholic:** "can I come back?" (fear of sin, mercy over shame); first Mass back ("the hardest part is the door");
  faith and science (evolution); Confirmation names; Hispanic and Filipino family practice.
- **Judaism:** "you belong, no test" (now not until year 4); Jews of India and Ethiopia moved from year 3 to year 1;
  more Yiddish; shiva and seder guest etiquette earlier. (The fasting lesson already exempts the ill and at-risk.)
- **Islam:** the pray-stop-restart cycle, named and normalized; "low iman"; dating; doubt and mental health moved from
  years 1/4 to early year 1; hijab, music and converts moved from year 4 to year 1.
- **Sikhism:** "can I still be Sikh?" (cut hair, no amrit, no Punjabi) moved from day 272 to week 1; explaining
  yourself to outsiders; when Sikhs are targeted (chardi kala); can't read Gurmukhi is fine; first turban; fake AI
  photos of the Gurus.
- **Simply Spiritual:** "am I doing it wrong?" moved from day 304 into the first 96 days; a week on manifesting, tarot
  and spotting a spiritual grifter; "I left my religion, now what?"; zen, ego death, higher self; year 2's hooks lead
  with the feeling, not the philosopher's name.

## Sensitive ground (Keeper review before shipping)
Caste and colorism (Hindu); rebirth claims and nuns' ordination (Buddhist); hell, sexuality, women pastors, Old
Testament violence (Christian); mortal sin and clergy abuse (Catholic); Israel/Palestine, who is a Jew, antisemitism
safety (Jewish); hijab, Aisha's age, doubting family members (Muslim); 1984, Khalistan, attacks (Sikh); psychedelics and
grifters, never naming living teachers (Simply Spiritual). No political sides anywhere.

## Caveats
- Several maps read only the headings of years 2–5, and the outlines are one line per day. Each change must be checked
  against the actual lesson before rewriting (one flag, the Yom Kippur fast, was already handled in the lesson).
- The maps size the **content** changes (about 70–90 lessons across all paths, plus Simply Spiritual's year-2 hooks).
  The **style** pass ("week one" feel for every lesson after day 21) is separate and much bigger.

## Two jobs, two sizes
1. **The content changes above** (~70–90 lessons, mostly year 1): moderate; can be written and reviewed per path.
2. **The style pass** over every written lesson after day 21 (about 8,300 lessons, years 1–3; years 4–5 are planned in
   outline): the big job; needs "use a workflow".
