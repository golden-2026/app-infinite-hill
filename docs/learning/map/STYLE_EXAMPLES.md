# Before and after: the style pass (pilot, days 22–34)

Two real lessons. Same story, same facts, same sources; shorter, no homework, no "the bell holds the time".

## Christianity, day 30: The rich fool (Luke 12:16–21)

**Spoken words:** 582 → 480

### hook

**Before:** It starts with a family fight. Someone in the crowd shouts at Jesus: tell my brother to split the inheritance with me. And Jesus won't referee. Instead, he tells a story. A rich man's land has a bumper year. More grain than he's ever seen. More than his barns can hold. So he lies awake, and he talks it over with himself. What shall I do? I know. I'll tear down my barns and build bigger ones. I'll store everything. And then I'll say to my soul: you're set for years. Relax. Eat, drink, enjoy. It's a good plan. Any financial advisor would sign off on it. And that same night, God speaks to him, for the only time in the story, and calls him a fool. Today, fool.

**After:** It starts with a family fight. Someone in the crowd shouts at Jesus: tell my brother to split the inheritance with me. Jesus won't referee. He tells a story instead. A rich man's land has a bumper year, more grain than his barns can hold. He lies awake, talking it over with himself. What shall I do? I know: I'll tear down my barns and build bigger ones. I'll store everything. Then I'll say to my soul: you're set for years. Eat, drink, enjoy. It's a good plan. Any financial advisor would sign off on it. And that same night, God speaks to him, for the only time in the story, and calls him a fool.

### practice

**Before:** Here's the practice: find one thing you're only storing. Settle, and breathe. Walk through your home in your mind. The closet. The drawer you don't open. The shelf in the garage. Find one thing you're keeping just to keep it. A coat you never wear. A book you've finished. Picture who could use it. If you pray, you can ask where it should go. If you don't, just picture it leaving your barn and landing somewhere it's needed. The bell holds the time.

**After:** Walk through your home in your mind: the closet, the drawer you never open. Find one thing you keep just to keep it, like a coat you never wear, and picture who could use it. If you pray, ask where it should go; if not, just picture it leaving your barn and landing where it's needed.

### carry

**Before:** Your line: you can't take the barn. Today, give away the one thing you found. A coat, a book, a tool. Out of the barn and into somebody's hands. Day thirty.

**After:** Your line: you can't take the barn. But you can be rich toward God. Thirty days now, a whole month.

### new "what do you think it means?" choices

- someone who lives as if God weren't there (right)
- someone short on brains or common sense
- a jester who entertains a king's court

## Islam, day 26: The whisper

**Spoken words:** 542 → 481

### hook

**Before:** It's two in the morning and you can't sleep. A voice in your head starts up. Not loud. It never shouts. Just reasonable. You know, one more episode won't hurt. Actually, you deserve that. Or the other kind: they were laughing at you, weren't they. You'll never be good enough. It sounds exactly like your own voice. That's what makes it work. Today, the garden has everything, and one tree, and then someone starts to talk. Not with a shout, or a threat. With a whisper.

**After:** It's two in the morning and you can't sleep. A voice in your head starts up. It never shouts. Just reasonable. One more episode won't hurt. You deserve that. Or the other kind: they were laughing at you, weren't they. You'll never be good enough. It sounds exactly like your own voice. That's what makes it work. Today, the garden has everything, and one tree, and then someone starts to talk. Not with a shout, or a threat. With a whisper.

### practice

**Before:** The bell will give you two minutes. Sit quietly, and just listen to your own mind. Thoughts will come. Some will be kind, some will be sharp, some will be selling you something. Don't argue with any of them. When one comes that pulls at you, a craving, a cruel line about yourself, a grudge, just label it softly: whisper. And let it pass, like a car going by outside. If you pray, you can say a'udhu billah, I seek refuge in God, the words Muslims use when a whisper comes. The bell holds the time.

**After:** Listen to your own mind; thoughts will come, some kind, some sharp, some selling you something. Don't argue with any of them, but when one pulls at you, a craving, a cruel line about yourself, a grudge, label it softly, whisper, and let it pass like a car going by outside. If you pray, you can say a'udhu billah, I seek refuge in God, the words Muslims use when a whisper comes.

### carry

**Before:** Your line: not every voice is yours. Today, catch one whisper, the craving, the cruel line, the grudge, and let it pass without doing what it says. Day twenty-six.

**After:** Your line: not every voice is yours. The story isn't over yet. See you tomorrow.

### new "what do you think it means?" choices

- a quiet whisper that pulls you toward wrong (right)
- a shouted order to break God's law outright
- a guilty feeling that comes after a mistake
