# Five-year map: Islam (2026-10-06)

Reviewed against `docs/curriculum/islam/y1.md`–`y5.md` (days 1–1,826) and the new `docs/learning/week1/ISLAM.json`
(days 1–21, already live). Nothing in `docs/curriculum` is changed in this step — this is the plan for the owner
to review first.

## 1. What the research says this audience needs

1. The Arabic-without-meaning gap is the single loudest, most-repeated finding: people recite salah and even
   memorize Qur'an without knowing what the words say (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md §1, §3; REDDIT.md #5 "the Arabic
   gap"; THREADS_AND_COMMENTS.md §5 "Arabic: the distinction that clicks").
2. The #1 everyday struggle is the "pray two weeks, stop, start again" cycle — not doubt, not theology
   (REDDIT.md #5; REDDIT_REPLIES.md §6 "the theme is one thing"; THREADS_AND_COMMENTS.md §5 "salah guilt is the
   everyday struggle").
3. "Low iman" is the community's own word for spiritual dryness, with no doctrine behind it, peaking around Ramadan
   (REDDIT.md #5, patterns 2).
4. People feel shame, not sin, around missed prayers — "am I a bad Muslim?" — and the warm answer (start with one
   prayer, come back after a lapse) beats fear-based lines every time (REDDIT_REPLIES.md §6c–d;
   THREADS_AND_COMMENTS.md §5 takeaway 1, 4).
5. The most-liked comments on the biggest Islam videos are literally suicidal or grieving people — crisis care must
   be visible here, not an afterthought (THREADS_AND_COMMENTS.md §5 "the lowest point is very literal").
6. Doubt is treated as normal and even healthy ("more questions means you're reading well"), and Yaqeen's whole
   brand is built on this (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md §1, Top 20 #8; REDDIT_ROUND2.md §6).
7. "Is it haram to date?" is the most-asked teen question in this research, alongside music and tattoos as
   genuinely contested (not flatly settled) rulings (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md §2 "daily rules," Top 20 #12, #13,
   #17).
8. Mercy, not fear, is the actual center of the faith by the Qur'an's own weighting, and this is exactly what
   doubting seekers say they need to hear first (REDDIT_ROUND2.md §6 "seeking God through love, not fear").
9. New Muslims (converts/reverts) need from-scratch teaching with zero assumed knowledge, and say born Muslims
   sometimes gatekeep who "counts" (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md §3 "what converts say is missing"; REDDIT.md #5
   "new-Muslim overwhelm"; REDDIT_TRENDING.md §5 "converts and returners").
10. Hijab, modesty and gender-conduct questions are high-engagement and sensitive; the ask is "dignity, not blame,"
    for both men and women, not a one-sided lecture (REDDIT_TRENDING.md §5, §Islam proposal; AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md
    Top 20 #10).
11. Sunni/Shia differences are real and come up (Ashura, "prove God to atheists" threads, family-drift threads) and
    must never be ranked — the outline already has a strong model for this in year 3 (REDDIT_ROUND2.md §6; this is
    a strength to protect, not a gap).
12. Sacred seasons (Ramadan, the two Eids, Laylat al-Qadr, Arafah) are the highest, most reliable engagement spikes
    and the most-lived, least-explained-as-one-arc practices (SOCIAL.md §Islam; AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md Top 20
    #5–7, #15).
13. Confrontational/debate framing (the Hijab-style lane) draws reach but real controversy even among Muslims —
    explicitly not a model (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md §1, "what to avoid early").
14. Hard, contested history (Aisha's age, slavery, women's rights, apostasy, family members drifting from faith)
    needs careful, later handling with the range of scholarship shown side by side, never settled one way
    (REDDIT_ROUND2.md §6 flags).
15. Utility prayer apps dominate this market and trust is shaky (ads, Muslim Pro's 2020 data-sale scandal); the
    clear open space is an ad-free, privacy-first daily lesson on the *why* of salah and Ramadan — exactly this
    app's pitch (APP_REVIEWS.md "Islam" and cross-cutting lessons).

## 3. Proposed changes, ranked by impact

1. **New lesson: the pray-stop-restart cycle, named and normalized.** Where: early Y1, Camp 3 (salat block, around
   days 112–119, right after "one whole prayer" lands). Why: the single most-repeated Reddit pattern across r/islam
   and r/MuslimLounge (REDDIT.md #5; REDDIT_REPLIES.md §6). Say both things research flags: the duty is five
   prayers, and nearly everyone rebuilds by starting with one and adding on — our rest-day streak design already
   matches this, so the lesson can say so directly. Size: small edit — one new day, written carefully to avoid
   picking a side on the gradualism-vs-obligation question (REDDIT_REPLIES.md §6d flags this explicitly).

2. **New lesson: "is it haram to date?"** Where: Y1, after the halal/haram block (around day 148, where "the gray
   area" already sits) or as a Camp 3 add. Why: the most-asked teen question in this research and currently absent
   entirely (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md Top 20 #12). Treat like the existing music/tattoo model: here's what's
   agreed, here's what's genuinely discussed, framed as "different communities and scholars draw this line in
   different places" rather than a flat ruling. Size: small edit, one new day.

3. **Move hijab, music-and-tattoos-as-contested, and converts/reverts from year 4 into year 1.** Where: currently
   Y4 days 1376, 1378, 1400 → move into Y1, ideally into the existing Camp 3 "practices" block (days 143–156) or as
   a short new mini-week right after it. Why: these are daily-lived, high-interest, Top-20 topics
   (AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_ISLAM.md; SOCIAL.md top Yaqeen posts are literally hijab and heartbreak) that the research
   says should build trust early, not wait four years. The tattoo topic should be added alongside music since it's
   equally contested and currently has zero coverage. Size: rewrite part of a week — roughly 4–5 days moved/added,
   plus a corresponding trim or rename of the Y4 "diverse ummah" block so it isn't left thin.

4. **New lesson: "low iman," named and welcomed.** Where: Y1, paired with or right after the pray-stop-restart
   lesson (recommendation #1), and lightly echoed in a Ramadan-season day (Y1 days 120–129 block already exists —
   add one line acknowledging the Ramadan iman dip specifically, since REDDIT.md notes it peaks there). Why: this is
   the community's own name for a universal feeling and currently never named in the curriculum. Size: small edit.

5. **Bring "is doubt okay?" forward from day 297 (al-Ghazali) to Camp 1/early Camp 2**, matching what week 1 already
   started at day 5. Where: Y1, early Camp 2 (days 22–30 range, alongside the Adam/whisper story which already
   touches "not every voice is yours"). Why: this is a Top-20, Quora-verbatim question and the research is explicit
   that Yaqeen's entire growth is built on answering it early, not as a year-1 history lesson about a scholar's
   crisis four months in. Keep al-Ghazali's story later as the deeper version. Size: small edit — add a short
   standalone day, keep day 297 as is.

6. **Bring mental-health-and-faith forward from Y4 day 1391 to Y1**, as a short, gentle pairing with the
   pray-stop-restart and low-iman lessons (recommendations #1 and #4). Why: THREADS_AND_COMMENTS.md's top comments
   on the biggest Islam videos are literally suicidal and grieving people; the existing line ("many Muslim voices
   now encourage therapy alongside prayer") is good and just needs to reach users years earlier, tied to crisis
   care. Size: small edit, duplicate or move the day, keep the Y4 slot for the fuller "today's Islam" treatment if
   useful, or simply move it.

7. **Apply the "traditions differ, neither ranked" framing (already excellent for Sunni/Shia in year 3) to the new
   dating/music/tattoo/hijab lessons above.** This is a recurring pattern to carry forward, not a new lesson: every
   contested-ruling day should read like Y4 day 1400 ("scholars differ... respect a view") rather than a flat
   statement. Size: no new days — a style note applied while writing #2 and #3.

8. **Apply a "you're not behind" moment to the new consistency/low-iman lessons.** Pattern already used elsewhere in
   the app; here it should explicitly borrow the "start with one prayer, then two" ladder from the Reddit research
   (REDDIT_REPLIES.md §6c) rather than inventing new language. No new days — applied while writing #1 and #4.

## 4. Sensitive ground

- **Aisha's age and marriage history.** Real, contested, raised by young Muslims who encounter it online
  (REDDIT_ROUND2.md §6, "my sister is slipping away from Islam"). Do not add a lesson that settles it; if it's ever
  addressed, it belongs in year 2+ as a "range of Muslim scholarship" treatment, with Keeper review before writing a
  word, and only alongside a plan for how the Guide answers it when asked directly (which it must, per the Guide
  playbook — never refuse).
- **Gradualism vs. the five-prayer obligation.** Reddit's own pastoral advice ("start with one") can read as
  softening a duty. Recommendation #1 must state both plainly: the duty is five, and building up is how real people
  get there. Keeper review before publishing.
- **Sunni/Shia.** Already handled well in year 3 — any new day that touches Ashura, the caliphate, or Karbala should
  be reviewed against that existing model, not written fresh.
- **Hijab and gender-conduct debates.** Keep the new, earlier hijab lesson (recommendation #3) to "many choices, a
  devotion," as the existing Y4 day already does — do not expand it into the MuslimLounge gender-conduct arguments
  (REDDIT_TRENDING.md §5), which stay later and need Keeper review given how heated those threads run.
- **Apostasy and doubting family members.** The Guide must stay kind to both the doubter and the anxious relative
  (REDDIT_ROUND2.md §6d) — this is a Guide-playbook concern more than a curriculum one, worth flagging to whoever
  maintains `docs/GUIDE_PLAYBOOK.md`.
- **LGBTQ and Islam, interfaith marriage, and any Gaza/Israel-adjacent political content.** All surfaced in the
  research as real questions this audience meets online; all explicitly out of scope for this map, per the owner's
  "stay out of politics" and "never rank traditions" rules. Not proposed here at all.
- Every new or moved day above needs a repeated Keeper review pass before any year goes live, per standing project
  rules — nothing here is cleared to ship on this map alone.

## 5. What stays

- The seerah (Camp 2), the Sunni/Shia history in year 3, and the Qur'an-structure strand (Camp 4) are thorough,
  well-sourced, and already follow the "describe, don't rank" rule the research asks for. Keep as is.
- Week 1 (days 1–21) was just rebuilt from this exact research and is in good shape — bismillah, Al-Fatiha,
  wudu, inshallah/mashallah, doubt-as-iman, Ramadan-to-Eid. No changes proposed to week 1 here.
- The names-of-Allah strand (mercy-forward, not fear-forward) is exactly what the research asks for in substance;
  it just needs the earlier-arriving companions proposed above (#1, #2, #4, #5, #6).

## 6. Estimated size of this revision

Small. Roughly 10–12 days total change across year 1: about 5 new days (pray-stop-restart, low iman, dating, is
doubt okay, mental health) and about 5–7 days moved earlier from year 4 (hijab, music/tattoo-as-contested,
converts/reverts) with a light backfill of the year 4 slots they leave. No new camps, no week rewritten wholesale,
and years 2, 3 and 5 are untouched. This is a touch-up to year 1's early camps, not a restructuring.
