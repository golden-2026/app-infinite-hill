# Five-year map: brief for each path (2026-10-06)

The owner: "how are your learnings impacting the full five years of curriculum? i assume you're gonna need to revise
the full curriculum based on what you learn from your study." This map is step 1: a plan the owner reviews before any
lesson changes. Nothing in docs/curriculum is edited in this step.

## Read
Research, all under `docs/learning/` (use your path's sections; skim the cross-path ones):
- `AUDIENCE_RESEARCH*.md` (your path's report; Hinduism's is `AUDIENCE_RESEARCH.md`)
- `research-extra/REDDIT.md`, `REDDIT_REPLIES.md`, `REDDIT_TRENDING.md`, `REDDIT_ROUND2.md`
- `research-extra/THREADS_AND_COMMENTS.md` (YouTube), `SOCIAL.md` (Instagram/TikTok), `APP_REVIEWS.md`
- `week1/<DOOR>.json` (the new first camp, days 1–21, now live)

The five-year outline (365 days a year, 1,826 days):
- `docs/curriculum/<door>/y1.md` … `y5.md` (camps, weeks, one line per day). Hinduism has no y-files: use
  `packages/content/generated/outline-hinduism.js` and `docs/curriculum/golden_hinduism_years2-5.md`.
- Years 1–3 also have written scripts (`docs/curriculum/<door>/scripts/y1..y3/day-NNNN.json`); read only a sample
  if you need to see how a topic is actually taught.

Owner rules that shape the map: light and fun early, scholarly depth later; at most one famous text named a day early;
stories open with the drama; say it once; no homework asks; warm mascot voice; never rank traditions or sects; stay out
of politics; facts exact; crisis care (988); meet people where they are; real teaching, not a religion class.

## Write `docs/learning/map/<DOOR>.md`
1. **What the research says this audience needs** (10–15 bullets, each tagged with its source file).
2. **Coverage check:** for each need, where the outline covers it now (year/camp/days) and a verdict:
   covered well · covered too late · covered too early/too heavy · missing.
3. **Proposed changes**, ranked by impact, each with: what, where (year/camp/day range), why (source), size
   (small edit / rewrite a week / new camp). Include: topics to add, to move earlier or later, to lighten, to cut or
   merge; recurring patterns to apply ("you're not behind" moments, "traditions differ" framing, milestone moments,
   a hard-questions strand, the "week one" style for days 22+).
4. **Sensitive ground** for this path and how/when to handle it (and what Keeper review is needed).
5. **What stays**: what the outline already does well (keep it short).
6. **Estimated size of the revision** for this path: how many weeks/days change, roughly.
Plain American English; specific day numbers; no fluff. Keep it under ~2,500 words.
