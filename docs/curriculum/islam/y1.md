# CAMP 3 · THE PRACTICES (Days 97–156) — "I can do this"
*Format: how-to. Hook is short; the practice is the session. Cumulative. Where schools differ in detail, the session says so without ranking.*

## Weeks 15–16 · Salat, step by step (Days 97–112)
97. **The intention** · niyyah · every prayer begins in the heart, not the tongue · pause before starting anything · *mean it first*
98. **Clean body, clean place** · taharah · wudu, clean clothes, a clean spot, and the idea behind them · lay a clean cloth down · *a clean corner*
99. **Facing the House** · qiblah · wherever you are, you face the Ka'ba in Mecca · find your qiblah with a compass · *all turned one way*
100. **The five times** · — · dawn, midday, afternoon, sunset, night, and how the sun sets the clock · look up today's five times · *the sun keeps the times*
101. **Allahu akbar** · takbir · hands raised, the prayer opens, the world is set behind you · raise your hands, say it once · *greater than all of it*
102. **Standing and reciting** · qiyam · you stand and recite al-Fatiha, then a short surah · stand still, recite the Fatiha · *stand and speak*
103. **The bow** · ruku' · back straight, hands on knees, glory to my Lord the Great · bow once, say it three times · *bowing is honest*
104. **Rising from the bow** · — · you rise saying Allah hears whoever praises Him · rise slowly, whisper it · *he hears*
105. **The prostration** · sujud · forehead on the ground, glory to my Lord the Most High, the closest a person gets · one prostration, unhurried · *closest when lowest*
106. **Sitting between** · jalsa · a short sitting between prostrations, asking forgiveness · sit, breathe, ask once · *a breath between*
107. **The testimony in the sitting** · tashahhud · the finger raised, the greeting on the Prophet ﷺ and the righteous · learn the first line · *peace on us and the good*
108. **The peace to both sides** · taslim · turn right, then left: peace be upon you and the mercy of Allah · turn right and left · *peace, both ways*
109. **Counting the units** · rak'ah · two at dawn, four, four, three at sunset, four at night · count on your fingers once · *two, four, four, three, four*
110. **Different hands, same prayer** · madhhab · schools differ on hand placement and small details, and all are valid within the tradition · watch two people pray differently, calmly · *many hands, one prayer*
111. **When you can't stand** · — · you pray sitting, lying, or by signs; the prayer bends to the body · pray one sitting prayer · *the prayer fits you*
112. **One whole prayer** · — · putting it together: dawn prayer, two units, start to finish · pray two rak'ahs · *the whole thing*

## Week 17 · Dhikr with the fingers (Days 113–119)

115. **Alhamdulillah, thirty-three** · tahmid · all praise to Allah, for what is given · thirty-three on your fingers · *thanks, again*
116. **Allahu akbar, thirty-four** · — · the Prophet ﷺ taught this set to his daughter Fatimah instead of a servant (Bukhari) · the full hundred after a prayer · *better than a servant*
117. **La ilaha illallah** · tahlil · the heaviest words on the scale, no god but Allah · say it ten times · *there is only one*
118. **Astaghfirullah, a hundred times** · istighfar · asking forgiveness a hundred times a day, as he did (Muslim) · a hundred on the beads or fingers · *i ask forgiveness*
119. **Prayers on the Prophet ﷺ** · salawat · sending blessings on him, returned tenfold (Muslim) · ten salawat, slowly · *peace on him*

## Weeks 18–19 · Ramadan and the two Eids, as they arrive (Days 120–129)
120. **Ramadan: the new moon** · hilal · the month begins when the crescent is seen or the month is counted · look for the moon tonight · *watch for the crescent*
121. **Ramadan: the early meal** · suhur · eat before dawn, for there is blessing in it (Bukhari) · plan tomorrow's pre-dawn water · *blessing before dawn*
122. **Ramadan: breaking with a date** · iftar · at sunset, a date and water, and a du'a · break one fast gently · *sweet at sunset*

125. **Ramadan: the fast of the tongue** · — · whoever doesn't leave false speech, Allah has no need of his hunger (Bukhari) · fast from one unkind word · *fast with the tongue too*

## Week 20 · Du'a in your own words (Days 130–136)
130. **Just ask** · du'a · du'a is asking Allah directly, any time, in any language · ask for one thing plainly · *just ask*

## Week 21 · The mosque visit explained (Days 137–142)

138. **Shoes off, right foot in** · — · shoes at the door, enter with the right foot, a greeting prayer on the lips · practice entering a room slowly · *come in gently*

## Week 22 · Halal, plainly (Days 143–148)
143. **Halal and haram** · halal · permitted and forbidden, with most of life in the wide permitted middle · list five plainly halal joys · *most things are allowed*
144. **Good and wholesome** · tayyib · eat of what is lawful and good (2:168), not just permitted but good · eat one wholesome thing, slowly · *lawful and good*
145. **The name on the meat** · dhabihah · the animal is killed swiftly, with Allah's name, a sharp blade, and mercy · say bismillah before eating · *mercy even here*
146. **Intoxicants set aside** · khamr · wine and intoxicants are set aside because their harm outweighs their benefit (2:219; 5:90) · notice what clouds your mind · *keep the mind clear*
147. **The gray area** · — · between the clear halal and clear haram are doubtful things, and whoever avoids them protects himself (Nawawi 6) · skip one doubtful thing today · *leave what makes you doubt*
148. **Halal earning** · rizq · halal is also how money is made: honest scales, no cheating, no interest (83:1–3; 2:275) · check one thing you earn or spend · *clean money*

## Week 23 · Charity as habit (Days 149–155)
149. **A smile is charity** · sadaqah · even meeting your brother with a cheerful face is charity (Tirmidhi) · smile at three people · *a face can give*
150. **Charity from the right hand** · — · give so the left hand doesn't know what the right gave (Bukhari) · give one secret gift · *hidden is best*
151. **Zakat, plainly** · nisab · two and a half percent of savings held a year above a threshold, to the poor · learn your own nisab figure · *wealth has a share in it*
152. **The eight who receive** · — · the Qur'an names who zakat is for: the poor, the needy, debtors, travelers and others (9:60) · learn the eight categories · *it has addresses*
153. **Charity that keeps flowing** · sadaqah jariyah · a well, a tree, knowledge taught: good that runs after you're gone (Muslim) · plant or teach something · *the well keeps giving*
154. **The neighbor** · — · Jibril kept urging kindness to the neighbor until the Prophet ﷺ thought neighbors would inherit (Bukhari) · take something to a neighbor · *start next door*
155. **Removing a stone from the road** · — · taking harm out of a path is a branch of faith (Muslim) · clear one obstacle for others · *clear the path*

## Week 23 · Close (Day 156)

# CAMP 4 · THE TEXT (Days 157–231) — "the thirtieth part"
*Juz' 'Amma, read from the end, the way children learn it: Arabic sound first, English meaning second (Pickthall), one verse carried each day. Mostly early Meccan surahs: short, rhythmic, about the Day, the orphan, and the One.*

## Week 24 · How to hear it (Days 157–158)
157. **Hearing before reading** · tilawah · the Qur'an is first recited, heard, memorized, and only then read · listen to one recited surah · *hear it first*
158. **Seeking refuge first** · ta'awwudh · recitation begins with refuge from Shaytan, then bismillah (16:98) · say both, then be silent · *refuge, then the name*

## Weeks 24–25 · The last surahs (Days 159–176)
159. **An-Nas: the people** · — · refuge with the Lord of people from the whisperer who withdraws (Surah 114) · recite it before sleep · *the whisperer withdraws*
160. **Al-Falaq: the daybreak** · — · refuge with the Lord of the dawn from darkness, knots and envy (Surah 113) · recite it in the morning · *lord of the daybreak*
