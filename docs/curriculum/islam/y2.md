# YEAR TWO — "the book, surah by surah" (Days 332–696)
*Year one carried Juz' 'Amma. Year two reads the rest of the Qur'an in order, Surah 1 to Surah 77: short surahs whole, long surahs by theme. Format: hear a passage → one idea → sit with it → carry a line. Meanings from Pickthall (1930), paraphrased and cited. Blocks: A · The Opening and the long Medinan surahs · B · Prophets, parables and light · a holy-days interlude · C · The middle of the book · D · Towards the thirtieth part · E · The whole book in view.*
