# YEAR THREE — "the Prophet ﷺ, his companions, and the long story" (Days 697–1061)
*Year one told the seerah in outline. Year three tells it in full, then meets the Companions one by one, then walks through fourteen centuries of Muslim history and civilisation. Where Sunni and Shia Muslims remember events differently, both are told plainly and neither is ranked. Sensitive history (wars, the first split, Karbala, colonial rule, partition) is told honestly and carefully. No depictions: the Prophet ﷺ, his family and the prophets are described only in words.*
