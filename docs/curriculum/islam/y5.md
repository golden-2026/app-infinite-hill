# YEAR FIVE — "the living book" (Days 1427–1791)
*The last range: reading the sources themselves, and living them. Al-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, one a day; the ninety-nine names in full, one a day; primary texts read slowly (the Qur'an, daily remembrances, al-Ghazali, Ibn Ata'illah, Rumi, al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, sermons, travelers, poets, commentators); then a long stretch of living it, ending at the summit on day 1791. Qur'an meanings from Yusuf Ali (1934) and Pickthall (1930); Rumi from Whinfield (1898) and Nicholson; other texts paraphrased with their references.*

# BLOCK A · AL-NAWAWI'S FORTY HADITH (Days 1428–1473)

## Hadith 1–14 (Days 1429–1442)
1429. **Actions are by intentions** · — · actions are only by intentions, and each gets what he intended (Nawawi 1, Umar) · state your intention before each task · *what did i intend?*
1430. **The stranger in white** · — · Jibril, unknown, asks about Islam, iman, ihsan and the Hour (Nawawi 2, Umar) · reread the hadith slowly · *he came to teach you your religion*
1431. **Built on five** · — · Islam is built on five: the testimony, prayer, zakat, Hajj and the fast (Nawawi 3, Ibn Umar) · touch each pillar today · *built on five*
1432. **Written in the womb** · — · each person is formed in stages, and their provision, lifespan, deeds and end are written (Nawawi 4, Ibn Mas'ud) · thank Allah for your life's first days · *written, and still we strive*
1433. **What isn't of it** · bid'ah · whoever introduces into this matter of ours what isn't of it, it is rejected (Nawawi 5, Aisha) · keep one practice as it was taught · *keep it as it came*
1434. **The clear and the doubtful** · — · the halal is clear, the haram is clear, between them are doubtful things, and in the body is a lump of flesh: the heart (Nawawi 6, al-Nu'man ibn Bashir) · avoid one doubtful thing · *guard the heart*
1435. **Religion is sincere counsel** · nasihah · religion is sincere goodwill: to Allah, His book, His messenger, leaders and all people (Nawawi 7, Tamim al-Dari) · give one person honest advice kindly · *sincere goodwill*
1436. **A hard hadith, read in context** · — · a hadith about fighting people until they testify (Nawawi 8, Ibn Umar), which scholars read as about the wars of its time, alongside no compulsion in religion (2:256) · read the scholars' explanation · *read it with the scholars*
1437. **Do what you can** · — · what I forbid, avoid; what I command, do as much as you can (Nawawi 9, Abu Hurayrah) · do what you can today · *as much as you can*
1438. **Allah is good** · — · Allah is good and accepts only the good, and the traveler's du'a is blocked by unlawful food (Nawawi 10, Abu Hurayrah) · check one source of your income · *clean food, open door*
1439. **Leave what makes you doubt** · — · leave what makes you doubt for what doesn't (Nawawi 11, al-Hasan ibn Ali) · drop one doubtful thing · *leave the doubt*
1440. **What doesn't concern you** · — · part of a person's good Islam is leaving what doesn't concern him (Nawawi 12, Abu Hurayrah) · stay out of one matter · *not my concern*
1441. **Love for your brother** · — · none of you believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself (Nawawi 13, Anas) · wish someone your own good · *as for myself*
1442. **Sacred blood, read in context** · — · a hadith naming three narrow cases for a court (Nawawi 14, Ibn Mas'ud), which scholars discuss at length, and whose heart is how sacred a life is · pray for the sanctity of every life · *blood is sacred*
