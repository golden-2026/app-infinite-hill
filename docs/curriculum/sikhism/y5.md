# YEAR FIVE — "the Granth itself, and living it" (Days 1427–1791)
*DRAFT — written for Keeper review. Year five teaches you to read the Guru Granth Sahib yourself, walks through it in order from ang 1 to ang 1430 (one reading a day, not the whole text), then turns to living Sikhi through a whole life, and climbs to the summit. Ang ranges are approximate and for the Keeper to confirm against the standard 1430-ang edition. Translations follow Macauliffe (1909) where used.*

## Block A · Reading the Granth yourself (Days 1427–1446)
1427. **Coming to the Guru** · — · many cover their head and wash their hands before reading the Guru's words, on paper or a screen · wash your hands and cover your head · *come ready*
1428. **The gutka and the steek** · gutka · a gutka is a small prayer book; a steek is the Granth with commentary · look at a gutka or a steek online · *small book, big word*
1429. **The vowel signs** · laga matra · Gurmukhi letters carry small vowel signs above, below and beside · learn three vowel signs · *the small signs*
1430. **The nasal dots** · tippi · small dots and marks add nasal sounds and double letters · find one tippi on a page · *small marks matter*
1431. **Joined or spaced** · larivaar · old copies run all the words together; most printed copies separate them · read one line slowly, word by word · *word by word*
1432. **Reading the heading** · — · each hymn's heading gives the raga, the Guru's mahala, and the ghar · read three headings · *the heading tells you*
1433. **Finding the centre** · — · find the rahao line first; it holds the hymn's meaning · find rahao in a hymn · *start at the centre*
1434. **The numbers at line ends** · — · numbers count the verses and hymns, like a built-in index · count the verses in one hymn · *the numbers guide*
1435. **Kinds of poem** · — · shabad, ashtpadi, chhant, var, pauri, salok: each is a form · name two forms you've read · *many forms*
1436. **Learning with a teacher** · santhiaa · many learn to read the Granth with a teacher who corrects each sound · ask someone to hear you read · *a teacher's ear*
1437. **Using translations** · — · Macauliffe's 1909 English and later translations help, but each is partial · read one line in two translations · *translations help*
1438. **The translation is not the Guru** · — · the Gurmukhi is the Guru; translations are aids · read the Gurmukhi sound first · *sound first*
1439. **A daily hukamnama** · — · many read the daily hukam from the Harmandir or open a gutka each morning · read today's hukam · *today's order*
1440. **What a sehaj path is** · sehaj path · a slow reading of the whole Granth at your own pace · decide your own pace · *your own pace*
1441. **The pace of a sehaj path** · — · a page a day takes about four years; five pages a day, under a year · work out your pace · *page by page*
1442. **The bhog of a sehaj path** · — · a completed reading ends with the last pages, Anand and Ardas · plan how you'd close · *finish with joy*
1443. **The Granth at home** · — · keeping a full Granth needs a dedicated, respectful room; many use a gutka or screen instead · choose a clean place to read · *a clean place*
1444. **Aloud or silent** · — · reading aloud lets the sound work; reading silently helps focus · read one line both ways · *aloud and silent*
1445. **Reading with family** · — · a household can share a reading, one page each · read with someone · *read together*
1446. **Opening at ang 1** · — · begin where the Guru begins · open to ang 1 · *begin at the beginning*
