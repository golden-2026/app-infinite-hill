# YEAR TWO — "the daily banis" (Days 332–696)
*DRAFT — written for Keeper review. Year two reads, in order, the prayers Sikhs say every day, then Sukhmani Sahib, the dawn Asa di Var, the twelve months, Guru Tegh Bahadur's bani and the Ardas. Translations follow Macauliffe (1909), paraphrased; where Macauliffe gives only extracts (parts of the Tenth Guru's writings), the Keeper should check each paraphrase against the Gurmukhi. Stanza and pauri numbers marked in brackets are for the Keeper to confirm.*

## Block A · Jaap Sahib (Days 332–361)
*Guru Gobind Singh's great hymn of praise, 199 short stanzas in quick-changing meters. It opens the Dasam Granth, the collection of writings associated with the Tenth Guru; scholars discuss how that collection was compiled, while the Jaap itself is prayed daily and at every amrit ceremony.*
332. **The Tenth Guru's morning praise** · Jaap Sahib · Jaap Sahib is a rolling litany of the Timeless One's qualities, said each morning after Japji · listen to the first two minutes · *praise at pace*
333. **No mark, no caste** · — · the opening stanza says He has no mark or sign, no color, caste or lineage (Jaap 1) · drop one label you use for yourself · *no mark, no sign*
334. **King of kings** · — · He is called king of kings and lord of countless Indras (Jaap 1) · bow your head for a moment · *above every throne*
335. **Named by deeds** · — · no one can tell all His names; the wise name Him by His works (Jaap 1) · name one thing you've seen Him do · *named by deeds*
336. **The snake's gait** · bhujang prayat · the next stanzas move in a meter named for a snake's gliding motion · read four lines in rhythm · *glide*
337. **Salutation to the Timeless** · akaal · namastvam akaale: salutation to the One beyond time (Jaap, bhujang prayat) · say namastvam akaale three times · *beyond time*
338. **Salutation to the Merciful** · — · namastvam kripaale: salutation to the Merciful One (Jaap, bhujang prayat) · show mercy once today · *salute mercy*
339. **Formless, beyond compare** · — · arupe hain, anupe hain: without form, beyond comparison (Jaap, chachari) · look at something without comparing it · *beyond compare*
340. **Unborn, beyond the elements** · ajooni · ajoo hain, abhoo hain: never born, not made of earth or water (Jaap, chachari) · touch earth and water, then be still · *not made of this*
341. **Salutation to the armed One** · — · namo sastr paane: salutation to the One with weapons in hand, the protector (Jaap) · think of someone who protects you · *the protector*
342. **Sun of suns, moon of moons** · — · the Timeless is praised as the light within every light (Jaap) · look at a light source briefly · *light of lights*
343. **Song of songs, dance of dances** · — · salutation to the song within songs, the sound within sounds, the dance within dances (Jaap) · move to one song · *the dance of dances*
344. **Lamp of beauty** · — · the Jaap moves into Persian: He is the glorious one, the lamp of beauty (Jaap) · notice one beautiful thing · *lamp of beauty*
345. **The provider** · — · in Persian again: He is the one who gives daily bread (Jaap) · thank the hands behind your food · *daily bread*
346. **Gobind, Mukand** · — · gobinde mukande udaare apaare: the Sustainer, the Liberator, generous, infinite (Jaap) · be generous once · *generous and infinite*
347. **One form, many sights** · — · ek moorat anek darsan: one form seen in countless ways (Jaap, roaaval) · look at a friend with fresh eyes · *one, seen many ways*
348. **Ender and sustainer of all** · — · sarab kaal hain, sarab paal hain: He ends all and He sustains all (Jaap) · notice one ending and one beginning · *ends and keeps*
349. **House of compassion** · karunaa · karunaalay hain: He is the home of compassion (Jaap, harbolmana) · be a home of compassion once · *home of compassion*
350. **Breaker of the cruel** · — · khal khanddan hain, mahi manddan hain: He breaks the cruel and adorns the earth (Jaap, harbolmana) · refuse one small cruelty · *break cruelty, adorn earth*
351. **The one-letter meter** · — · ajai alai abhai abai: unconquerable, indestructible, fearless, unchanging (Jaap, ek achhari) · say the four words slowly · *ajai, alai, abhai, abai*
352. **Fearless, changeless** · abhai · the Jaap keeps returning to fearlessness, the gift the Khalsa asks for · name one fear and breathe · *fearless like him*
353. **Present in every land** · — · the Jaap salutes the One who dwells in every country and every garb · think of a faraway place kindly · *in every land*
354. **Beyond the fear of death** · — · praising the One who ends all things loosens the fear of our own ending · sit one minute with calm · *death is in his hand*
355. **Keeping the pace** · — · the meters quicken like drums; many recite Jaap briskly, as marching music for the spirit · recite ten lines briskly · *march in praise*
356. **The last stanza** · — · sadaa sachidaanand: ever truth, consciousness and bliss, breaker of enemies, dwelling in all (Jaap 199) · say the last line aloud · *ever true, ever bliss*
357. **Jaap and the amrit bowl** · — · Jaap is one of the five banis recited over the iron bowl as amrit is prepared · stir a glass of water, reciting · *prayer stirred in*
358. **Jaap for courage** · — · many Sikhs turn to Jaap when they need strength · listen before something hard · *borrow the courage*
359. **Why so many names** · — · a thousand names say that no single name can hold Him · write three names for the Divine · *more names than stars*
360. **The whole Jaap** · — · all 199 stanzas take about fifteen to twenty minutes · listen to it whole · *all the names*
361. **Jaap close** · — · the Tenth Guru's praise now sits beside Nanak's Japji in your morning · add Jaap to your mornings · *japji, then jaap*

## Block B · Tav Prasad Savaiye (Days 362–369)
*Ten savaiyas from the Akal Ustat, said each morning: outward power and ritual count for nothing without love.*
362. **By your grace** · tav prasad · the heading means by Your grace; ten verses follow · listen to all ten · *by your grace*
363. **The ascetics I have seen** · — · the poet has seen hosts of monks, siddhas and yogis, yet without love for the Lord it is all worth nothing (Savaiye 1) · do one thing with love, not show · *nothing without love*
364. **Elephants in gold** · — · drunken elephants decked in gold, horses swift as deer, kings in glory, and all leave this world empty-handed (Savaiye 2) · look at one thing you own and smile · *they left empty-handed*
365. **The conquerors** · — · emperors who conquered every land, with drums beating, also went without the Name (Savaiye) · let go of one small ambition · *drums fade*
366. **Eyes shut like a crane** · — · what is gained by closing both eyes and sitting like a crane, pretending (Savaiye) · pray once with no one watching · *not for show*
367. **Every outward ritual** · — · the Tenth Guru lists outward practices of many kinds and says all miss the secret without love (Savaiye) · check one habit for its heart · *the secret is love*
368. **Those who loved found Him** · — · I tell the truth, listen all: those who loved found the Lord (Savaiye) · tell someone you love them · *those who loved, found*
369. **Savaiye close** · — · the ten verses sharpen one point: love, not display · read your favorite one again · *love, not display*

## Block C · Chaupai Sahib (Days 370–379)
*The Benti Chaupai, a prayer for protection from the Dasam Granth, said at dusk in Rehras and over the amrit bowl.*
370. **A prayer for protection** · Benti Chaupai · the Tenth Guru's quiet prayer asking to be sheltered · read the first four lines · *shelter me*
371. **Protect me with your hand** · — · hamree karo hath dai rachhaa: give me Your hand and keep me safe (Chaupai) · rest your hand on your heart · *keep me by the hand*
372. **The heart's wish** · — · pooran hoe chit kee ichhaa: may my heart's longing be fulfilled, my mind rest at Your feet (Chaupai) · name your deepest wish · *the heart's wish*
373. **The enemies within and without** · — · the prayer asks for enemies to be ended; many read them also as the five thieves inside · name one inner enemy · *end the enemy within*
374. **Keep my Sikhs** · — · sevak sikh hamaare taareeh: carry my servants and Sikhs across (Chaupai) · pray for a friend by name · *carry them across*
375. **The one who spreads and gathers** · — · the Creator spreads creation out and draws it back into Himself (Chaupai) · breathe out, then in · *spread and gathered*
376. **Knower of every heart** · — · ghat ghat ke antar kee jaanat: He knows every heart and recognizes the pain of good and bad alike (Chaupai) · be known, and rest · *he knows the pain*
377. **Since I held your feet** · — · since I grasped Your feet I look to no one else (Chaupai, savaiya) · choose one thing to trust · *held at the feet*
378. **Every other door** · — · leaving every other door, I have come to Yours; keep my honor (Chaupai, dohra) · stand still at a doorway · *your door*
379. **Chaupai close** · — · a short prayer for the times you feel unsafe · keep it for night-time · *keep me*

## Block D · Anand Sahib (Days 380–404)
*Guru Amar Das's song of bliss in Raga Ramkali, forty pauris (ang 917–922). The first five and the last are sung at every Sikh ceremony.*
380. **Anand Sahib: the song of bliss** · Anand · forty pauris of joy found through the Guru, closing every Sikh gathering · listen to the six ceremony pauris · *bliss, together*
381. **Pauri 1, sound: anand bhaiaa** · — · anand bhaiaa meree maae satguroo mai paaiaa · say the first line aloud · *anand bhaiaa*
382. **Pauri 1, meaning: joy, mother** · — · joy, mother, I have found the True Guru, easily, and the songs of joy are sung (Anand 1) · share one good news · *joy, mother*
383. **Stay with the Lord, my mind** · — · ae man meriaa too sadaa rahu har naale: stay always with the Lord and your troubles will pass (Anand 2) · stay with one thing for five minutes · *stay with him*
384. **What your house doesn't hold** · — · true Master, what is there that is not in Your house (Anand, opening pauris) · list what you already have · *nothing is missing*
385. **The Name my support** · — · the true Name is my support and satisfies every hunger (Anand, opening pauris) · eat slowly, satisfied · *my support*
386. **Everyone talks of bliss** · — · everyone speaks of bliss, but bliss is known through the Guru (Anand 6) · notice what real joy felt like · *real joy*
387. **The one you bless** · — · only the one You bless receives this gift (Anand, early pauris) · thank grace for one thing · *given, not grabbed*
388. **Cleverness never found it** · — · the restless, clever mind has never found Him (Anand) · drop one clever plan · *not by cleverness*
389. **The Word clears doubt** · — · the Guru's Word cuts through doubt like light through dark (Anand) · write down one doubt, then rest · *the word clears*
390. **Sharper than a sword** · — · the devotees' way is unique, sharper than a sword and finer than a hair (Anand 14) · walk one narrow line carefully · *the narrow way*
391. **Treasure in the Name** · — · the Name is a treasure found within, not in markets (Anand) · look inward for one minute · *treasure within*
392. **The unripe word** · — · without the True Guru, other words are unripe (Anand 24) · speak only ripe words today · *ripe words*
393. **Listening for the ripe** · — · the Anand invites us to hear what is true beneath the noise · listen for one true sentence · *hear the true*
394. **O my body** · — · the Guru speaks to the body: what have you done since you came into the world (Anand, the body pauris) · thank your body for one thing · *o my body*
395. **The light placed in you** · — · the body came into the world when the Lord placed His light within it (Anand, the body pauris) · feel your pulse · *light in the body*
396. **O my eyes** · — · the eyes are told the Lord put His light in them, so see Him in all (Anand, the eyes) · see one person as light · *eyes, see him*
397. **O my ears** · — · the ears were sent to hear the truth (Anand, the ears) · listen to one true thing · *ears, hear truth*
398. **The cave and the breath** · — · the Lord set the soul in the cave of the body and played the breath like music (Anand) · breathe and hear it · *breath is music*
399. **Grace gives the Word** · — · the Word comes by grace, and with it the bliss (Anand) · receive one gift without earning it · *grace gives*
400. **Pauri 40, sound: anad sunahu** · — · anad sunahu vaddabhaageeho sagal manorath poore · say the first line aloud · *anad sunahu*
401. **Pauri 40, meaning: hear the bliss** · — · hear the song of bliss, fortunate ones; all longings are fulfilled (Anand 40) · listen to the last pauri whole · *every longing met*
402. **At every ceremony** · — · births, weddings, deaths and every service end with the six pauris and Ardas · notice how you end things · *end in bliss*
403. **Anand at dusk** · — · the six pauris also sit inside the evening Rehras · hum the first pauri at dusk · *dusk, with joy*
404. **Anand close** · — · forty pauris of joy that is steady, not giddy · name your steadiest joy · *steady joy*

## Block E · Rehras Sahib (Days 405–420)
*The evening prayer at day's end. Its exact contents differ slightly between traditions; this follows the common core printed after Japji in the Granth (ang 8–12).*
405. **Provisions for the road** · Rehras · Rehras is said as work ends, like provisions for the night's journey · pause at dusk for one minute · *provisions for the road*
406. **So dar: the door, again** · — · so dar: what is that door where You sit and care for all, sung at dusk (Asa, ang 8–9) · look at a doorway at dusk · *the door at dusk*
407. **Great by hearsay** · — · suni vaddaa aakhai sabh koe: everyone calls You great from hearing, but only one who has seen knows (Asa, ang 9) · say what you have seen, not heard · *seen, not heard*
408. **Remembering, I live** · — · aakhaa jeevaa visrai mar jaao: chanting I live, forgetting I die (Asa, ang 9) · remember once at dusk · *remember and live*
409. **So Purakh: that Being** · — · so purakh niranjan: that Being is pure, beyond reach, and all meditate on Him (Asa M4, ang 10–11) · say so purakh niranjan · *pure being*
410. **You are the true Creator** · — · too kartaa sachiaar maiddaa saanee: You are the true Creator, my Master (Asa M4, ang 11) · thank the maker of your day · *my master*
411. **A pool of fire and water** · — · in the world's pool we dwell, where water and fire both are, feet stuck in the mud of attachment (Asa M1, ang 12) · lift your feet and wiggle your toes · *out of the mud*
412. **This human body is your chance** · — · bhaee paraapat maanukh dehureeaa: you have this human body; now is your chance (Asa M5, ang 12) · do today's good now · *now is the chance*
413. **The humble plea** · — · the Lord's humble servants ask the True Guru for the Name (Gujri M4, ang 10) · ask for help once · *the humble ask*
414. **Why worry, mind** · — · kaahe re man chitveh udam: why plot and scheme, mind, when even creatures in the rocks are fed (Gujri M5, ang 10) · put one worry down · *even the rock-creatures eat*
415. **Chaupai at nightfall** · — · the Benti Chaupai, now prayed as darkness comes · read two lines at dusk · *shelter for the night*
416. **Anand at nightfall** · — · the six ceremony pauris close the day in joy · hum anand bhaiaa · *joy at dusk*
417. **Ardas at day's end** · — · many families close Rehras standing together in Ardas · stand for one minute's prayer · *stand at day's end*
418. **Dusk as a practice** · sandhiaa · lamps lit, work put down, the family gathered · light one lamp at dusk · *put the day down*
419. **Rehras with others** · — · a household that prays Rehras together hears the day end the same way · invite one person to join · *end the day together*
420. **Rehras close** · — · morning Japji, evening Rehras: the day is held at both ends · pray at both ends of today · *both ends held*

## Block F · Kirtan Sohila (Days 421–435)
*Five short hymns said before sleep, and at funerals (ang 12–13).*
421. **The night song** · Sohila · Sohila means song of praise; its five hymns close the day and close a life · read it before bed · *the night song*
422. **Hymn 1, sound: jai ghar keerat** · — · jai ghar keerat aakheeai kartey kaa hoe beechaaro (Gauri Deepaki M1, ang 12) · say the first line aloud · *jai ghar keerat*
423. **Oil at the threshold** · — · in the house where praise is sung, friends pour oil at the door for the bride, whose day has come (Gauri Deepaki M1, ang 12) · bless someone's doorway · *the bride is called*
424. **Hymn 2, sound: chhia ghar** · — · chhia ghar chhia gur chhia updes (Asa M1, ang 12) · say the first line aloud · *chhia ghar*
425. **Six schools, one Guru** · — · six schools, six teachers, six teachings, but one Guru of gurus in many forms (Asa M1, ang 12) · respect one path unlike yours · *one guru, many forms*
426. **Many seasons, one sun** · — · seconds, hours, days and seasons are many, but the sun is one (Asa M1, ang 12) · look at the sun's light today · *one sun*
427. **Hymn 3, sound: gagan mai thaal** · — · gagan mai thaal rav chand deepak bane (Dhanasri M1, ang 13) · say the first line aloud · *gagan mai thaal*
428. **The sky is the platter** · aarti · the sky is the platter, sun and moon the lamps, the stars the pearls: creation performs the aarti (Dhanasri M1, ang 13) · look at the night sky · *the sky is the platter*
429. **The wind as incense** · — · tradition sets this hymn at Puri, where Nanak saw a temple aarti and pointed to the one the universe performs · smell the evening air · *the whole world offering*
430. **Hymn 4, sound: kaam karodh** · — · kaam karodh nagar bahu bhariaa (Gauri Purbi M4, ang 13) · say the first line aloud · *kaam karodh*
431. **A city full of thieves** · — · the body is a city crowded with lust and anger; meeting the holy, they are broken (Gauri Purbi M4, ang 13) · name one thief to evict · *clear the city*
432. **Hymn 5, sound: karau benantee** · — · karau benantee sunahu mere meetaa (Gauri Purbi M5, ang 13) · say the first line aloud · *karau benantee*
433. **Now is the time** · — · listen, friends: now is the time to serve the holy and earn the Name (Gauri Purbi M5, ang 13) · do one kind act before bed · *now is the time*
434. **A wedding song at a funeral** · — · Sohila is also sung at cremations, a song of the soul going home · think kindly of someone who died · *going home*
435. **Sohila close** · — · five hymns to sleep on: praise, one sun, the sky's aarti, the city, now · read Sohila tonight · *sleep in praise*

## Block G · Sukhmani Sahib (Days 436–535)
*Guru Arjan's Psalm of Peace in Raga Gauri (ang 262–296): twenty-four ashtpadis, each opening with a salok and eight stanzas. Four days each: salok, first half, second half, living it.*
436. **The jewel of peace** · Sukhmani · Sukhmani means the jewel of peace; many read it daily or in groups when life is hard · listen to the opening · *the jewel of peace*
437. **How people read it** · — · it takes around ninety minutes; many read one ashtpadi a day · plan an ashtpadi a week · *one ashtpadi at a time*

### Ashtpadis 1–6
438. **Ashtpadi 1, salok: I bow to the Guru** · — · aad gure namah: I bow to the primal Guru, the Guru of the ages, the True Guru (Sukhmani 1) · bow your head once · *i bow*
439. **Remember, and find peace** · simran · simro simar simar sukh paavo: remember, remember, and find peace (Sukhmani 1) · repeat the Name ten times · *remember, find peace*
440. **What remembering does** · — · in remembering God there is no pain, and death does not frighten (Sukhmani 1) · remember during one hard moment · *no fear in remembering*
441. **Living ashtpadi 1** · — · Guru Arjan begins with the simplest practice: remember · set one reminder today · *begin with remembering*
442. **Ashtpadi 2, salok: destroyer of pain** · — · deen darad dukh bhanjanaa: He destroys the pain of the humble (Sukhmani 2) · pray for someone in pain · *he breaks pain*
443. **Where no family can go** · — · where no mother, father, child or friend can go, the Name goes with you (Sukhmani 2) · sit with who goes with you · *the name goes too*
444. **The Name on the hard road** · — · the Name helps on the long road where all else fails (Sukhmani 2) · carry one line into a hard task · *company on the road*
445. **Living ashtpadi 2** · — · the Name as a companion, not a charm · remember once when alone · *never alone*
446. **Ashtpadi 3, salok: many scriptures** · — · I have searched many scriptures; none equals the Name (Sukhmani 3) · read one line slowly · *nothing equals the name*
447. **Beyond every pilgrimage** · — · pilgrimage, austerity and charity do not equal the Name (Sukhmani 3) · do something simple and sincere · *simple and sincere*
448. **The best of all ways** · — · the highest dharma is to repeat the Name and do pure deeds (Sukhmani 3) · do one pure deed · *name and pure deeds*
449. **Living ashtpadi 3** · — · the Name first, and deeds to match it · match one word to a deed · *say it, do it*
450. **Ashtpadi 4, salok: O virtueless one** · — · O ignorant and virtueless one, always remember the Lord who made you (Sukhmani 4) · remember your maker once · *remember who made you*
451. **The ungrateful guest** · — · He gave you life, food and body, and you forget Him (Sukhmani 4) · thank the giver of three things · *remember the giver*
452. **You are the Master** · — · too thaakur tum peh ardaas: You are the Master, to You this prayer; You are mother and father, we Your children (Sukhmani 4) · say this before sleep · *mother and father*
453. **Living ashtpadi 4** · — · many say this stanza just before Ardas · stand and say it once · *to you, this prayer*
454. **Ashtpadi 5, salok: giving and taking** · — · the salok warns against clinging to gifts and forgetting the Giver (Sukhmani 5) · hold something loosely · *hold gifts loosely*
455. **Ten things and one** · — · given ten things, one is held back, and he loses faith (Sukhmani 5) · count ten things you've been given · *ten given, one held back*
456. **If He took the ten** · — · what could the ungrateful say if the ten were taken too (Sukhmani 5) · be grateful for one small thing · *thank for the ten*
457. **Living ashtpadi 5** · — · gratitude is the practice here · list ten gifts · *count the ten*
458. **Ashtpadi 6, salok: the thieves leave** · — · lust, anger, greed and attachment go, and pride too, by grace (Sukhmani 6) · breathe out one thief · *grace clears the house*
459. **By whose grace** · — · jih prasaad: by whose grace you eat fine foods and wear fine clothes, remember Him (Sukhmani 6) · thank grace before eating · *by his grace, this meal*
460. **By whose grace, the body** · — · by His grace you have a healthy body and a home (Sukhmani 6) · thank your body and home · *by his grace, this home*
461. **Living ashtpadi 6** · — · every comfort points back to a Giver · trace one comfort to its source · *every comfort a gift*

### Ashtpadis 7–12
462. **Ashtpadi 7, salok: the holy company** · — · the salok praises the company of the holy (Sukhmani 7) · think of your best company · *good company*
463. **The face grows bright** · saadh sangat · in the company of the holy the face grows bright and filth is washed (Sukhmani 7) · spend time with someone good · *company shapes you*
464. **Ego leaves in good company** · — · in the holy company ego departs and wisdom comes (Sukhmani 7) · listen more than you speak · *ego leaves quietly*
465. **Living ashtpadi 7** · — · choose your company as you'd choose food · choose one good company this week · *choose your company*
466. **Ashtpadi 8, salok: the one who knows God** · brahm giaanee · the salok introduces the God-conscious one (Sukhmani 8) · think of someone wise and gentle · *the god-conscious one*
467. **Like a lotus in water** · — · the God-conscious one lives untouched, like a lotus in water (Sukhmani 8) · stay calm in one mess · *untouched, like the lotus*
468. **Like the earth** · — · the God-conscious one is like the earth, dug by some, anointed by others, the same to both (Sukhmani 8) · treat two people the same · *like the earth*
469. **Living ashtpadi 8** · — · the brahm giaanee is a portrait to grow into, not a title to claim · practice one quality from the portrait · *grow into it*
470. **Ashtpadi 9, salok: the true names** · — · the salok turns to what the true devotee is like (Sukhmani 9) · think what makes a person true · *true, not titled*
471. **The true scholar** · — · the true pandit is one who instructs his own mind (Sukhmani 9) · teach yourself one thing · *teach your own mind*
472. **The true devotee** · — · titles mean nothing without the life that matches (Sukhmani 9) · live one value quietly · *the life, not the title*
473. **Living ashtpadi 9** · — · drop one title and keep the substance · do good without a label · *substance over title*
474. **Ashtpadi 10, salok: countless** · — · the salok marvels at countless beings (Sukhmani 10) · look at a crowd kindly · *so many*
475. **Many millions** · — · kaee kott: many millions worship, many millions wander, many millions are learned (Sukhmani 10) · wish well to many millions · *millions, all his*
476. **All in His creation** · — · the good and the lost are all within His play (Sukhmani 10) · judge no one today · *all in the play*
477. **Living ashtpadi 10** · — · seeing the countless makes our worries smaller · look at the stars · *smaller worries*
478. **Ashtpadi 11, salok: the doer of all** · — · karan kaaran prabh ek hai: He alone is the doer and the cause (Sukhmani 11) · let go of one outcome · *he is the doer*
479. **His play** · — · all happens within His will and His play (Sukhmani 11) · accept one thing you can't change · *his will*
480. **Nothing outside** · — · nothing happens outside His order (Sukhmani 11) · breathe through one frustration · *nothing outside*
481. **Living ashtpadi 11** · — · trust and effort together: act, and leave the rest · act, then release · *act, then release*
482. **Ashtpadi 12, salok: the humble** · nimrataa · the humble abide in peace (Sukhmani 12) · take the lower seat once · *the humble rest easy*
483. **The proud fall** · — · the one who is proud of power will fall (Sukhmani 12) · catch one proud thought · *pride falls*
484. **The lowest of all** · — · whoever knows himself lowest of all is counted highest (Sukhmani 12) · serve someone quietly · *lowest is highest*
485. **Living ashtpadi 12** · — · humility is the doorway to every virtue in Sukhmani · say sorry once · *humility first*
