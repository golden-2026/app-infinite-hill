*DRAFT — written for Keeper review. ## Block B · Guru Nanak's longer teachings (Days 1132–1176)

### Sidh Gosht: the dialogue with the yogis (Ramkali, ang 938–946)
1132. **A conversation on the mountain** · Sidh Gosht · Guru Nanak's seventy-three stanzas of dialogue with the Siddh yogis · listen to the opening · *a conversation*
1133. **Who are you** · — · the yogis ask Nanak who he is, where he comes from and where he goes (Sidh Gosht) · answer who you are in one line · *who are you*
1134. **Living in the world** · — · Nanak answers that one can live in the world untouched, like a lotus or a duck in water (Sidh Gosht) · stay calm in one busy moment · *the duck stays dry*
1135. **Why leave your home** · — · the yogis ask why he left the world; he says to seek the Gurmukhs and share the Name (Sidh Gosht) · share one good thing · *seeking the good*
1136. **The yogis' earrings** · — · he says the true earrings are the Guru's Word and the true patched coat is humility (Sidh Gosht) · wear an inner ornament · *the word as earring*
1137. **Where did it begin** · — · asked where creation began, Nanak speaks of wonder and of the void before all things (Sidh Gosht) · sit in wonder · *it began in wonder*
1138. **Where does the mind live** · — · the yogis ask where the mind dwells; he says the Word steadies it (Sidh Gosht) · steady your mind with one line · *the word steadies*
1139. **The Word is the Guru** · — · sabad guroo surat dhun chelaa: the Word is the Guru, and attention tuned to it is the disciple (Sidh Gosht 44, ang 943) · tune your attention for two minutes · *the word is guru*
1140. **Crossing the ocean** · — · the world is an ocean; the Word is the boat (Sidh Gosht) · look at water and breathe · *the word is the boat*
1141. **Without the Guru** · — · without the Guru, Nanak says, no one finds the way (Sidh Gosht) · thank a guide · *a guide is needed*
1142. **The breath and the Name** · — · the yogis speak of breath practice; Nanak points to the Name within the breath (Sidh Gosht) · simran on ten breaths · *the name in the breath*
1143. **The true yogi** · — · a true yogi is one who lives the Guru's teaching in ordinary life (Sidh Gosht) · do one ordinary thing well · *yoga in daily life*
1144. **Humble before truth** · — · the dialogue ends with the yogis honoring the Word (Sidh Gosht) · honor someone who taught you · *the yogis bowed*
1145. **Dialogue, not debate** · — · Nanak listens and answers; he does not mock · answer one question kindly · *answer kindly*
1146. **Sidh Gosht close** · — · a conversation with other seekers, still worth having · have one deep conversation · *keep talking*
