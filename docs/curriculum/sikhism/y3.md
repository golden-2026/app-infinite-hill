# YEAR THREE — "the long story" (Days 697–1061)
*DRAFT — written for Keeper review. The big narratives: the first tellers, the janamsakhis, the Bhagats of the Granth, the people around the Gurus, the Khalsa's hard century, the Misls, Maharaja Ranjit Singh, loss and exile, reform, and the twentieth century. Gurbani paraphrased from Macauliffe (1909). History follows widely documented accounts; "tradition says" marks sakhi and legend. Sensitive history is told plainly and without blaming any people as a whole.*
