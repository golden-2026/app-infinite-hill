# golden — HINDUISM · THE RANGES · YEARS 2–5 (~1,560 sessions)
*With year one (331 sessions), the full path: ~1,890 sessions over five years. Year 2 is mapped session by session; years 3–5 week by week (7 sessions a week, each week's sessions named by content). Full scripts follow the Camp 1 format once the Keeper approves the sequence.*

**Rules that hold throughout:** public-domain translations (Müller / Hume for Upanishads, Griffith / Dutt for Valmiki, Ganguli for Vyasa, Woods for Patanjali, Arnold / Besant for the Gita, Sanderson / Burnouf for the Bhagavata); one word per session at most; every session keeps the five-part shape — bell, review, hook, teach/read, practice, carry; the calendar interrupts for festivals; the lineage track chosen in year one shapes the electives.

## Block C · The Ramayana as a serial — Valmiki, kanda by kanda (Days 490–669 · 180 sessions · ~26 weeks)
*Year one told the story in 35 episodes. Year two reads it: 180 sessions, each a canto or cluster, the Ambassador reading the verse. Kanda by kanda, Griffith / Dutt.*
- **Bala Kanda** (490–513, 24 sessions): Valmiki and Narada; the crow and the first verse (the origin of poetry); Dasharatha's sacrifice; Vishvamitra takes the boys; Tataka; the weapons; Ahalya restored; Janaka's bow; the four weddings; Parashurama's challenge
- **Ayodhya Kanda** (514–549, 36 sessions): the coronation planned; Manthara; Kaikeyi's boons; Rama's calm; Sita's argument; Lakshmana's rage and restraint; the departure; Guha the boatman; Bharadvaja; Chitrakoot; Dasharatha's death and the curse that caused it (Shravana Kumara); Bharata's journey; the sandals
- **Aranya Kanda** (550–579, 30 sessions): the sages; Viradha; Sharabhanga; Agastya's weapons; Panchavati; Shurpanakha; Khara and Dushana; Ravana and Maricha's warning; the golden deer; the line; the abduction; Jatayu; Kabandha; Shabari
- **Kishkindha Kanda** (580–603, 24 sessions): Hanuman's first words; the pact with Sugriva; Vali's death and Tara's lament; the rains; Lakshmana's warning; the search parties; the cave of Svayamprabha; Sampati; the leap decided
- **Sundara Kanda** (604–633, 30 sessions) — *the most-recited book; read whole*: the leap; Mainaka; Surasa; Simhika; Lanka by night; Ravana's palace; the ashoka grove; Sita found; the ring; her doubt; Hanuman's counsel; the fight; the burning; the return; "seen, Sita"
- **Yuddha Kanda** (634–663, 30 sessions): the bridge; Vibhishana's crossing; the siege; Indrajit's arrows; the serpent-arrows and Garuda; Kumbhakarna; Lakshmana wounded; the mountain of herbs; Indrajit's death; Ravana's final battle; Mandodari; the fire; the pushpaka's flight home; Diwali
- **Uttara Kanda** (664–669, 6 sessions, with the Keeper's framing): the later book — Sita's second exile, Lava and Kusha, Sita's return to the earth; presented as the tradition's own unresolved argument
