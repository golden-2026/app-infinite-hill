# YEAR TWO — "the book, in order" (Days 332–696)
*The core texts read in order: the beginnings in Genesis and Exodus, the prayer book of the Psalms and the wisdom books, the prophets, then Luke and John straight through, and three short letters. A passage a day; the practice stays small.*
