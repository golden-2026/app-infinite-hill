# YEAR FIVE — "the sources, and living it" (Days 1427–1791)
*Reading the primary texts for yourself: Matthew, Acts and the letters straight through, then the saints and teachers in their own words. Then the last stretch: living it, daily, weekly and yearly, and the summit.*
