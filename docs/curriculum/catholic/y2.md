# YEAR TWO — "the book, in order" (Days 332–696)
*The Catholic Bible read in order: Genesis, the exodus, kings, prophets, wisdom and the seven books Protestants don't include, then Luke, John, Acts, the letters and the Apocalypse, and finally the year's great feasts with their readings. Book names are modern; the Douay's older names are noted where they differ (the Douay's 1–4 Kings are 1–2 Samuel and 1–2 Kings; Ecclesiasticus is Sirach).*
