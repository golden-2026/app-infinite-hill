# CAMP 2 · THE STORIES (Days 22–96)
*The gospel told as a serial, then the people who loved it: Mary and the saints. One word every two or three days. Practices stay about two minutes.*

## Week 11 · The fire spreads (Days 71–77)
71. **Breakfast on the beach (John 21:1–19)** · — · a charcoal fire, fish, and three times, do you love me · undo one failure with one kindness · *thou knowest that i love thee*
72. **The Ascension (Acts 1:6–11)** · Ascension · he goes up and two men ask why they're staring at the sky · look up, then get to work · *why stand you looking up*
73. **Pentecost (Acts 2:1–13)** · Pentecost · wind, tongues of fire, and everyone hears in their own language · pray come, Holy Spirit · *the birthday of the church*
74. **The first community (Acts 2:42–47)** · — · teaching, breaking bread, prayers, and all things in common · share one thing you own · *they held all things in common*
75. **Stephen (Acts 6–7)** · martyr · the first to die for the faith, forgiving those who stoned him · forgive one old hurt · *lord, lay not this sin*
76. **The Damascus road (Acts 9:1–19)** · — · Saul the persecutor is knocked down, blinded, and becomes Paul · notice your own blind spot · *the scales fall*
77. **Peter and Cornelius (Acts 10)** · — · a vision of a sheet of animals teaches Peter that God shows no partiality · welcome someone different from you · *no one is unclean to god*

## Week 12 · Mary (Days 78–84)
78. **Anne and Joachim** · — · tradition names Mary's parents; their feast is kept on 26 July · thank someone who raised you · *someone raised her too*
79. **Conceived without sin** · Immaculate Conception · Catholics teach that Mary was kept free of original sin from her first moment (defined 1854, CCC 491) · note it's about Mary's conception, not Jesus's · *prepared from the start*
80. **Mother of God (Council of Ephesus, 431)** · Theotokos · the crowds in Ephesus cheered when the council called Mary God-bearer, a claim about who Jesus is · pray one Hail Mary slowly · *a claim about her son*
81. **Seven sorrows** · — · the prophecy, the flight, the loss, the road, the cross, the pietà, the tomb · hold one sorrow gently · *a sword shall pierce thy soul*
82. **Taken up (Assumption, 15 August)** · Assumption · defined in 1950, Catholics hold Mary was taken body and soul into heavenly glory (CCC 966) · treat your body with care today · *the body matters*
83. **Guadalupe, 1531** · — · Mary appears to Juan Diego near Mexico City and roses fill his cloak in December · pray for the people of the Americas · *she spoke his language*
84. **Why Catholics ask Mary** · intercession · asking Mary to pray for us, as we ask friends to, not worshiping her (CCC 971) · ask one friend to pray for you · *pray for us*

## Week 13 · The saints, one by one (Days 85–91)
85. **Augustine (354–430)** · — · a brilliant, restless young man hears a child singing take and read and opens the Bible · read one verse at random · *our heart is restless*
86. **Patrick (c. 5th century)** · — · kidnapped from Britain as a boy, enslaved in Ireland, he returns freely to the people who held him · pray for someone who hurt you · *he went back*
87. **Benedict and Scholastica (6th century)** · — · twin brother and sister; she prays for a storm so he'll stay and talk · keep an unhurried conversation · *pray and work*
88. **Clare of Assisi (1194–1253)** · — · she follows Francis into poverty and leads her sisters for forty years · simplify one thing · *poor and glad*
89. **Catherine of Siena (1347–1380)** · — · a dyer's daughter who cared for plague victims and wrote hard letters to popes · say one true thing kindly · *be who god meant*
90. **Joan of Arc (1412–1431)** · — · a farm girl who led armies, was burned at nineteen, and was declared innocent 25 years later · stand by what you know is right · *i am not afraid*
91. **Thomas More (1478–1535)** · — · Henry VIII's chancellor, beheaded for conscience, joking on the scaffold · keep one quiet promise · *the king's good servant, god's first*

## Week 14 · Modern saints and the close (Days 92–96)
92. **Bernadette at Lourdes (1858)** · — · a sick, poor, fourteen-year-old sees a lady in a grotto and digs where a spring appears · pray for someone who is ill · *she was only asked to tell*
93. **Thérèse of Lisieux (1873–1897)** · — · she died at 24 in an obscure convent and became one of the most loved saints · do one small thing with great love · *the little way*
94. **Maximilian Kolbe (1894–1941)** · — · in Auschwitz a Franciscan priest offers to die in place of a father of a family · remember those who died in the camps · *he stepped forward*
95. **Teresa of Kolkata (1910–1997)** · — · a teacher leaves her convent to care for the dying poor, and writes later of long spiritual darkness · see the person in front of you · *small things with great love*
96. **Camp 2 close: the cloud of witnesses (Hebrews 12:1)** · communion of saints · the saints aren't statues but a crowd cheering you on · pick one saint to walk with this year · *so great a cloud of witnesses*

# CAMP 3 · THE PRACTICES (Days 97–156)
*How-to. The hook is short, the practice is the session. By the end: a small daily rule of life.*

## Part 1 · The body prays (Days 97–103)
97. **The sign of the cross, slowly** · Trinity · Father on the forehead, Son on the heart, Holy Spirit across the shoulders · make the sign once, taking five seconds · *one god, three persons, one gesture*
98. **Holy water at the door** · — · touching water at the church door recalls your baptism · bless yourself with water at a tap · *every door a baptism*
99. **Knees and bows** · genuflect · the knee bends toward the tabernacle; the head bows at the name of Jesus · bow your head once, meaning it · *the body says it first*
100. **The Glory Be** · doxology · the shortest Catholic prayer, praise to the three persons · pray it at the end of a task · *as it was in the beginning*
101. **The Apostles' Creed, line by line** · creed · twelve lines, from creator to life everlasting · read the creed and pause after each line · *i believe is a verb*
102. **The morning offering** · — · offering the whole day, work and joys and trouble, before it starts · offer today before you check your phone · *today is already given*
103. **The Angelus** · Angelus · the noon bell, three verses, three Hail Marys, remembering the angel came · stop at noon and pray one Hail Mary · *the word was made flesh*

## Part 2 · The Mass, scene by scene (Days 104–117)
*Camp 1 met three scenes. Now the whole Mass, in order.*
104. **The entrance** · — · the procession, the sign of the cross, and the priest kissing the altar · arrive somewhere early and still · *we gather first*
105. **Through my fault** · Confiteor · the whole church admits it has sinned and asks each other for prayer · strike your chest gently once · *we all begin as sinners*
106. **Lord, have mercy** · Kyrie eleison · three short Greek cries left inside the Latin Mass · pray Kyrie eleison three times · *mercy, three times*
107. **The Gloria (Luke 2:14)** · Gloria · the angels' song from Bethlehem, silent in Advent and Lent · hum or say one line of praise · *glory to god in the highest*
108. **The readings and the psalm** · lectionary · Sundays run a three-year cycle so most of the Bible is read aloud · look up this Sunday's readings · *the whole book, in turns*
109. **The gospel** · — · everyone stands, three small crosses on forehead, lips and heart · trace those three crosses before reading · *mind, lips, heart*
110. **The homily** · homily · the priest or deacon opens the readings for this crowd on this day · write one sentence you'd keep from a sermon · *the word, for today*
111. **The Creed and the prayers of the faithful** · Nicene Creed · the whole church says what it believes, then prays for the world · pray for one need in the news · *we believe, then we ask*
112. **The gifts come up** · offertory · bread and wine are carried forward; a drop of water mingles with the wine · offer one small thing of your day · *the ordinary is carried up*
113. **Holy, holy, holy (Isaiah 6:3)** · Sanctus · the preface ends and heaven and earth sing the same song · say holy three times, quietly · *heaven joins in*
114. **The consecration** · transubstantiation · the words of Jesus are spoken and Catholics believe bread and wine become his body and blood (CCC 1376) · kneel or sit still for one minute · *this is my body*
115. **The Great Amen and the Our Father** · — · the whole church says one loud Amen, then his own prayer · say amen like you mean it · *so be it, all of us*
116. **Lamb of God (Matthew 8:8)** · Agnus Dei · the bread is broken and all say Lord, I am not worthy, only say the word · say the centurion's words for someone · *only say the word*
117. **Communion and the sending** · Mass · Catholics receive; others often come forward for a blessing; then go forth, the Mass is ended · go do one kind thing after · *the mass is a sending*

## Part 3 · The rosary (Days 118–124)
118. **How the rosary is built** · decade · the creed, an Our Father, three Hail Marys, then five decades each on one mystery · hold beads or count on fingers · *a garden of roses*
119. **The joyful mysteries** · mystery · annunciation, visitation, nativity, presentation, finding in the Temple (Mondays and Saturdays) · pray one joyful decade · *joy has a story*
120. **The luminous mysteries** · — · added by John Paul II in 2002: baptism, Cana, the kingdom, transfiguration, Eucharist (Thursdays) · pray one luminous decade · *the light years*
121. **The sorrowful mysteries** · — · the agony, the scourging, the thorns, the carrying, the cross (Tuesdays and Fridays) · pray one sorrowful decade for someone suffering · *walk the hard road with him*
122. **The glorious mysteries** · — · resurrection, ascension, Pentecost, assumption, Mary crowned (Wednesdays and Sundays) · pray one glorious decade · *the end is glory*
123. **Hail, Holy Queen** · Salve Regina · the closing prayer calls us poor banished children, sighing in this valley of tears · pray the Salve Regina once · *our life, our sweetness, our hope*
124. **One decade a day** · — · a whole rosary takes about twenty minutes; a single decade is a real prayer too · pray one decade on a walk · *ten beads is enough*

## Part 4 · The seven sacraments (Days 125–132)
125. **What a sacrament is** · sacrament · an outward sign, given by Christ, that gives the grace it signifies (CCC 1131) · notice one sign that does what it says · *signs that do something*
126. **Baptism** · — · water poured three times in the name of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit · find out your baptism date · *your first birthday*
127. **Confirmation** · chrism · the bishop anoints the forehead with perfumed oil: be sealed with the gift of the Holy Spirit · ask the Spirit for one gift · *sealed*
128. **The Eucharist** · — · the source and summit of the Christian life (CCC 1324) · sit with the words source and summit · *everything flows from the table*
129. **Reconciliation** · — · the sacrament of return, where sins are forgiven through a priest (CCC 1422) · think of what you'd want forgiven · *there's always a way back*
130. **Anointing of the sick** · — · not only for the dying; oil for anyone seriously ill or old (CCC 1514) · pray for someone in hospital · *the sick are not forgotten*
131. **Holy orders** · ordination · deacons, priests and bishops ordained by the laying on of hands · pray for a priest you know · *called to serve*
132. **Matrimony** · — · in the Latin Church the couple are the ministers; they give the sacrament to each other · pray for a married couple · *two ministers, one promise*

## Part 5 · Confession, up close (Days 133–137)
133. **The examination of conscience** · conscience · a slow look back using the commandments or the beatitudes · spend three minutes looking back honestly · *look honestly, not harshly*
134. **Walking in** · — · behind a screen or face to face; bless me Father, it's been this long · learn the opening words · *the hardest part is the door*
135. **The act of contrition** · contrition · sorry because of love, not only fear, and a promise to try again · pray an act of contrition in your own words · *sorry, and resolved*
136. **Absolution and penance** · absolution · I absolve you, and a small penance to begin the repair · do one act of repair today · *forgiven, then sent*
137. **The seal** · seal of confession · a priest may never reveal what he hears, under any pressure (CCC 1467) · keep someone's secret faithfully · *what's said there stays there*

## Part 6 · Adoration (Days 138–142)
138. **The tabernacle and the red lamp** · tabernacle · the lamp burning beside it tells you the Eucharist is reserved there · find the red lamp in a church · *someone is home*
139. **The monstrance** · monstrance · a gold sunburst that shows the host so people can pray before it · look at one thing for a full minute · *just looking is prayer*
140. **Could you not watch one hour (Matthew 26:40)** · holy hour · a holy hour answers his question in Gethsemane · sit five minutes without a phone · *watch one hour*
141. **Benediction and the Tantum Ergo** · Benediction · a blessing with the monstrance, and a hymn written by Aquinas · listen to Tantum Ergo once · *the blessing comes to you*
142. **Spiritual communion** · — · when you can't receive, you can ask to be united in desire · pray a short spiritual communion · *desire is a door*

## Part 7 · The liturgical year (Days 143–155)
143. **The colors of the year** · liturgical year · violet, white, green, red, and rose twice a year · notice the color at your next Mass · *time has colors*
144. **Advent** · Advent · four weeks of waiting, a wreath of four candles, one lit each Sunday · light one candle this evening · *wait well*
145. **Christmas is a season** · — · the octave and the season run on to the Baptism of the Lord · keep one Christmas thing past the day · *christmas isn't one day*
146. **Epiphany and the chalked door** · — · many families chalk C+M+B and the year above the door, for the magi and a blessing · bless your doorway · *christ bless this house*
147. **Ordinary Time** · — · ordinary means counted Sundays, the long green growing season · do your ordinary work as prayer · *green, growing*
148. **Ash Wednesday (Genesis 3:19)** · — · ashes on the forehead: dust thou art, and into dust thou shalt return · sit with your own mortality kindly · *dust, and loved*
149. **Prayer, fasting, almsgiving (Matthew 6:1–18)** · almsgiving · Lent's three practices, done in secret · pick one of the three for today · *in secret*
150. **The Stations of the Cross** · stations · fourteen stops from condemnation to the tomb · walk slowly and pray at three stops · *walk it with him*
151. **Holy Thursday** · Triduum · the Mass of the Lord's Supper, washing of feet, and the altar stripped bare · wash something for someone · *the three holiest days begin*
152. **Good Friday** · — · no Mass anywhere; people come forward to venerate the cross · keep a quiet hour at three o'clock · *the one day with no mass*
153. **The Easter Vigil** · Exsultet · a fire in the dark, one candle, then the whole church lit, and the ancient Easter song · light a candle in the dark · *this is the night*
154. **Fifty days of Easter** · — · Easter lasts until Pentecost; the alleluia returns · say alleluia out loud · *longer than lent*
155. **Holy days and the saints' calendar** · holy day of obligation · a few great feasts are kept like Sundays (the list varies by country) · find today's saint · *every day belongs to someone*
156. **Camp 3 close: a rule of life** · rule of life · a morning offering, a decade, the Angelus, an evening look back · write your small daily rule · *small, daily, mine*

# CAMP 4 · THE TEXT (Days 157–231)
*The Psalms, the Church's own prayer book, then the Gospel of Mark read straight through. The Douay numbers most psalms one lower than modern Bibles; the Hebrew number is in brackets.*

## Part 1 · The Psalms (Days 157–191)

164. **Lift up your gates (Psalm 23 [24])** · — · the earth is the Lord's, and the king of glory comes in · open a window or a door on purpose · *the earth is the lord's*

167. **Blessed are they whose sins are covered (Psalm 31 [32])** · penitential psalms · one of seven penitential psalms; silence made the bones grow old · admit one small wrong to God · *silence was heavier*
168. **Taste and see (Psalm 33 [34])** · — · O taste, and see that the Lord is sweet · eat one thing slowly with thanks · *taste and see*
169. **As the hart panteth (Psalm 41 [42])** · — · a deer thirsty for water, and a soul asking why art thou sad · drink a glass of water as prayer · *why art thou sad, my soul*
170. **Be still (Psalm 45 [46])** · — · mountains fall into the sea, and God says be still and see that I am God · be still for sixty seconds · *be still and see*
171. **Have mercy on me (Psalm 50 [51])** · Miserere · David's psalm after his great sin, prayed every Friday morning · pray the first verse · *a clean heart*
