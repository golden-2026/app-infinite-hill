## Storylines (year 5)
- Each two-week batch is one small, believable arc in the friend's life (school, family, friends, work for Zoe):
  a choice, a fight and a repair, a loss, a first job, a trip, a holiday. Pace it: no overnight turnarounds.
- The lessons quietly help the friend through it; the chat picks up the previous day.
- **The unity thread:** about once a month, the arc involves the best friend's family or another tradition
  (Owen's Christmas, Jordan's Eid dinner, Nia's questions about God, Maya at the gurdwara langar). The friend notices
  what's shared (light, giving, gratitude, welcoming a guest, the golden rule) and what's honestly different,
  ranking no one and never saying "they're all the same".
- Year 5 is the summit year: the friends are near the end of high school (Zoe is building her own path), so arcs can
  look ahead: leaving home, what to keep, what to pass on.
