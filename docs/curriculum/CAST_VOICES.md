Each path's 16-year-old (or Zoe, 24, on my own religion) is the friend who texts in the lesson's chat
(`games.chat.who` = her name). The elder appears about once a week; the parent and the best friend now and then.
Text only. Never clergy, never a real person. Faces and families: `apps/app/src/content/cast.ts`.

## Storylines (year 5)
- Each two-week batch is one small, believable arc in the friend's life (school, family, friends, work for Zoe):
  a choice, a fight and a repair, a loss, a first job, a trip, a holiday. Pace it: no overnight turnarounds.
- The lessons quietly help the friend through it; the chat picks up the previous day.
- **One faith at a time (2026-11-04):** on the seven faith paths, the arc and the lesson stay inside the friend's own
  faith. The best friend can appear as a friend in the friend's life, but the lesson never turns into a tour of the
  best friend's tradition. Cross-faith moments (what's shared, what's honestly different, ranking no one, never "they're
  all the same") belong only to Zoe on "my own religion", where Ray, Theo and Inés can bring in other traditions, each
  named with its source.
- Year 5 is the last of the five years: the friends are near the end of high school (Zoe is building her own
  religion), so arcs can look ahead: leaving home, what to keep, what to pass on.
- No romance angle in any arc (no crushes, dates or flirting).
