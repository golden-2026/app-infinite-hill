# YEAR FIVE — "the summit" · reading the sources, walking with the poets, living your own way (Days 1427–1791)
*The last year climbs to the top. First, short sacred and wisdom texts read whole and slowly, as a guest in each tradition's house. Then the poets as daily companions, public-domain translations only. Then the long middle: building a personal practice that lasts, and living it at work, at home and in the world. The summit is not an ending; it walks you back down to ordinary life with open hands. Sources: KJV; JPS 1917; Yusuf Ali; Müller (Dhammapada, Heart Sutra, Upanishads); Arnold (Gita); Macauliffe (Japji); Taylor (Pirkei Avot, 1897); Legge (Analects); Nicholson (Rumi); Clarke and Bell (Hafiz); Tagore (Songs of Kabir, Gitanjali); Grierson and Barnett (Lalla); Giles and Waley (Chinese poets); original texts of Herbert, Hopkins, Wordsworth, Blake, Whitman, Dickinson, Rossetti.*
