# YEAR THREE — "the great questions" · wisdom from every tradition, by theme (Days 697–1061)
*Six questions every tradition has faced: suffering, death, love, work, forgiveness, awe. Each block lets the traditions speak one at a time, in their own words and with their own sources, then adds what science can honestly say, then the practices. Teachers and saints appear inside the themes. The traditions don't give the same answers, and we never say they do. Holy days fall inside the blocks where they belong. Sources: KJV, JPS 1917, Yusuf Ali, Arnold (Gita), Müller and Hume (Upanishads), Rhys Davids and Müller (Pali texts), Legge (Chinese classics), Macauliffe (Sikh), Jowett (Plato), Warrack (Julian), Garnett (Dostoevsky, Tolstoy); modern writers paraphrased and cited.*
