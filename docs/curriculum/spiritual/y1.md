# CAMP 2 · THE STORIES (Days 22–96) — "wait, these are GOOD"

## Week 4 · The Stoics wake up (Days 22–28)
22. **Marcus doesn't want to get up** · — · the emperor argues with himself under the blankets: was I made to lie here warm? (Meditations 5.1) · get up at the first alarm, once · *up, to do a human's work*
23. **The master and the leg** · — · enslaved, Epictetus warns his master he'll break his leg, it breaks, and he says calmly "didn't I tell you?" (told by Celsus, in Origen, Against Celsus 7.53; another ancient source says illness) · notice one pain you can't change today · *said calmly*
24. **Epictetus goes free** · — · freed, he teaches in Rome, is banished with all philosophers by Domitian, and opens a school at Nicopolis around 93 CE · write down one thing no one can take · *the school is inside*
25. **Zeno's shipwreck** · — · a merchant loses his cargo of purple dye off Athens and wanders into a bookshop (Diogenes Laërtius 7.2–4) · name a loss that sent you somewhere good · *i sailed well when i was wrecked*
26. **Follow that man** · — · Zeno asks the bookseller where men like Socrates can be found, and Crates the Cynic walks past (Diogenes Laërtius 7.3) · follow one curiosity for five minutes · *follow that man*

28. **Cleanthes carries water** · — · the boxer who drew water at night so he could study by day (Diogenes Laërtius 7.168–169) · do one humble task well · *carry the water*

## Week 5 · Rome's hard nights (Days 29–35)
29. **Cato's last night** · — · at Utica in 46 BCE, refusing Caesar's pardon, Cato reads Plato on the soul through the night, and his ending is told honestly, not as a model (Plutarch, Cato the Younger 66–70) · if a night feels heavy, tell someone · *reach out before morning*

31. **The ledger of hours** · — · we guard our money closely and hand out our time to anyone who asks (On the Shortness of Life 3) · guard one hour tomorrow · *time is the only property*
32. **Seneca in exile** · — · banished to Corsica, he writes to comfort his mother instead of asking for comfort (Consolation to Helvia) · write two lines to someone who worries about you · *comfort the comforter*
33. **Seneca's last hours** · — · ordered to die by Nero, he tells his friends he leaves them the one thing he has, the image of his life (Tacitus, Annals 15.62) · write one line: the image you'd leave · *the image of a life*

# CAMP 3 · THE PRACTICES (Days 97–156) — "I can do this"

## Week 15 · Breath (Days 97–103)

98. **Longer out than in** · — · slow breathing with a longer exhale tends to calm the body, a finding repeated in many lab studies · in for 4, out for 6, three minutes · *longer out*
