# CAMP 2 · THE STORIES (Days 22–96) — "the original serial"
*Genesis read as a serial, then Exodus. One story beat a day, in order. A word every two or three days.*

## Week 4 · In the beginning (Days 22–28)
22. **Before anything, a voice (Genesis 1:1–5)** · Bereshit · the Torah opens "in the beginning" and speaks light into the dark · say one kind sentence out loud before noon · *words make worlds*
23. **Six days of making (Genesis 1:6–31)** · — · sky, sea, land, lights, creatures, each day closing "and it was good" · name six good things you can see · *count the good*
24. **In the image (Genesis 1:26–27)** · tzelem Elohim · every person is made in God's image; the rabbis say a king's coins look alike, but God's people never do (Mishnah Sanhedrin 4:5) · look at one stranger's face with care · *no two faces alike*
25. **The seventh day (Genesis 2:1–3)** · — · God finishes, then stops, and blesses the stopping itself · stop everything for sixty seconds · *finished is holy*
26. **Dust and breath (Genesis 2:4–7)** · adam · the first human is shaped from adamah, the earth, and given breath · breathe in and feel your feet · *earth and breath*
27. **Not good to be alone (Genesis 2:18–25)** · — · the first thing the Torah calls "not good" is loneliness · message someone who might be lonely · *not good alone*
28. **The garden and the question (Genesis 3)** · — · the fruit, the hiding, and God's first question to a person: "Where art thou?" · ask yourself honestly where you are today · *where are you*

## Week 5 · Brothers and floods (Days 29–35)
29. **Clothes for the road (Genesis 3:21–24)** · — · before they leave the garden, God makes them garments, a first act of care · do one caring thing for someone leaving · *care goes with you*
30. **Cain and Abel (Genesis 4:1–8)** · — · two offerings, one accepted, and a jealousy that crouches at the door · notice one jealous thought and name it · *it crouches at the door*
31. **My brother's keeper (Genesis 4:9–16)** · — · Cain asks "Am I my brother's keeper?" and the rest of the Torah answers him · check on a sibling or an old friend · *yes, you are*
32. **Righteous in his generation (Genesis 6:9)** · tzaddik · the rabbis argue whether Noah was great in any age or only beside his neighbours · do one right thing no one sees · *good where you are*
33. **The ark (Genesis 6:14–7:24)** · — · a box of wood, pairs of every creature, forty days of rain · give an animal or a plant some care · *carry what's alive*
34. **The dove and the olive leaf (Genesis 8)** · — · the waters go down, and the dove comes back with a fresh leaf · look for one sign that things are easing · *the leaf comes back*
35. **The rainbow (Genesis 9:8–17)** · brit · God's first covenant is with all living things, and the bow in the cloud is its sign · make one promise small enough to keep · *a promise to everything*

## Week 6 · Babel and the call (Days 36–42)
36. **The tower of Babel (Genesis 11:1–9)** · — · one language, one tower, and a scattering into many peoples · learn a greeting in another language · *many tongues*
37. **Go forth (Genesis 12:1–3)** · Lech Lecha · Abram is told to leave land, birthplace and father's house for a place not yet named · take a different route somewhere today · *go before you know*
38. **Abram breaks the idols (Genesis Rabbah 38)** · midrash · a rabbinic story: young Abram smashes his father's idols and blames the biggest one · question one thing you just assume · *question the statue*
39. **Abram and Sarai in Egypt (Genesis 12:10–20)** · — · famine, fear and a half-truth that nearly costs everything; the Torah doesn't hide its heroes' faults · admit one small untruth to yourself · *heroes stumble too*
40. **Abram lets Lot choose (Genesis 13)** · — · to keep the peace, the elder lets his nephew take first pick of the land · let someone else choose first today · *you choose first*
41. **Count the stars (Genesis 15:1–6)** · — · childless and old, Abram is told to look up and count the stars · look at the sky tonight for one minute · *look up and count*
42. **Hagar names God (Genesis 16)** · El Roi · a runaway servant meets an angel at a spring and calls God "the God who sees me" · notice someone who usually goes unseen · *you are seen*

## Week 7 · Tents and tests (Days 43–49)
43. **Three strangers at the tent (Genesis 18:1–8)** · hachnasat orchim · Abraham, still healing, runs in the heat to welcome three travellers · offer someone a drink or a seat · *run to the guest*
44. **Sarah laughs (Genesis 18:9–15)** · — · told she will bear a child at ninety, she laughs; the child will be Isaac, "he laughs" · laugh at something that seems impossible · *nothing is too hard*
45. **Arguing for Sodom (Genesis 18:16–33)** · — · Abraham bargains with God from fifty righteous people down to ten · speak up once for someone not in the room · *argue for mercy*
46. **Lot's wife looks back (Genesis 19)** · — · the cities fall, and a woman with no name turns to look · let go of one thing you keep looking back at · *face forward*
47. **Hagar and Ishmael in the desert (Genesis 21:9–21)** · — · sent away with bread and water, Hagar weeps; God hears the boy and opens her eyes to a well · look for the well you haven't noticed · *the well was there*
48. **The binding of Isaac (Genesis 22)** · Akedah · the hardest story in Genesis; Jewish readers have argued over it for two thousand years · sit with one question you can't resolve · *the tradition wrestles here*
49. **Sarah's burial (Genesis 23)** · — · Abraham buys the cave of Machpelah and pays in full to bury his wife with dignity · honour the memory of someone gone · *grief deserves ground*

## Week 8 · Isaac and Rebekah, Jacob and Esau (Days 50–56)
50. **A wife at the well (Genesis 24:1–27)** · chesed · the servant's test: the one who offers water to him and to his camels too · offer help nobody asked for · *water the camels too*
51. **Rebekah says "I will go" (Genesis 24:57–58)** · — · her family asks her, and she chooses for herself · say yes to one good invitation · *ask her first*
52. **Isaac redigs the wells (Genesis 26:17–22)** · — · the quiet patriarch reopens his father's wells and gives them their old names · go back to one good old habit · *redig the well*
53. **A bowl of red stew (Genesis 25:19–34)** · — · twins who wrestle in the womb, and a birthright sold for lentils · notice what you'd trade too cheaply · *don't sell it for stew*
54. **The stolen blessing (Genesis 27)** · — · goat skins, a blind father, and Esau's cry "Bless me, even me also, O my father" · think of someone who feels passed over · *bless me too*
55. **Jacob's ladder (Genesis 28:10–22)** · — · asleep on a stone, he dreams of angels going up and down · stop in an ordinary place and call it holy · *god was here all along*
56. **Seven years like a few days (Genesis 29:1–20)** · — · Jacob works seven years for Rachel and they seem to him but a few days · do one task for love, not the clock · *love shortens the time*

## Week 9 · Jacob's long road (Days 57–63)
57. **The switched bride (Genesis 29:21–30)** · — · the trickster is tricked, and wakes to find Leah instead of Rachel · notice when what you did comes back · *it comes back around*
58. **Leah's sons (Genesis 29:31–35)** · — · the unloved sister names each son for a hope, and at Judah simply praises · give thanks for one thing without asking for more · *this time, praise*
59. **Rachel's longing (Genesis 30:1–24)** · — · years of waiting, then Joseph; the family grows through rivalry and prayer · hope honestly for one thing · *long honestly*
60. **Leaving Laban (Genesis 31)** · — · twenty hard years, stolen idols, and a heap of stones as a boundary of peace · set one boundary kindly · *a heap of witness*
61. **Wrestling at the river (Genesis 32:23–33)** · Yisrael · Jacob wrestles until dawn and is renamed Israel, one who strives with God · name a struggle you're still in · *the struggle is the name*
62. **The brothers meet (Genesis 33)** · — · Esau runs to him, embraces him, and they weep · reach out to someone you once wronged · *he ran to meet him*
63. **Dinah (Genesis 34)** · — · a hard chapter of violence and revenge that the Torah tells without softening · sit quietly with a hard story · *not every story ends well*

## Weeks 10–11 · Joseph (Days 64–76)
64. **Rachel dies on the road (Genesis 35:16–20)** · — · she dies bearing Benjamin and is buried by the roadside; tradition says she still weeps for her children · remember a mother, yours or another · *she weeps for her children*
65. **The coat (Genesis 37:1–11)** · — · a favourite son, a special coat, and dreams of sheaves bowing down · notice a favour that breeds resentment · *favourites cost*
66. **Into the pit (Genesis 37:12–36)** · — · the brothers throw Joseph in a pit and sell him; Reuben comes back too late · do the right thing now, not later · *don't come back too late*
67. **Judah and Tamar (Genesis 38)** · — · shown his own seal, Judah says "She is more righteous than I" · admit one time someone else was right · *she was right*
68. **Potiphar's house (Genesis 39)** · — · Joseph rises, is falsely accused, and lands in prison · keep doing good in a bad place · *good in the pit too*
69. **Dreams in prison (Genesis 40)** · — · the butler and the baker; the butler forgets him · remember someone who helped you · *don't forget joseph*
70. **Pharaoh's dreams (Genesis 41)** · — · seven fat cows, seven thin ones, and a plan to store grain · put something aside for later · *store in the good years*
71. **The brothers come for bread (Genesis 42)** · — · they bow to a man they don't recognise · notice someone you may have misjudged · *you don't see who it is*
72. **The silver cup (Genesis 44:1–17)** · — · the test: will the brothers abandon another favourite son? · stand by someone when it costs you · *not this time*
73. **Judah's plea (Genesis 44:18–34)** · — · Judah offers himself as a slave in Benjamin's place · offer to carry someone's load · *take me instead*
74. **I am Joseph (Genesis 45:1–15)** · — · he weeps so loudly Egypt hears; "it was not you that sent me hither, but God" · reframe one old hurt, gently · *it led somewhere*
75. **Blessing the grandsons (Genesis 48)** · — · Jacob blesses Ephraim and Manasseh, whose names parents still use to bless children on Friday night · bless a child or a younger person · *may you be like them*
76. **Genesis ends (Genesis 49–50)** · chazak · Jacob's blessings, Joseph's forgiveness, and a coffin waiting to go home; finishing a book, the synagogue says "be strong" · finish one small thing and say chazak · *be strong, be strong*

## Weeks 11–13 · Exodus (Days 77–96)
77. **A king who knew not Joseph (Exodus 1:8–14)** · — · a new Pharaoh, fear of a growing people, and slavery · notice where fear turns into cruelty · *fear forgets*
78. **Shiphrah and Puah (Exodus 1:15–21)** · — · two midwives quietly refuse Pharaoh's order to kill the baby boys · refuse one small wrong thing · *the midwives said no*
79. **A basket on the Nile (Exodus 2:1–10)** · — · Yocheved hides her baby, Miriam watches, Pharaoh's daughter draws him out · watch over someone from a distance · *someone watches the basket*
80. **Moses goes out and sees (Exodus 2:11–15)** · — · the prince goes out to his people and sees their burdens · look straight at one hard reality · *he went out and saw*
81. **The burning bush (Exodus 3:1–6)** · — · a bush burns and is not consumed, and Moses turns aside to look · turn aside for one ordinary wonder · *turn aside and see*
82. **A name that is a verb (Exodus 3:13–15)** · Ehyeh · Moses asks God's name and hears "I AM THAT I AM" · sit for a minute with not fully knowing · *i will be what i will be*
83. **Who am I? (Exodus 3:11, 4:10–17)** · — · Moses protests that he is slow of speech; Aaron will speak for him · accept help with a weakness · *slow of speech, still sent*
84. **Let my people go (Exodus 5)** · — · the first demand, and Pharaoh makes the bricks harder to make · keep going when the first try fails · *it gets harder first*
85. **The ten plagues (Exodus 7–10)** · makot · blood, frogs, lice, swarms, pestilence, boils, hail, locusts, darkness · name one thing that holds people captive today · *ten ways to say let go*
86. **Pharaoh's hard heart (Exodus 8–10)** · — · each time he relents, then hardens; the rabbis say a heart can harden by habit · soften one stance held too long · *hearts harden by habit*
87. **The night of the firstborn (Exodus 11–12)** · Pesach · blood on the doorposts, and the Lord passes over the houses · eat one thing slowly and remember · *this night is different*
88. **Bread with no time to rise (Exodus 12:33–42)** · matzah · dough on their shoulders, and a mixed multitude going with them · leave one thing unfinished on purpose · *go when it's time*
89. **At the sea (Exodus 14)** · — · the army behind, water ahead; midrash says Nachshon walked in first · take the first step before it's easy · *walk in first*
90. **The Song of the Sea (Exodus 15:1–18)** · — · the people sing; the rabbis say God hushed the angels because the Egyptians were drowning (Megillah 10b) · celebrate without gloating · *don't sing over the drowning*
91. **Miriam's timbrel (Exodus 15:20–21)** · — · the prophetess takes a drum and leads the women in dance · dance or move for one minute · *bring the drum*
92. **Manna (Exodus 16)** · — · bread from heaven, enough for each day, and a double portion before Shabbat · take only what you need today · *enough for today*
93. **Jethro's advice (Exodus 18)** · — · Moses' father-in-law tells him he can't judge everyone alone · hand one task to someone else · *you can't carry it alone*
94. **The mountain (Exodus 19–20)** · — · thunder, a shofar, the whole people at the foot of Sinai; the rabbis say every Jewish soul was there · listen for one minute in silence · *everyone was there*
95. **The calf and the second tablets (Exodus 32–34)** · — · the covenant breaks, the tablets shatter, new ones are carved; the Talmud says both were kept in the ark (Berakhot 8b) · keep something broken with honour · *the broken tablets were kept too*
96. **Camp 2 close: the tabernacle (Exodus 35–40)** · Mishkan · Exodus ends with a portable dwelling built from everyone's gifts · give something small to a shared project · *build a place, presence comes*

# CAMP 3 · THE PRACTICES (Days 97–156) — "I can do this"
*How-to sessions. The hook is short; the practice is the session. Holiday sessions are read as their date comes near.*

## Weeks 14–15 · Shabbat as a practice (Days 97–108)
97. **Holy time, not holy place** · — · Shabbat is built in time; it begins at sundown on Friday and ends at nightfall Saturday · choose one hour this week to make nothing · *holy time*
98. **Getting ready** · erev Shabbat · Friday is for cooking, cleaning and washing, as if a guest were coming · tidy one surface before Friday · *get ready for a guest*
99. **Covering the eyes** · — · hands circle the candle flames, then cover the eyes while the blessing is said · cover your eyes and breathe three times · *eyes closed, the week ends*
100. **Shalom Aleichem** · — · the Friday night song welcomes angels; the Talmud says two walk you home (Shabbat 119b) · hum a song as you walk in your door · *angels walk you home*
101. **Blessing the children** · — · hands on a child's head: may you be like Ephraim and Manasseh, or like Sarah, Rebekah, Rachel and Leah · bless someone you love, aloud or silently · *hands on heads*
102. **Kiddush** · Kiddush · a cup of wine or grape juice declares the day holy, remembering creation and the Exodus · raise any drink and say one sentence of thanks · *make it holy out loud*
103. **Two loaves** · challah · two loaves recall the double manna; they stay covered while the wine is blessed · cover your bread before you eat · *two loaves, enough*
104. **A woman of valour (Proverbs 31:10–31)** · — · many homes sing Eshet Chayil on Friday night to honour the women of the house · praise someone in your home out loud · *say the praise*
105. **What isn't done, and why (Mishnah Shabbat 7:2)** · melacha · thirty-nine kinds of creative work, linked to building the tabernacle, are set aside · choose one kind of work to skip · *not doing is a practice*
106. **Shabbat afternoon** · menuchah · rest, a nap, a walk, study and friends; the Talmud calls Shabbat one sixtieth of the world to come (Berakhot 57b) · take a real nap or a slow walk · *a taste of what's coming*
107. **The third meal** · seudah shlishit · a slow meal at dusk, with quiet songs as the day fades · eat one meal slowly at dusk · *don't rush the ending*
108. **Havdalah** · Havdalah · a braided candle, sweet spices and wine separate holy time from the week · smell something fragrant as the week begins · *carry the scent in*

109. **Modeh Ani** · Modeh Ani · the first words on waking thank God for returning the soul · say thank you before you get up · *thank you, first*
110. **Washing the hands** · netilat yadayim · a cup of water poured over each hand begins the day clean · pour water over your hands slowly · *begin clean*
111. **Asher Yatzar** · — · a blessing after the bathroom thanks God for a body whose openings work · say thanks once after the bathroom · *the body is a wonder*

114. **A hundred blessings a day (Menachot 43b)** · — · the Talmud teaches a person should say a hundred blessings each day · count the thanks you give today · *a hundred small thanks*
115. **Blessing after eating (Deuteronomy 8:10)** · Birkat Hamazon · the one blessing the Torah itself commands: eat, be satisfied, and bless · give thanks after a meal, not just before · *full, then grateful*
116. **For firsts** · Shehecheyanu · a blessing for new things, new seasons and first times each year · say it for one first today · *you kept me alive for this*
117. **Blessings for wonders** · — · there are blessings for lightning, thunder, the sea, a rainbow and trees in blossom · look for one wonder to bless · *the world has blessings built in*
118. **Blessing on seeing people** · — · blessings exist for seeing a great scholar, a ruler, or a friend not seen in a year · greet an old friend today · *seeing you is a blessing*
119. **Hard news** · Baruch Dayan HaEmet · on hearing of a death, Jews bless "the true judge", a blessing inside grief · sit with grief without explaining it · *even here, a word*
120. **The bedtime Shema** · — · before sleep: the Shema, and forgiveness for anyone who hurt you that day · forgive one thing before you sleep · *forgive before sleeping*

# CAMP 4 · THE TEXT (Days 157–231) — "one portion a week"

## Week 23 · How the portion works (Days 157–160)

## Bereshit (Genesis 1:1–6:8) · Days 161–165

162. **Bereshit: two creation accounts (Genesis 1–2)** · — · chapter one is vast and ordered, chapter two close and earthy; tradition holds both · hold two views of one thing today · *both are true*
163. **Bereshit: one person, a whole world** · — · one human was created first, the Mishnah says, to teach that one life is a whole world (Sanhedrin 4:5) · treat one person as a whole world · *each life a world*
164. **Bereshit: you can rule over it (Genesis 4:7)** · yetzer hara · God tells Cain the urge crouches, but he can master it · delay one impulse by a minute · *you can rule it*
165. **Bereshit: walking with God (Genesis 5:24)** · — · among long lists of generations, Enoch "walked with God, and he was not" · walk slowly for five minutes · *walk with god*

## Noach (Genesis 6:9–11:32) · Days 166–170
