# YEAR THREE — "the story of a people" (Days 697–1061)
*Two halves. First, the Prophets and the Writings read as one long story: Joshua to Chronicles, the whole Tanakh. Then the history after the Bible, from the Maccabees to today, with its teachers. The Holocaust sessions are marked, slow, and can be paused at any time. Scripture quoted only in JPS 1917 (Hebrew verse numbering).*
