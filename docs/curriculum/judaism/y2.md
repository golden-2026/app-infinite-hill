# YEAR TWO — "the whole Torah, the whole prayer book" (Days 332–696)
*Three blocks: the Torah cycle finished portion by portion, from Bo to the last verse of Deuteronomy · the siddur, the prayer book, walked through in order · the Jewish year in depth. Scripture quoted only in JPS 1917; the siddur after Singer (1890).*

## Block A · Exodus finished (Days 332–378)
332. **Year two opens: back in the cycle** · — · last year stopped at Va'era; this year the Torah is read through to its last word · find this week's portion · *the scroll keeps rolling*

### Bo (Exodus 10:1–13:16)
333. **Bo: come, not go (Exodus 10:1)** · — · God tells Moses "come unto Pharaoh", as if God is already in the room · walk into a hard room knowing you're not alone · *come, not go*
334. **Bo: darkness that could be felt (Exodus 10:21–23)** · — · three days so dark "they saw not one another"; yet in Israel's homes there was light · check on someone in a dark time · *see your brother*
335. **Bo: this month is yours (Exodus 12:1–2)** · — · the first command to the whole people is to mark time by the new moon · mark today's date on purpose · *own your time*
336. **Bo: tell your child (Exodus 13:8)** · haggadah · "thou shalt tell thy son in that day", the root of the seder's telling · tell a younger person a family story · *tell your child*
337. **Bo: a sign upon your hand (Exodus 13:9, 16)** · — · the Exodus is remembered on the hand and between the eyes, a root of tefillin · wear something that reminds you · *remember with your body*

### Beshalach (Exodus 13:17–17:16)
338. **Beshalach: the long way round (Exodus 13:17)** · — · God leads them away from the short road lest they see war and turn back · take the longer road once · *the long way can be kinder*
339. **Beshalach: Joseph's bones (Exodus 13:19)** · — · while others gather silver, Moses carries Joseph's bones, keeping an old promise · keep one old promise · *carry the bones*
340. **Beshalach: stop praying, move (Exodus 14:15)** · — · at the sea, God asks Moses why he cries out, and tells the people to go forward · act after you pray · *pray, then go forward*
341. **Beshalach: bitter water made sweet (Exodus 15:22–26)** · — · at Marah the water is bitter until a tree is thrown in · sweeten one bitter moment · *a tree in the water*
342. **Beshalach: held-up hands (Exodus 17:8–13)** · Amalek · when Moses' arms tire, Aaron and Hur hold them up until sunset · hold up someone who's tired · *hold up the hands*

### Yitro (Exodus 18:1–20:23)
343. **Yitro: named for an outsider** · — · the portion with the Ten Words is named for Jethro, a Midianite priest · learn from someone outside your group · *wisdom from outside*
344. **Yitro: one person, one heart (Exodus 19:2)** · — · Rashi: at Sinai they camped like one person with one heart · agree with someone on one thing · *one heart*
345. **Yitro: on eagles' wings (Exodus 19:4)** · — · "I bore you on eagles' wings, and brought you unto Myself" · remember a time you were carried · *on eagles' wings*
346. **Yitro: the first word (Exodus 20:2)** · anochi · the Ten Words begin not with a rule but with "I am the LORD thy God, who brought thee out" · remember what set you free · *first, a relationship*
347. **Yitro: the last word is inside (Exodus 20:14)** · — · the final commandment, not to covet, governs the heart rather than the hands · notice one envy and let it go · *the last word is inside*

### Mishpatim (Exodus 21:1–24:18)
348. **Mishpatim: straight after Sinai, the details** · mishpatim · after the thunder come laws about workers, damages and loans · treat one ordinary rule with respect · *holiness lives in details*
349. **Mishpatim: the heart of a stranger (Exodus 23:9)** · ger · "for ye know the heart of a stranger, seeing ye were strangers" · do one kind thing for a newcomer · *you know the stranger's heart*
350. **Mishpatim: your enemy's donkey (Exodus 23:4–5)** · — · return your enemy's straying ox and help his fallen donkey up · help someone you don't like · *help your enemy's donkey*
351. **Mishpatim: the widow and the orphan (Exodus 22:21–23)** · — · God hears the cry of the widow and the fatherless · support someone without protection · *god hears them*
352. **Mishpatim: we will do, and we will hear (Exodus 24:7)** · na'aseh v'nishma · the people promise to act before they fully understand · try one practice before you understand it · *do, then understand*

### Terumah (Exodus 25:1–27:19)
353. **Terumah: willing hearts (Exodus 25:2)** · terumah · the sanctuary is built only from gifts "of every man whose heart maketh him willing" · give something freely today · *only what's willing*
354. **Terumah: among them (Exodus 25:8)** · — · "that I may dwell among them", not in it; teachers read it as within each person · make a small space for quiet · *among them, not in it*
355. **Terumah: gold inside and out (Exodus 25:11)** · — · the ark is gilded inside and out; the Talmud says a scholar's inside must match his outside (Yoma 72b) · let your inside match your outside · *gold inside and out*
356. **Terumah: the cherubim face each other (Exodus 25:20–22)** · keruvim · two golden figures face one another, and God speaks from between them · face someone fully as you talk · *the voice between the faces*
357. **Terumah: the poles stay in (Exodus 25:15)** · — · the ark's carrying poles are never removed, always ready to travel · keep one thing ready to move · *ready to travel*

### Tetzaveh (Exodus 27:20–30:10)
358. **Tetzaveh: beaten olives (Exodus 27:20)** · ner tamid · the eternal lamp burns pure oil from crushed olives · honour a light that came from pain · *crushed olives give light*
359. **Tetzaveh: Moses' name is missing** · — · Moses isn't named in this portion; some link it to his offer to be blotted out for his people (Exodus 32:32) · do good without your name on it · *step out of the frame*
360. **Tetzaveh: clothes for splendour (Exodus 28:2)** · kohen · the priest's garments are "for splendour and for beauty", each with meaning · dress with intention today · *clothes carry meaning*
361. **Tetzaveh: names over the heart (Exodus 28:12, 29)** · — · the high priest carries the tribes' names on his shoulders and over his heart · carry someone's name in prayer · *names on the heart*
362. **Tetzaveh: holy on the forehead (Exodus 28:36–38)** · — · a gold plate on his forehead reads "HOLY TO THE LORD" · write one word to guide your thinking · *holy on the forehead*

### Ki Tisa (Exodus 30:11–34:35)
363. **Ki Tisa: the half shekel (Exodus 30:11–16)** · — · rich and poor give the same half coin; no one counts as whole alone · add to something shared · *half makes a whole*
364. **Ki Tisa: Bezalel (Exodus 31:1–6)** · — · an artist filled "with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding" · make something with your hands · *craft is spirit*
365. **Ki Tisa: even the sanctuary rests (Exodus 31:12–17)** · V'shamru · the building work stops for Shabbat; these verses are sung on Friday night · pause a project for Shabbat · *even the temple rests*
366. **Ki Tisa: show me thy glory (Exodus 33:18–23)** · — · Moses asks to see God, and sees only from behind, from a cleft in the rock · look back at where God was · *seen only from behind*
367. **Ki Tisa: the thirteen attributes (Exodus 34:6–7)** · — · "The LORD, the LORD, God, merciful and gracious, long-suffering", chanted on holy days · practise one of these qualities · *merciful and gracious*

### Vayakhel (Exodus 35:1–38:20)
368. **Vayakhel: he gathered them (Exodus 35:1)** · kehillah · after the golden calf, Moses gathers everyone together again · gather people for something good · *gather the people*
369. **Vayakhel: too much (Exodus 36:5–7)** · — · the people bring so much that Moses asks them to stop · notice when there is enough · *they had to say stop*
370. **Vayakhel: the women's mirrors (Exodus 38:8)** · — · the washing basin is made from women's copper mirrors; Rashi says God held them most dear · honour an overlooked gift · *mirrors became the basin*
371. **Vayakhel: wise-hearted spinners (Exodus 35:25–26)** · — · women whose hearts stirred them spun the goats' hair with skill · credit someone's quiet skill · *wise hearts spin*
372. **Vayakhel: no fire on Shabbat (Exodus 35:3)** · — · the one Shabbat labour the Torah names is kindling fire; some read anger into it too · keep your temper today · *no fire today*

### Pekudei (Exodus 38:21–40:38)
373. **Pekudei: an open account (Exodus 38:21)** · — · Moses gives a public account of every gift, though no one asked · be transparent about one thing · *account for it*
374. **Pekudei: as the Lord commanded** · — · the phrase repeats as each piece is finished · do one task exactly as promised · *as it was asked*
375. **Pekudei: Moses blessed them (Exodus 39:43)** · — · Moses sees the finished work and blesses the workers · bless someone's finished work · *bless the workers*
376. **Pekudei: the cloud filled it (Exodus 40:34–35)** · — · the presence is so full that Moses himself cannot enter · leave room for something bigger · *presence fills*
377. **Pekudei: in all their journeys (Exodus 40:38)** · — · Exodus ends with cloud by day and fire by night, travelling with them · notice what guides you · *cloud by day, fire by night*
378. **Exodus close: chazak** · — · slaves have become a people with a law and a sanctuary · say chazak for finishing Exodus · *be strong, be strengthened*

## Block B · Leviticus, the book of holiness (Days 379–429)
379. **Where children once began** · Vayikra · Jewish children traditionally started Torah with Leviticus: let the pure come and study purity (Leviticus Rabbah 7:3) · read Leviticus 1:1 · *begin with the pure*

### Vayikra (Leviticus 1:1–5:26)
380. **Vayikra: the small alef (Leviticus 1:1)** · — · the first word is written with a small letter; midrash sees Moses' humility · make yourself a little smaller · *the small letter*
381. **Vayikra: offering means nearness** · korban · the word for offering comes from the root "to come near" · draw near to something today · *offering means nearness*
382. **Vayikra: the poor person's flour (Leviticus 2:1)** · — · for the flour offering the text says "soul"; Rashi: the poor person gives their very soul · honour a small gift · *the poor bring their soul*
383. **Vayikra: salt on every offering (Leviticus 2:13)** · — · "the salt of the covenant" on every offering, still sprinkled on bread today · salt your bread and remember · *salt of the covenant*
384. **Vayikra: when a leader errs (Leviticus 4:22)** · — · the text says "when", not "if"; leaders will make mistakes · forgive a leader's honest mistake · *when, not if*

### Tzav (Leviticus 6:1–8:36)
385. **Tzav: the fire shall not go out (Leviticus 6:6)** · — · "it shall not go out"; a fire tended every day · tend one thing daily · *keep the fire*
386. **Tzav: first, the ashes (Leviticus 6:3–4)** · — · each day the priest begins by clearing yesterday's ashes, in plain clothes · clear yesterday's clutter · *clear the ashes first*
387. **Tzav: the thanks offering (Leviticus 7:12)** · todah · after a rescue, a thanks offering; midrash says thanks alone will never cease · give thanks for a rescue · *thanks outlasts everything*
388. **Tzav: ear, thumb, toe (Leviticus 8:23–24)** · — · priests are anointed on the ear, the thumb and the big toe: hear, do, walk · pay attention to ears, hands and feet · *listen, do, walk*
389. **Tzav: seven days at the door (Leviticus 8:33–35)** · — · the priests wait seven days at the entrance before serving · wait before starting something big · *wait at the door*

### Shemini (Leviticus 9:1–11:47)
390. **Shemini: the eighth day (Leviticus 9:1)** · — · after seven days of preparation, the service begins on the eighth · begin something you prepared for · *the eighth day begins*
391. **Shemini: strange fire (Leviticus 10:1–2)** · — · Nadav and Avihu bring fire not commanded and die; commentators still ask why · sit with a story that has no easy answer · *zeal without listening*
392. **Shemini: Aaron's silence (Leviticus 10:3)** · — · after his sons die, "Aaron held his peace" · sit silently with someone grieving · *and aaron held his peace*
393. **Shemini: grief has its reasons (Leviticus 10:16–20)** · — · Aaron doesn't eat the offering in his grief; Moses hears him out and agrees · let someone grieve their own way · *moses heard, and agreed*
394. **Shemini: be holy (Leviticus 11:44–45)** · kadosh · the food laws end with a call: "be holy, for I am holy" · eat one meal with full attention · *holy in the everyday*

### Tazria (Leviticus 12:1–13:59)
395. **Tazria: after a birth (Leviticus 12)** · — · a new mother's time apart and her offering mark a life-changing passage · honour a new parent · *birth changes everything*
396. **Tazria: the skin affliction (Leviticus 13)** · tzara'at · not leprosy as we know it; the rabbis linked it to harmful speech · watch your words for a day · *words show on the skin*
397. **Tazria: the priest must look (Leviticus 13:2–3)** · — · the priest examines in person, never from a distance · look carefully before judging · *look, don't assume*
398. **Tazria: wait seven days (Leviticus 13:4–6)** · — · when unsure, the priest waits a week and looks again · wait before one decision · *look again in a week*
399. **Tazria: pray for the one outside (Leviticus 13:45–46)** · — · the afflicted cries "unclean"; the Talmud says so that others will pray for him (Shabbat 67a) · pray for someone isolated · *pray for the one outside*

### Metzora (Leviticus 14:1–15:33)
400. **Metzora: the priest goes out (Leviticus 14:3)** · — · the priest goes outside the camp to the person, not the other way round · go to someone who can't come to you · *go out to them*
401. **Metzora: cedar and hyssop (Leviticus 14:4)** · — · the tall cedar and the low hyssop; Rashi reads them as pride brought low · lower yourself one notch · *from cedar to hyssop*
402. **Metzora: the bird set free (Leviticus 14:7)** · — · one bird is let go over the open field · let one thing go · *let the bird go*
403. **Metzora: empty the house first (Leviticus 14:33–36)** · — · even walls can be afflicted; the house is emptied first to spare the owner's things · clear out one corner · *empty it first*
404. **Metzora: coming back in (Leviticus 14:8–9)** · — · washed and renewed, the person returns to the camp · welcome someone back · *there is a way back*

### Acharei Mot (Leviticus 16:1–18:30)
405. **Acharei Mot: after the deaths (Leviticus 16:1)** · — · the Yom Kippur service is given right after Aaron's loss · let one grief teach you · *after grief, atonement*
406. **Acharei Mot: not at all times (Leviticus 16:2)** · — · even the high priest may enter the holiest place only once a year · honour one limit · *not at all times*
407. **Acharei Mot: the goat to Azazel (Leviticus 16:21–22)** · Azazel · wrongs are confessed over a goat sent into the wilderness · write one wrong down and tear it up · *send it away*
408. **Acharei Mot: ask the person first (Mishnah Yoma 8:9)** · — · Yom Kippur atones between people and God, but wrongs between people need their forgiveness · apologise to someone directly · *ask the person first*
409. **Acharei Mot: live by them (Leviticus 18:5)** · pikuach nefesh · "which if a man do, he shall live by them"; so saving a life overrides almost every law (Yoma 85b) · look after your health today · *live by them*

### Kedoshim (Leviticus 19:1–20:27)
410. **Kedoshim: holiness for everyone (Leviticus 19:2)** · — · holiness is spoken to the whole congregation, not only to priests · do one ordinary thing in a holy way · *holiness for everyone*
411. **Kedoshim: the corners of the field (Leviticus 19:9–10)** · pe'ah · leave the field's edges and fallen grapes for the poor and the stranger · leave something for someone else · *leave the corners*
412. **Kedoshim: no stumbling block (Leviticus 19:14)** · — · don't put a stumbling block before the blind; the rabbis read it as never misleading anyone · give one piece of honest advice · *no stumbling blocks*
413. **Kedoshim: don't stand idly by (Leviticus 19:16)** · — · "neither shalt thou stand idly by the blood of thy neighbour" · step in once instead of watching · *don't stand idly by*
414. **Kedoshim: as yourself (Leviticus 19:18)** · — · "thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself"; Rabbi Akiva called it the great principle of the Torah · do one loving thing for a neighbour · *as yourself*

### Emor (Leviticus 21:1–24:23)
415. **Emor: say it twice (Leviticus 21:1)** · — · "speak" and "say" are doubled; Rashi reads it as elders teaching the young · teach one small thing · *the old teach the young*
416. **Emor: appointed times (Leviticus 23)** · mo'adim · Shabbat, Passover, the Omer, Shavuot, the new year, Yom Kippur, Sukkot, all in one chapter · mark the next holiday on your calendar · *appointed times*
417. **Emor: counting fifty days (Leviticus 23:15–16)** · — · from barley harvest to wheat, seven weeks counted day by day · count one day toward something · *count toward something*
418. **Emor: the poor at the feast (Leviticus 23:22)** · — · in the middle of the festivals, a reminder to leave gleanings for the poor · give during a celebration · *remember them at the feast*
419. **Emor: one law (Leviticus 24:22)** · — · "Ye shall have one manner of law, as well for the stranger, as for the home-born" · treat an outsider as an equal · *one law for all*

### Behar (Leviticus 25:1–26:2)
420. **Behar: the land rests (Leviticus 25:1–7)** · shmita · every seventh year the land lies fallow and its produce is open to all · let one thing lie fallow · *the land rests too*
421. **Behar: proclaim liberty (Leviticus 25:8–10)** · yovel · in the fiftieth year, "proclaim liberty throughout the land" · free someone from an old debt · *proclaim liberty*
422. **Behar: strangers and settlers (Leviticus 25:23)** · — · the land is God's, "for ye are strangers and settlers with Me" · hold one possession loosely · *we are guests here*
423. **Behar: wronging with words (Leviticus 25:17)** · ona'at devarim · the rabbis read "ye shall not wrong one another" as a ban on hurtful words (Bava Metzia 58b) · catch one hurtful remark before it's said · *words can wrong*
424. **Behar: hold him before he falls (Leviticus 25:35)** · — · support a struggling neighbour before he falls; Maimonides calls this the highest giving · help someone before they fall · *hold him before he falls*

### Bechukotai (Leviticus 26:3–27:34)
425. **Bechukotai: if ye walk (Leviticus 26:3)** · — · the blessings begin with walking in God's ways · take a walk and think about your path · *walk in them*
426. **Bechukotai: I will walk among you (Leviticus 26:12)** · — · the promise is closeness: "I will walk among you" · look for God in your own street · *walking among you*
427. **Bechukotai: hard words, softly** · tochacha · the chapter of warnings is traditionally read quickly and quietly in synagogue · read something difficult gently · *hard words, softly*
428. **Bechukotai: still remembered (Leviticus 26:42–45)** · — · even after the warnings, God remembers the covenant with Jacob, Isaac and Abraham · recall a promise once made to you · *still remembered*
429. **Leviticus close: chazak** · — · the book of holiness ends and the synagogue says chazak · say chazak for finishing Leviticus · *be strong*

## Block C · Numbers, the wilderness (Days 430–480)
430. **In the wilderness** · Bamidbar · the book's Hebrew name means "in the wilderness", where the Torah was given in no one's land · spend a minute somewhere empty · *the wilderness teaches*

### Bamidbar (Numbers 1:1–4:20)
431. **Bamidbar: counted by name (Numbers 1:2)** · — · a census "by their polls", each person counted, not just a total · learn a name you should know · *count every head*
432. **Bamidbar: banners around the centre (Numbers 2)** · — · each tribe camps under its own banner, all facing the sanctuary · honour your banner and other people's · *everyone around the centre*
433. **Bamidbar: set apart to serve (Numbers 3:12)** · Levi · the tribe of Levi is set apart to serve the sanctuary · notice someone who serves quietly · *set apart to serve*
434. **Bamidbar: teachers are parents (Numbers 3:1)** · — · Aaron's sons are called Moses' too; whoever teaches a child is like a parent (Sanhedrin 19b) · thank someone who taught a child you love · *teachers are parents too*
435. **Bamidbar: wrap the holy things (Numbers 4:5–20)** · — · the holiest objects are carefully covered before travel · protect something precious · *cover what's holy*

### Naso (Numbers 4:21–7:89)
436. **Naso: lift up the heads (Numbers 4:22)** · — · the census phrase means both "count" and "lift up" · lift one person's spirit · *counting is lifting*

438. **Naso: the nazirite (Numbers 6:1–21)** · nazir · a person may vow to give up wine and haircuts for a season · give up one small thing for a week · *a vow for a season*
439. **Naso: the priestly blessing (Numbers 6:24–26)** · Birkat Kohanim · "The LORD bless thee, and keep thee", still said over children and congregations · bless someone with these words · *bless thee, and keep thee*
440. **Naso: the same gift twelve times (Numbers 7)** · — · each tribal leader brings an identical gift, and each is listed in full · honour the same good act done by many · *each gift counted in full*

### Beha'alotcha (Numbers 8:1–12:16)
441. **Beha'alotcha: until it rises alone (Numbers 8:2)** · — · Rashi: Aaron holds the light to the wick until the flame rises by itself · help someone catch their own flame · *until it rises alone*
442. **Beha'alotcha: a second Passover (Numbers 9:6–14)** · Pesach Sheni · people who missed Passover ask for a chance, and one is given · give someone a second chance · *second chances exist*
443. **Beha'alotcha: we remember the fish (Numbers 11:4–6)** · — · the people crave Egypt's fish and cucumbers, forgetting the whip · notice nostalgia for a bad place · *egypt wasn't free*
444. **Beha'alotcha: seventy elders (Numbers 11:16–29)** · — · Moses shares the load and says "would that all the LORD's people were prophets" · share one burden and one credit · *would all were prophets*
445. **Beha'alotcha: five words (Numbers 12:13)** · — · Moses' prayer for Miriam is five Hebrew words: heal her now, O God · pray for someone in five words · *short prayers count*

### Shelach (Numbers 13:1–15:41)
446. **Shelach: go and see (Numbers 13:1–2)** · — · twelve scouts are sent to see the land · go and look for yourself · *go and see*
447. **Shelach: as grasshoppers (Numbers 13:33)** · — · "we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight" · notice how you see yourself · *how you see yourself shows*
448. **Shelach: another spirit (Numbers 14:24)** · — · Caleb "had another spirit with him" and trusted they could go up · be the one who says we can · *a different spirit*
449. **Shelach: forty years (Numbers 14:33–34)** · — · a year for each day of scouting; a new generation will enter · accept one slow road · *change takes a generation*
450. **Shelach: the fringes (Numbers 15:37–41)** · tzitzit · fringes on the corners of garments, to look upon and remember · tie a knot to remember something · *see and remember*

### Korach (Numbers 16:1–18:32)
451. **Korach: sounds fair (Numbers 16:3)** · — · Korach says all the congregation is holy; the words are true, the motive is not · check your motive in one argument · *what's under the argument*
452. **Korach: not for heaven's sake (Avot 5:17)** · — · the Mishnah names Korach's quarrel as the dispute not for heaven's sake · argue for truth, not for winning · *not for heaven's sake*
453. **Korach: Moses falls on his face (Numbers 16:4)** · — · attacked, Moses first falls on his face before answering · pause before replying to an attack · *fall silent first*
454. **Korach: the man who went home (Sanhedrin 109b)** · — · On ben Peleth vanishes from the rebellion; the Talmud says his wife talked him out of it · let someone talk you down · *she saved him*
455. **Korach: the staff that blossomed (Numbers 17:16–24)** · — · Aaron's staff sprouts blossoms and ripe almonds overnight · look for something budding · *the staff blossoms*

### Chukat (Numbers 19:1–22:1)
456. **Chukat: the red heifer (Numbers 19)** · chok · a ritual that purifies the impure and defiles the pure; tradition says even Solomon couldn't explain it · accept one thing you don't understand · *not everything explains itself*
457. **Chukat: Miriam's well (Numbers 20:1–2)** · — · Miriam dies and the water stops; the Talmud says a well went with them for her sake (Taanit 9a) · thank someone who quietly sustained you · *miriam's well*
458. **Chukat: striking the rock (Numbers 20:7–12)** · — · told to speak to the rock, Moses strikes it, and will not enter the land · speak instead of striking · *speak to the rock*
459. **Chukat: Aaron dies (Numbers 20:22–29)** · — · all the house of Israel weeps thirty days for the man who pursued peace · remember a peacemaker · *all the house wept*
460. **Chukat: look up (Numbers 21:4–9)** · — · looking at the copper serpent heals; the Mishnah says it was turning hearts toward heaven (Rosh Hashanah 3:8) · look up for one minute · *look up and live*

### Balak (Numbers 22:2–25:9)
461. **Balak: a hired curse (Numbers 22:2–6)** · — · the king of Moab pays the prophet Balaam to curse Israel · notice who's paying for an opinion · *who's paying*
462. **Balak: the donkey saw first (Numbers 22:21–35)** · — · the donkey sees the angel before the famous prophet does · listen to a humble voice · *the donkey saw first*
463. **Balak: only the word I'm given (Numbers 22:38)** · — · Balaam can speak only the word God puts in his mouth · say only what's true today · *only the true word*
464. **Balak: how goodly are thy tents (Numbers 24:5)** · Mah Tovu · the curse becomes a blessing, and his words now open the morning service · say Mah Tovu entering a room · *how goodly are thy tents*
465. **Balak: dwelling alone (Numbers 23:9)** · — · Balaam sees "a people that shall dwell alone" · appreciate one way you're different · *alone isn't lonely*

### Pinchas (Numbers 25:10–30:1)
466. **Pinchas: a covenant of peace (Numbers 25:12)** · — · after violent zeal, a covenant of peace, and the word "peace" is written with a broken letter · temper zeal with peace · *peace is written broken*
467. **Pinchas: the five sisters (Numbers 27:1–11)** · — · Zelophehad's daughters ask to inherit; God tells Moses they speak right · ask for what's fair · *the daughters speak right*
468. **Pinchas: a shepherd for the flock (Numbers 27:15–17)** · — · Moses asks for a successor so the people won't be sheep without a shepherd · prepare someone to follow you · *who comes next*
469. **Pinchas: some of thy honour (Numbers 27:18–23)** · — · Moses lays hands on Joshua and puts "of thy honour upon him" · share your standing with a newcomer · *hands on the next one*
470. **Pinchas: morning and evening (Numbers 28:1–8)** · tamid · a daily offering at morning and evening, the pattern for daily prayer · keep one morning and one evening habit · *morning and evening*
