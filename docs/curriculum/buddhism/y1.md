# CAMP 2 · THE STORIES (Days 22–96)
*The Buddha's life as a serial, told from the palace to the sal trees; then the Jataka as shorts. Hook is the story beat; practice stays two minutes; a word every two or three days.*

## Week 4 · Before the palace gates (Days 22–28)
22. **The white elephant dream** · — · Queen Maya dreams a white elephant enters her side; the wise men say a great son is coming · recall a dream that stayed with you · *some things announce themselves*
23. **Born in a grove** · Lumbini · on the road to her parents' home, Maya gives birth holding a branch in the Lumbini garden · stand under a tree for one minute · *born on the way somewhere*
24. **The old sage weeps** · — · the sage Asita holds the baby, smiles, then weeps: he will not live to hear this child teach · think of a good thing you won't see finished · *plant it anyway*
25. **Seven days** · — · Maya dies a week after the birth; her sister Mahapajapati raises him as her own · thank someone who raised you, silently · *love can be adopted*
26. **The one whose aim succeeds** · Siddhattha · the naming; the brahmins read the marks: a great king or a great teacher · say your own full name slowly · *a name is a wish*
27. **The rose-apple tree** · jhana · at the ploughing festival the boy sits in the shade and falls into a quiet joy (MN 36) · sit in shade, doing nothing, two minutes · *you've been here before*
28. **The swan** · — · a later telling: cousin Devadatta shoots a swan, Siddhattha tends it, and the court says it belongs to the one who saved it · care for one small living thing · *life belongs to who protects it*

## Week 5 · The palace (Days 29–35)
29. **Three palaces** · — · one for the rains, one for winter, one for summer; "I was delicately nurtured" (AN 3.38) · notice one comfort you forgot you had · *comfort can be a wall*
30. **Yasodhara** · — · the marriage; in the stories she is his equal, and later a nun in her own right · honor someone who is your equal · *partner, not prize*
31. **The first sight: an old man** · — · on a chariot ride with Channa he sees a man bent with age and asks, will this happen to me · look kindly at an old face today · *this is coming for all of us*
32. **The second sight: a sick man** · — · a man shaking with fever by the road; and this too, he asks · sit with one small ache in your body · *the body is borrowed*
33. **The third sight: a dead man** · — · a funeral procession, the mourners weeping; the prince goes quiet · name one person you have lost · *everyone we love will go*
34. **The fourth sight: a wanderer** · samana · a shaven man in a robe, owning nothing, calm · find one calm face today · *there is another way to live*
35. **A son is born** · Rahula · news of his son arrives; the child is named Rahula, "a fetter" · hold something you love without gripping · *love, and the fear inside it*

## Week 6 · The going forth (Days 36–42)
36. **The sleeping palace** · — · he wakes at night to see the musicians asleep and sprawled; the glamour is gone · see a familiar room with fresh eyes · *the spell breaks*
37. **One look back** · — · he looks in on Yasodhara and the baby and does not wake them · think of a goodbye you couldn't say · *the hardest leaving is quiet*
38. **Channa and Kanthaka** · — · the charioteer and the white horse ride out at midnight through the city gate · thank someone who got you somewhere · *someone rode with you*
39. **The cut hair** · pabbajja · at the river Anoma he cuts his hair with his sword and puts on a simple robe · let one small vanity go today · *less to carry*
40. **Kanthaka's heart** · — · a later telling: the horse, sent home without him, dies of grief · sit with an animal's love · *they grieve too*
41. **The king's offer** · — · in Rajagaha King Bimbisara offers him wealth; he declines and promises to return if he finds the way (Sn 3.1) · decline one thing kindly · *a promise to come back*
42. **Two teachers** · — · he masters the deep states taught by Alara Kalama and Uddaka Ramaputta, and leaves: good, but not the end (MN 26) · learn one thing, then ask what's next · *good, but not the end*

## Week 7 · The ascetics (Days 43–49)
43. **Five companions** · — · Kondañña and four others join him in the forest near Uruvela · name five people who walk with you · *you don't go alone*
44. **Holding the breath** · — · he tries stopping the breath; his head roars like a blacksmith's bellows (MN 36) · breathe easily, three long breaths · *forcing isn't finding*
45. **A handful of beans** · — · food shrinks to almost nothing; his spine shows through his belly like a string of beads (MN 36) · eat one meal slowly · *starving isn't holy*
46. **Six years** · — · years of hard austerity; some think he has died · rest when you are tired today · *harder isn't always better*
47. **Remembering the rose-apple tree** · — · he recalls the boyhood joy under the tree: could that be the way (MN 36) · recall a moment of easy calm · *the joy was a clue*
48. **Sujata's bowl of rice** · — · a village woman offers milk-rice; he eats, and the five leave him in disgust · eat something with thanks · *feed the one who walks*
49. **The bowl upstream** · — · a later telling: he sets the bowl on the river; if it floats upstream he will awaken, and it does · set down one hope gently · *against the current*

## Week 8 · The night under the tree (Days 50–56)
50. **Eight handfuls of grass** · — · a grass-cutter gives him grass; he makes a seat beneath the tree, facing east · make a seat and sit · *a seat is a decision*
51. **"I will not rise"** · bodhi · he resolves not to move until he awakens · commit to five minutes, fully · *stay put*
52. **Mara's army** · Mara · Mara comes with storms and armies, then his daughters; the arrows fall as flowers · name what attacks your quiet · *the arrows become flowers*
53. **Touching the earth** · — · Mara asks who witnesses his right to that seat; he touches the ground, and the earth answers · touch the ground with your hand · *the earth is your witness*
54. **The three watches** · — · first watch, his past lives; second, beings passing by their deeds; third, the ending of the taints (MN 36) · sit three slow breaths, watching · *night has watches*
55. **Dawn, awake** · Buddha · the morning star rises; he is the Buddha, the one who woke up · watch a sunrise, or picture one · *awake*
56. **The house-builder** · — · craving named and seen; the rafters broken, the ridgepole shattered (Dhammapada 153–154) · name one habit you have seen through · *house-builder, you are seen*

## Week 9 · Setting the wheel turning (Days 57–63)
57. **Seven weeks** · — · he stays near the tree; in a storm the serpent Mucalinda coils around him as shelter · sit somewhere sheltered from weather · *sheltered*
58. **Two merchants** · — · Tapussa and Bhallika offer honey-cakes and become the first lay followers · give a small snack to someone · *the first gift*
59. **Should he teach?** · — · he hesitates; Brahma Sahampati asks him to teach; he sees people like lotuses near the surface (MN 26) · picture a lotus rising · *some are ready*
60. **The man on the road** · — · Upaka the wanderer hears him, shakes his head, and takes another road (MN 26) · let someone disagree with you today · *not everyone is ready*
61. **The Deer Park** · — · the five agree to ignore him, then rise to greet him as he comes · welcome someone you had written off · *they stood up*
62. **The first sermon** · Dhammacakka · the middle way, the four truths, the eightfold path, at Isipatana (SN 56.11) · say the four truths in your words · *the wheel turns*
63. **Kondañña sees** · — · whatever has the nature to arise has the nature to cease; Kondañña understands (SN 56.11) · watch one thing begin and end · *it arises, it ends*

## Week 10 · The sangha grows (Days 64–70)
64. **Not-self, taught** · — · to the five: is the body you, is feeling, is any of the five heaps mine (SN 22.59) · ask of one feeling, is this me · *not mine*
65. **Yasa's night** · — · a rich young man wakes after a party, sickened, and walks out into the night (Vinaya, Mahavagga 1) · leave one thing that has lost its shine · *walk out of the party*
66. **Sixty sent out** · — · wander for the good of the many, for the happiness of the many (Vinaya, Mahavagga 1.11) · do one good thing for a stranger · *for the good of many*
67. **The fire sermon** · — · to a thousand fire-worshipers: all is burning, with greed, hate and delusion (SN 35.28) · notice where you are burning · *all is burning*
68. **Sariputta and Moggallana** · — · Assaji's short verse: things arise from a cause, and the Buddha tells their cause; Sariputta understands at once · pass on one good line · *one verse was enough*
69. **Coming home** · — · he returns to Kapilavatthu; young Rahula asks for his inheritance and is given the teaching · give someone something that lasts · *the real inheritance*
70. **The women ask** · bhikkhuni · Mahapajapati and many women shave their heads and walk to ask for ordination; Ananda speaks for them · honor a woman who had to ask twice · *they walked barefoot to ask*

## Week 11 · Hard stories (Days 71–77)
71. **The man with the finger garland** · — · Angulimala, a killer on the forest road, sees the Buddha walking alone toward him (MN 86) · walk calmly past something you fear · *walk on*
72. **"I have stopped. You stop."** · — · he runs and cannot catch the Buddha, who walks slowly; the sword drops (MN 86) · stop one harmful habit for today · *I have stopped*
73. **The monk who was stoned** · — · Angulimala, now a monk, is hit with stones in town; told to bear it; his blessing eases a woman's birth (MN 86) · wish ease on someone in labor of any kind · *born into a new life*
74. **Kisa Gotami's child** · — · a young mother carries her dead son from house to house, asking for medicine · sit quietly with someone's grief · *she carried him everywhere*
75. **The mustard seed** · — · bring a mustard seed from a house where no one has died; there is no such house · think how many share your loss · *no house is untouched*
76. **Patacara** · — · she loses husband, children and parents in one day and runs wild; the Buddha says, sister, recover your mind · steady your breath when overwhelmed · *recover your mind*
77. **The wild elephant** · — · Devadatta sets the elephant Nalagiri loose; the Buddha meets it with metta and it kneels (Vinaya, Cullavagga 7) · meet one flash of anger with calm · *the elephant kneels*

## Week 12 · The last days (Days 78–84)
78. **Eighty years** · — · his body like an old cart kept going with straps (DN 16) · stretch gently and thank your body · *the old cart still rolls*
79. **Be an island** · — · to Ananda: be islands to yourselves, a refuge to yourselves, the dhamma your refuge (DN 16) · decide one thing yourself today · *be your own island*
80. **Vesali is lovely** · — · he looks back at the city turning his whole body, like an elephant, and says how delightful it was (DN 16) · look back fondly at one place · *how lovely it was*
81. **Cunda's meal** · — · the smith Cunda serves a last meal; the Buddha falls ill and makes sure Cunda is not blamed (DN 16) · protect someone from blame · *no one is to blame*
82. **Between two sal trees** · — · at Kusinara he lies down on his right side; the trees blossom out of season (DN 16) · lie down for one mindful minute · *rest is part of the path*
83. **The last disciple** · — · the wanderer Subhadda is turned away at the door; the Buddha calls him in (DN 16) · make room for one late arrival · *there's still time*
84. **The last words** · parinibbana · "decay is inherent in all component things; work out your salvation with diligence" (DN 16, Rhys Davids) · sit in silence, three minutes · *strive on with care*

## Week 13 · The Jataka as shorts (Days 85–91)
85. **The hare in the moon** · — · a hare with nothing to give offers himself to a hungry guest; his image is set on the moon (Jataka 316) · look for the hare in tonight's moon · *give what you have*
86. **The banyan deer** · — · a deer king takes the place of a pregnant doe; the hunting king is moved to stop the hunt (Jataka 12) · take someone's hard turn today · *stand in for someone*
87. **The talkative tortoise** · — · two geese carry a tortoise on a stick; he opens his mouth to answer the jeers and falls (Jataka 215) · let one retort go unsaid · *not every word needs saying*
88. **The quarrelling quails** · — · the quails lift the net together and fly off, until they start to bicker (Jataka 33) · cooperate on one small thing · *together we lift the net*
89. **The monkey and the crocodile** · — · caught mid-river, the monkey says he left his heart up in the fig tree (Jataka 208) · use wit instead of force once · *keep your wits*
90. **The golden goose** · — · a golden bird gives one feather at a time; the greedy wife plucks it bare and the gold turns ordinary (Jataka 136) · take only what you need today · *greed plucks the goose bare*
91. **The ruru deer** · — · a golden deer saves a drowning man, who later betrays him to the king (Jataka 482) · do good without needing thanks · *do it anyway*

## Week 14 · The long Jataka and the close (Days 92–96)
92. **Vessantara gives** · — · the generous prince gives away the kingdom's rain-bringing white elephant and is exiled (Jataka 547) · give one thing you'd rather keep · *generosity costs*
93. **Vessantara's children** · — · he gives away even his children before the reunion; Buddhists have debated this story for centuries · sit with a story that troubles you · *the tradition argues too*
94. **Temiya the silent prince** · — · a prince pretends to be mute for sixteen years rather than rule by punishment (Jataka 538) · keep silent for ten minutes · *silence can be a choice*
95. **Mahajanaka's swim** · — · shipwrecked, he swims seven days without seeing land, because effort is never wasted (Jataka 539) · keep going on one thing · *swim anyway*
96. **Camp 2 close: the whole life** · — · from the palace to the sal trees, from the hare to the prince: stories that teach by being remembered · retell one story to someone · *you know him now*

# CAMP 3 · THE PRACTICES (Days 97–156)
*How-to. The hook is short; the practice is the session. Cumulative: by the close there is a morning, an evening, a week and a year.*

## Weeks 15–16 · Metta in full (Days 97–106)
97. **The four phrases** · — · may you be safe, may you be happy, may you be healthy, may you live with ease · say the phrases once, slowly · *may you be at ease*
98. **Starting with yourself** · — · the old manuals begin metta with oneself, the easiest proof that everyone wants to be happy (Visuddhimagga 9) · offer the phrases to yourself · *you count too*
99. **The benefactor** · — · someone who helped you; the easiest door for most people · send metta to one helper · *the door is open*
100. **The dear friend** · — · a friend, not a romantic partner, so the wish stays clean · send metta to one friend · *friendship, widened*
101. **The neutral person** · — · the cashier, the neighbor, someone you barely notice · send metta to a stranger · *they have a whole life*
102. **The difficult person** · — · begin with someone mildly difficult, never the hardest · send metta for one breath · *a little, honestly*
103. **Breaking the barriers** · — · the manual's test: bandits demand one of four people; metta hands over none (Visuddhimagga 9) · hold four people equally · *no one handed over*
104. **All beings** · — · east, west, north, south, above, below; seen and unseen · send metta in six directions · *everywhere*
105. **The metta sutta** · Karaniya · the discourse on kindness: as a mother protects her only child (Sn 1.8) · read the sutta aloud · *as a mother*
106. **The eleven blessings** · — · sleep well, wake well, no bad dreams, dear to people and to animals (AN 11.16) · send metta before sleep · *kindness sleeps well*

## Weeks 16–17 · The breath, deeper (Days 107–116)
107. **Sixteen steps** · anapanasati · the breathing discourse gives sixteen steps in four groups of four (MN 118) · sit five minutes with the breath · *sixteen steps, one breath*
108. **Long and short** · — · steps one and two: a long breath known as long, a short breath as short (MN 118) · five minutes, just knowing · *just know it*
109. **The whole body** · — · steps three and four: sensing the whole body, calming it (MN 118) · five minutes, body settling · *the body settles*
110. **Joy and ease** · piti · steps five and six: breathing with gladness, breathing with ease (MN 118) · five minutes, allowing pleasure · *you're allowed to enjoy it*
111. **Feeling the mind** · — · steps seven and eight: knowing the mind's activity and calming it (MN 118) · five minutes, noticing moods · *calm the weather*
112. **Gladdening the mind** · — · steps nine to twelve: the mind known, gladdened, steadied, set free (MN 118) · five minutes, mind-watching · *release*
113. **Watching change** · — · steps thirteen to sixteen: impermanence, fading, ceasing, letting go (MN 118) · five minutes, watching endings · *let go*
114. **Counting** · — · an old method: count breaths from one to ten, then begin again · count ten breaths, three rounds · *back to one*
115. **The hindrances arrive** · nivarana · desire, ill will, dullness, restlessness, doubt: five visitors every sitter meets · name the one visiting now · *name the visitor*
116. **Your sitting** · — · bell, posture, breath, a closing wish of metta · ten minutes · *ten minutes, yours*

## Week 17 · Walking meditation (Days 117–122)
117. **The lane** · cankama · monks walk a straight path of twenty or thirty paces, back and forth · pace fifteen steps and back · *nowhere to get to*
118. **Lifting, moving, placing** · — · three parts of a step, named softly · walk, naming three parts · *lift, move, place*
119. **The turn** · — · the turn at the end of the lane is its own practice · turn slowly, feeling it · *the turn counts too*
120. **Five blessings of walking** · — · stamina for travel, for effort, good health, good digestion, lasting concentration (AN 5.29) · walk slowly after a meal · *walking steadies*
121. **Walking in the city** · — · the walk to the bus can be meditation too · feel your feet on a real walk · *every step, here*
122. **Walking and sitting together** · — · retreats alternate: sit, walk, sit · sit five minutes, walk five · *two ways to stay*

## Week 18 · The refuges and the precepts (Days 123–130)
123. **The three refuges** · tisarana · the Buddha, the dhamma, the sangha: the three jewels people take refuge in · say the three refuges · *three places to rest*
124. **Not killing** · — · precept one: I undertake the training to refrain from taking life · let one insect live · *let it live*
125. **Not taking** · — · precept two: refrain from taking what is not given · return something borrowed · *only what's given*
126. **Sexual conduct** · — · precept three: refrain from sexual misconduct; harm, not shame, is the measure · keep one promise to someone close · *no harm in love*
127. **Speech** · — · precept four: refrain from false speech · tell one small truth · *say what's true*
128. **A clear mind** · — · precept five: refrain from drink and drugs that cloud the mind · notice what clouds you · *keep the mind clear*
129. **Uposatha** · uposatha · on moon days lay people may keep eight precepts for a day and a night (AN 8.41) · find the next full moon · *a day kept apart*
130. **When you break one** · — · the precepts are trainings, taken again and again; nobody keeps them perfectly · recommit to one precept · *begin again*

## Week 19 · Dana (Days 131–137)
131. **Giving first** · — · in the gradual talk the Buddha taught giving before anything else · give something small, first thing · *giving opens the door*
132. **The alms round** · pindapata · monks walk at dawn with bowls; lay people give food · picture an alms round, or watch one · *the bowl is open*
133. **Glad three times** · — · glad before giving, glad while giving, glad after (AN 6.37) · notice all three moments once · *glad three times*
134. **If people knew** · — · if beings knew the fruit of giving, they would not eat without sharing (Itivuttaka 26) · share part of a meal · *share the last bite*
135. **Giving attention** · — · the most common gift is simply listening · give someone full attention · *attention is a gift*
136. **The poor woman's lamp** · — · a Mahayana story: a beggar's cheap lamp burns all night after the king's lamps go out · give small, wholeheartedly · *small and wholehearted*
137. **Giving without a receipt** · — · give, and let it go; don't keep score · give anonymously once · *no receipt*

## Week 20 · The chant and the shrine (Days 138–144)
138. **Homage** · namo tassa · homage to the Blessed One, the worthy one, the fully awakened: the opening chant · chant it three times · *homage*
139. **The home shrine** · — · an image, a candle, incense, a flower, water: a small corner · clear a small space · *a quiet corner*
140. **Three bows** · — · one for the Buddha, one for the dhamma, one for the sangha; bowing as setting pride down · bow three times · *bow*
141. **Flowers that fade** · — · a traditional offering verse: these flowers fade, and so will this body · offer one flower · *fresh today, gone tomorrow*
142. **Candle and incense** · — · light for wisdom, scent for good conduct spreading · light a candle · *light and scent*
143. **Sharing the merit** · — · ending practice by dedicating whatever good was done to all beings · dedicate today's sitting · *may it be shared*
144. **Your morning** · — · bow, homage, breath, metta, dedication · ten minutes · *a morning, yours*

## Week 21 · The holy days (Days 145–150)
145. **Vesak** · Vesak · the May full moon: in Theravada lands, birth, awakening and passing remembered on one day · light a lamp tonight · *born, awake, gone*
146. **Bathing the baby Buddha** · Hanamatsuri · in Japan and elsewhere in East Asia the birth is kept in April by pouring sweet tea over a small standing Buddha · pour water gently over something · *a clean beginning*
147. **Lanterns** · — · Vesak lanterns in Sri Lanka, lotus lanterns in Korea: light for the many · make or picture a lantern · *light for the many*
148. **Keeping the day** · — · on Vesak many keep eight precepts, give food, visit temples · keep one extra precept today · *keep the day*
149. **Asalha Puja** · — · the July full moon: the first sermon remembered · reread the four truths · *the wheel still turns*
150. **Magha Puja** · — · in Thailand, Laos and Cambodia, the February or March full moon: 1,250 disciples gather unannounced · practice with someone today · *they came without being called*

## Week 22 · The retreat, explained (Days 151–156)
151. **The rains retreat** · Vassa · for three months of monsoon monks stay in one place; lay people take on a practice · choose one three-month practice · *stay in one place*
152. **What a retreat is** · — · days of silence, sitting, walking, simple food, one teacher · picture a day of silence · *less input, more seeing*
153. **Noble silence** · — · no talking, even no eye contact, so attention turns inward · stay silent for one hour · *silence, on purpose*
154. **A day of mindfulness at home** · — · a retreat you can keep on a free day: phone away, sit, walk, eat slowly · plan one quiet half-day · *a retreat at home*
155. **Kathina** · Kathina · at the end of the rains lay people offer cloth for new robes · give something useful to someone · *the rains end in gifts*
156. **Camp 3 close: your practice** · — · a morning bow, a sitting, a walk, metta, the precepts, a gift, the holy days · write down your week · *you can do this*

# CAMP 4 · THE TEXT: THE DHAMMAPADA (Days 157–231)
*All 26 chapters and 423 verses, in order, in Max Müller's translation. Read, understand, sit, carry: one verse carried each day.*

## Opening (Day 157)
157. **Opening the Dhammapada** · Dhammapada · 423 verses in 26 chapters, "the path of dhamma"; the best-loved book of the Buddha's sayings · read the list of chapter titles · *the path, in verses*

## Chapter 1 · The twin verses (Days 158–161)
158. **The mind comes first (1–2)** · — · all that we are is the result of what we have thought; pain follows an evil thought as the wheel follows the ox · notice one thought steering your mood · *the wheel follows the ox*
159. **"He abused me" (3–6)** · — · hatred never ceases by hatred, only by not-hatred; this is an old rule (Dhammapada 5) · drop one old grudge thought · *hatred ends by not-hatred*
160. **The wind and the rock (7–14)** · — · Mara overthrows the careless like wind a weak tree, not the steady; rain gets through a badly thatched roof · find one weak spot in your roof · *thatch the roof*
161. **Other people's cows (15–20)** · — · one who recites much but doesn't act is like a cowherd counting others' cows (Dhammapada 19) · live one verse instead of reading ten · *not someone else's cows*

## Chapter 2 · Earnestness (Days 162–164)
162. **The path of the deathless (21–23)** · appamada · earnestness is the path to the deathless; the careless are as if dead already · do one task with full attention · *heedful is alive*
163. **An island (24–28)** · — · by effort and restraint the wise make an island no flood can overwhelm · build one small daily habit · *make an island*
164. **The racehorse (29–32)** · — · awake among the sleeping, the wise move on like a racehorse leaving a nag behind · get up a little early once · *awake among the sleeping*

## Chapter 3 · Thought (Days 165–167)
165. **The fletcher (33–34)** · citta · the wise straighten the trembling mind as a fletcher straightens an arrow; the mind flaps like a fish on dry land · straighten one thought · *straighten the arrow*
166. **Guard the mind (35–39)** · — · hard to see, subtle, going where it likes: a guarded mind brings happiness · notice where the mind wanders · *guard the mind*
167. **The body like a jar (40–43)** · — · the body is fragile as a jar; a well-directed mind does more good than mother or father (Dhammapada 43) · hold a cup, knowing it can break · *more than a mother can*

## Chapter 4 · Flowers (Days 168–170)
168. **The garland-maker (44–48)** · — · who will pick the well-taught path as a skilled hand picks flowers; death carries off the one still gathering · pick one teaching for today · *pick the flower*
169. **The bee (49–52)** · — · as the bee takes nectar and leaves the flower unharmed; look to your own faults, not others' · take without harming today · *the bee leaves the flower*
170. **The lotus on the rubbish heap (53–59)** · — · the scent of virtue travels against the wind; a lotus grows on a heap by the road · find beauty in a rough place · *the lotus on the heap*

## Chapter 5 · The fool (Days 171–173)
171. **Long is the night (60–63)** · — · long is the night to the sleepless; the fool who knows he is a fool is wise so far · admit one thing you don't know · *wise so far*
172. **The spoon and the soup (64–68)** · — · a fool beside a wise person all his life knows the truth no more than a spoon tastes the soup · taste one conversation fully · *taste the soup*
173. **Milk that turns slowly (69–75)** · — · an evil deed, like fresh milk, does not turn at once; it smoulders like fire under ash · notice one slow consequence · *it turns slowly*

## Chapter 6 · The wise (Days 174–175)
174. **Hidden treasure (76–81)** · pandita · follow the one who points out your faults as if showing buried treasure; a solid rock is not shaken by wind · thank someone who corrected you · *the rock doesn't shake*
