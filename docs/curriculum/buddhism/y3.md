# YEAR THREE — "the great story and the teachers" (Days 697–1061)
*The Buddha's life as a great poem, the first disciples and their songs, the long Jataka, then the teaching's journey across Asia and the world, and the teachers who carried it. Sources: Cowell, The Buddha-karita of Asvaghosha (SBE XLIX, 1894); Mrs Rhys Davids, Psalms of the Early Buddhists (1909, 1913); Cowell and others, The Jataka (1895–1907); Rhys Davids, The Questions of King Milinda (SBE XXXV–XXXVI, 1890–94); Legge, Fa-hien (1886); Beal, Si-yu-ki (1884); Hultzsch, Inscriptions of Asoka (1925); Geiger, Mahavamsa (1912). Later teachers are paraphrased, never quoted. Hard history is told plainly and gently.*
