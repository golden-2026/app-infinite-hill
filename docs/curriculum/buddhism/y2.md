## Block A · The four noble truths in depth (Days 332–352)
332. **The elephant's footprint** · — · as every footprint fits inside an elephant's, every good teaching fits inside the four truths (MN 28) · recall the four truths · *the biggest footprint*
333. **A physician's pattern** · — · later teachers note the truths follow a doctor's steps: illness, cause, cure, treatment · name one problem this way · *diagnose first*
334. **The first truth, in full** · — · birth, ageing, sickness, death, sorrow, not getting what you want: this is dukkha (SN 56.11) · list today's small dukkhas · *it's not just you*
335. **Three kinds of dukkha** · — · plain pain, the pain of change, and the unease built into all made things (SN 45.165) · spot each kind once · *three kinds*
336. **To be understood** · — · the first truth's task is to be understood, not fixed or blamed (SN 56.11) · understand one pain · *understand it*
337. **The second truth** · samudaya · craving for sense pleasure, for being, for not-being (SN 56.11) · notice all three cravings · *three thirsts*
338. **To be let go** · — · the second truth's task is to be abandoned (SN 56.11) · let go of one craving · *let it go*
339. **The third truth** · nirodha · the fading away and ending of that same craving: relief is possible · rest in one moment of not-wanting · *it can stop*
340. **Blowing out** · nibbana · Sariputta: nibbana is the ending of greed, hate and delusion (SN 38.1) · watch a candle go out · *the fire goes out*
341. **To be realised** · — · the third truth's task is to be tasted for oneself (SN 56.11) · taste one moment of peace · *taste it*
342. **The fourth truth** · magga · the way that leads to the ending of dukkha: the eightfold path · name the eight factors · *the path*
343. **To be developed** · — · the fourth truth's task is to be walked (SN 56.11) · walk one factor today · *walk it*
344. **Three turns, twelve aspects** · — · each truth known, its task known, its task done: three turns of four truths (SN 56.11) · write out the twelve · *three turns*
345. **A handful of leaves** · — · the Buddha holds a few leaves: what I have taught is this; what I know is the forest; I taught what helps (SN 56.31) · hold a handful of leaves · *only what helps*
346. **The poisoned arrow** · — · a wounded man refuses treatment until he knows who shot him and why (MN 63) · drop one idle question · *pull the arrow out*
347. **The blind turtle** · — · a turtle surfacing once a century through a yoke adrift on the ocean: human birth is that rare (SN 56.48) · value this hour · *rarer than the turtle*
348. **This, because of that** · paticcasamuppada · when this is, that is; from the arising of this, that arises (SN 12.21) · trace one mood to its causes · *this, because of that*
349. **The twelve links** · — · ignorance, formations, consciousness, name-and-form, the senses, contact, feeling, craving, clinging, becoming, birth, ageing-and-death (SN 12.1) · learn the twelve links · *twelve links*
350. **Where the chain breaks** · — · between feeling and craving there is a gap where the chain can be cut · catch one feeling before the craving · *cut between*
351. **Seeing it is seeing** · — · whoever sees dependent origination sees the dhamma (MN 28) · see one connection clearly · *seeing it is seeing*
352. **The truths, closed** · — · four truths, three turns, twelve links · sit with all four · *four truths, one path*
