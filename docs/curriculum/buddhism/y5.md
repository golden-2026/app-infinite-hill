# YEAR FIVE — "reading the sources, living the path" (Days 1427–1791)
*A little Pali, Shantideva's guide read whole, the forty meditation subjects, the ten perfections lived, the householder's path, death and dying, a season of reading the sources alone, and building a life of practice. Then the summit. Sources: L. D. Barnett, The Path of Light (Shantideva's Bodhicaryavatara, 1909); Fausbøll, Sutta Nipata (SBE X, 1881); Müller, Dhammapada (SBE X, 1881); Rhys Davids, Dialogues of the Buddha; Chalmers, Further Dialogues; the Visuddhimagga and later texts paraphrased and cited by name.*
