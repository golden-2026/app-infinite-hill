1079. **Six pairs** · — · calm, lightness, softness, workability, skill and straightness, in body and in mind · feel your body soften · *soft and workable*
1080. **Abstinences** · — · right speech, action and livelihood as moments of mind, not just rules · abstain once, knowingly · *a moment of restraint*
1081. **Compassion and wisdom** · — · compassion, gladness and wisdom as factors that arise in a moment · notice a moment of compassion · *compassion is a moment*

## Block C · The Diamond Sutra (Days 1118–1149)
*Thirty-two short sections, one a day, in Max Müller's 1894 translation (Vagrakkhedika). The Buddha and Subhuti in conversation.*
1118. **An ordinary morning (section 1)** · Vajracchedika · the Buddha dresses, walks for alms, eats, washes his feet and sits down · do one morning chore fully · *an ordinary morning*
1119. **Subhuti asks (section 2)** · Subhuti · how should one set out on the bodhisattva path, and hold the mind? · ask your own central question · *how to hold the mind*
1120. **All beings freed, none freed (section 3)** · — · lead all beings to nirvana, yet no being is led, because a bodhisattva has no idea of a self · help without keeping score · *no one to count*
1121. **Giving without resting on anything (section 4)** · — · give without dwelling on the gift, and the merit is boundless as space · give one thing without thinking of it again · *boundless as space*
1122. **Not seen by marks (section 5)** · — · can the Tathagata be known by bodily marks? no; where there are marks there is illusion · see someone beyond appearance · *beyond the marks*
1123. **The raft again (section 6)** · — · even the teaching is to be let go of, like a raft, and how much more what isn't the teaching · let go of one teaching · *even the raft*
1124. **Nothing fixed (section 7)** · — · has the Tathagata attained anything? there is no fixed thing called the highest awakening · hold one certainty loosely · *nothing fixed*
1125. **Four lines (section 8)** · — · teaching even four lines of this sutra is worth more than worlds full of treasure · share four lines of something good · *four lines*
1126. **No "I have attained" (section 9)** · — · a stream-enterer does not think "I have entered the stream"; Subhuti rests in peace without that thought · do good without the thought "I did" · *without "I did"*
1127. **A mind that rests on nothing (section 10)** · — · a mind should arise that rests on nothing: the line said to have woken Huineng · notice one moment without clinging · *resting on nothing*
1128. **Sands of the Ganges (section 11)** · — · as many rivers as the Ganges' sands, and worlds of treasure for each: still less than this teaching · count one blessing · *more than the sand*
1129. **A shrine where it's taught (section 12)** · — · wherever the sutra is taught, the ground becomes like a shrine · treat one place as holy · *the ground becomes a shrine*
1130. **The diamond's name (section 13)** · — · the sutra is named the diamond that cuts; the thirty-two marks are no marks · name one thing without grasping · *the diamond cuts*
1131. **Subhuti weeps (section 14)** · — · hearing it, Subhuti weeps; the Buddha recalls being cut apart by a king without anger, because he had no idea of a self · soften toward one hurt · *he wept to hear it*
1132. **Carrying the sutra (section 15)** · — · one who keeps and explains this sutra carries awakening on their shoulders · carry one teaching today · *on your shoulders*
1133. **Despised and cleansed (section 16)** · — · if someone is scorned for keeping this teaching, past wrongs are wiped away · bear one small scorn · *cleansed by scorn*
1134. **Suchness (section 17)** · — · "Tathagata" means suchness; there was nothing to receive from the past Buddha · see one thing just as it is · *suchness*
1135. **Past, present, future mind (section 18)** · — · past mind can't be grasped, present mind can't be grasped, future mind can't be grasped · try to grasp this moment · *can't be grasped*
1136. **Merit is no merit (section 19)** · — · merit is called merit because it is no fixed thing · do good lightly · *so-called merit*
1137. **Not the perfect body (section 20)** · — · the Tathagata isn't seen through a perfect body · look past one appearance · *not the body*
1138. **Nothing to teach (section 21)** · — · the Tathagata has no teaching to teach; that is called teaching · explain something without owning it · *no teaching, teaching*
1139. **Nothing gained (section 22)** · — · in the highest awakening, not even the least thing is gained · give up one gain · *not the least thing*
1140. **Equal, no high or low (section 23)** · — · this truth is level; there is nothing high or low in it · treat someone as your equal · *no high, no low*
1141. **Heaps of jewels (section 24)** · — · jewels piled high as mountains are less than keeping four lines · keep four lines by heart · *less than four lines*
1142. **No beings freed (section 25)** · — · the Tathagata doesn't think "I free beings"; there are no beings for him to free · help someone and forget it · *no one to free*
1143. **Form and sound (section 26)** · — · those who see me by form or seek me by sound walk the wrong road (as Müller renders the verse) · look past form once · *not by form or sound*
1144. **Not annihilation (section 27)** · — · don't think the bodhisattva destroys things; emptiness is not nothing · honour one real thing · *not nothing*
1145. **Merit not held (section 28)** · — · the bodhisattva does not take hold of merit · let merit go · *not held*
1146. **Neither coming nor going (section 29)** · — · the Tathagata neither comes nor goes; that is why he is called the Tathagata · rest in not going anywhere · *neither coming nor going*
1147. **Dust and worlds (section 30)** · — · a world ground into dust: are the specks many? the world is not a world, so called · look at dust in light · *a world of dust*
1148. **Views of self (section 31)** · — · views of self and being are not views; so they are called views · hold one view lightly · *so-called views*
1149. **Like stars, like dew (section 32)** · — · look on the world like stars, a lamp, an illusion, dew, a bubble, a dream, lightning, a cloud (Müller, section 32) · watch dew or a bubble · *like dew, like lightning*
