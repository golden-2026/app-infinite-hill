- **One day (2026-11-14):** a 60-second story film → the guide's questions → the reveal (the real place tied to the
  story lights up as a spot on your path, and the guide names it) → claim my card → your path for the week. Places are

  labeled "the Holy Land".

## Settled 2026-11-10 (still standing)
- **One faith at a time, the Guide included.** On a faith path: no cross-faith answers, no "visit another faith"
  offers. Cross-faith only in "my own religion".
- **Two-faith families follow two separate paths** and switch between them; never mixed in one lesson.
- **Hindu deities may be shown,** as Hindu art traditionally does. Never show God in traditions that don't depict God,
  or the faces of Jesus, Muhammad or the Sikh Gurus.
- **Trips earn story cards; stamps are only for real-life attendance.**
- **Name celebrities as guides, never "signed".**
- **AI-generated people are for mood drafts only;** anything that runs uses real people.

## The purpose
Big, bold statements that get people to see what we share and treat each other better. Faith is the substance; the brand is loud.

## The offer
- **Pick a faith to learn, or build "my own religion".** Seven faith paths (Christianity, Catholicism, Judaism, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism) plus "my own religion". The internal id is SPIRITUAL; "Simply Spiritual", "Wildwonder" and "my own path" are old names. "Build your own" is only a call to action.
- **The guides:** Denzel Washington (Christianity), Nicole Kidman (Catholicism), Natalie Portman (Judaism), Mahershala Ali (Islam), Priyanka Chopra (Hinduism), Orlando Bloom (Buddhism), Diljit Dosanjh (Sikhism), Justin Bieber (my own religion).
- **One faith at a time.** Inside a faith path, everything stays in that faith. Cross-faith exploring and "what we share" live only in "my own religion" and in the brand (billboards, social). Never comparative religion and never "all faiths are the same": "more in common than you think", each piece naming its tradition.
- "My own religion" may become the main path, so it must be incredible and curated by the AI guide.
- Nobody wakes up wanting to learn religion. Lead with what people want and what they get out of it; the lessons are how.

## The product
- **The new app** (`prototypes/greybox/hill-app-doors.html`, opened from the website at /app; test link https://brand-next--infinite-hill.netlify.app/) is the app we improve. **The old app** (Expo, `apps/app/src`) is frozen; Xogito builds the final app.
- **Core loop:** Duolingo-style short daily lessons, streaks with rest days (seven in a row is "lit"), Finn celebrating your wins, cards. Fun first.
- **Each day:** a 60-second story film told by your guide (a real story from the faith, then that faith's actual teaching, then life today) → the guide's questions → the reveal → your card → your path. No drills, no self-help tips. Standard: `docs/LESSON_STANDARD.md`.
- **The world is the wrapper:** the hill is home; each story's real place is revealed after the story and lights up on your path; stories earn rarity cards; Finn's "drip" is the other reward. Stamps only for real-life attendance.
- **The AI guide** gets to know you (intake, adaptive placement anywhere in 5 years) and curates your path. It never speaks as God.
- **Privacy:** no account; everything stays on the phone. Only guide questions and an anonymous return count leave it. We don't sell your faith.
- **Community** is the top unsolved problem, with Magna-style real-life gatherings as the model. 18+, verified organizers only, no leaderboards.
- **Celebrity guides** ("celebrity guides", not "famous") tell the stories and ask the questions; Finn never talks. Keepers are scholars who review content.
- Not launching soon: no date-driven moves. Rank work by how much it improves the product.

## The brand
- **North star: Magna** (drinkmagna.com): loud, confident, shock value, on trend; drops; a short homepage.
- **Tagline:** "making religion yours again." (never "cool again"). The owner approved "religion, reimagined." for the new website (2026-11-13); confirm with the owner before swapping it anywhere else.
- **Mascot: Finn**, a smiling, infinite sun in a backwards cap (name pending a trademark check before it goes in user copy). Only the approved backwards-cap art. Wordless.
- **Voice:** warm first, punchy and current, never corny or flat. The brand may roast boring faith apps but never a faith, a person or a named rival. Prayer is always a choice. No sing-alongs. No romance.
- **Look:** black #0A0A0A, lime #EEFF6A, cream #F3EFE4; Anton for display, Manrope for everything else; lowercase UI. The old night navy is gone. Episode-A accents in the lesson films (no rainbow gradients); sacred art inside the lesson films; present-day real people in brand work.
- **Billboards:** Direction A "every faith says" (`docs/brand/billboards-v2-brief.md`). Shock that carries weight, never jokey lines on glossy models.
- **Marketing leads Christian-first.** Don't lead with Diwali, kirtan or Ganesha in films or pitches.

## Never
- Say "signed" about voices or Keepers in anything shared.
- Put "draft" or "review pending" labels in user copy, or mention daily AI limits.
- Invent testimonials or community numbers.
- Promise "no ads".
- Show the faces of Jesus, of prophets in Islam, or of the Sikh Gurus.
- Publish religious content before a Keeper has reviewed it.
- State a Foundation share of proceeds (the Foundation is "in formation").
