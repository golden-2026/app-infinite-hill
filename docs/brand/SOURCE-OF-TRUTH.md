## The ledger (current decisions)

### Product and flow
- One faith at a time. Cross-faith exploring lives only in **"my own religion"** (the path's name everywhere;
  "build your own" is only a call-to-action verb; "Simply Spiritual", "Wildwonder", "spiritual" are old names).
- One day: "what's on your mind today?" (5 mood buttons: i'm angry at someone i love · a worry i can't shake · i feel
  lost · honestly? a good day · i'm missing someone) → a story picked for you → 60-second film → the guide's quick
  questions → "you showed up" → (faith paths, day 2+) "what spoke to you?" → the reveal: the real place lights up on
  your path → claim your card (once) → Today / your path. Places are named only after the story. (2026-11-14/15)
- Feeling-first, path in order (owner, 2026-11-15): after picking a faith, the guide asks "what's on your mind
  today?" first (5 feelings + "just show me today's story"). The path is the spine: your place moves forward one
  lesson at a time from where placement put you; a feeling never moves it. A feeling picks from stories NEAR you only
  (this week first, then the next week or two, or one you've done), never months ahead; a pick outside today's lesson
  is a labeled bonus (counts for the streak, lights a bonus spot) and the guide says "tomorrow we're back to …".
  The knowledge quiz comes after the first story, optional. Needs 2-3 feeling-tagged stories per feeling per month
  per path (curriculum work, scholar-checked) so later learners get stage-right picks.
- Never over their heads (owner, 2026-11-15: "we want to make sure we don't deliver content to someone that is way
  over their heads"): every story carries a level (starter / building / deep) and a "needs first" list of ideas it
  assumes, tagged in scholar review. A story is offered only when its "needs first" is met. Feeling picks come only
  from done, this week's, or starter-tagged stories (never above your level; fallback = the closest starter story).
  New words explained the first time. After a story a "this was a lot" tap slows things down (easier stories, more
  explaining, can move you back on the path, nothing lost). Missed quiz answers keep the next stories easier. The
  guide's AI answers follow the person's level.
- First-day details (owner, 2026-11-15): if a person takes a bonus instead of today's lesson, tomorrow is still that lesson (day 1 stays day 1: "tomorrow, the very first page"). Holy days show on Today only within 3 days of the day (full list in You). The "how it works" card leaves Today (the hello explains itself). Every path needs a scholar-tagged starter story for each feeling, including grief ("i'm missing someone"), near day 1.
- Repeats and pacing (owner, 2026-11-15: "make sure it's not a zoo of an experience"): today's story is always new
  (the path only moves forward). Feeling picks prefer unseen stories; if the best fit was seen, the guide asks
  ("want it again, or something new?"), never a silent repeat. Rewatching is user-started (cards / path). Review =
  a short question about an older story (spaced review), never a full rerun. One clear next step a day (guide's
  question → one story); at most ONE optional extra a day (a bonus or a holy-day moment, not both). Each day ends
  with "tomorrow: <next story>"; 7 stories light the week; a week-complete moment on day 7. Today screen: the guide's
  question + today's story on top, the week's path below, at most one optional card; everything else lives in its
  own tab or appears only when it matters.
- "my own religion": after each story "does this speak to you?" (this is me / not sure yet / not for me), an
  occasional big question from the guide, and the **"what i live by"** page (what matters to you / still figuring
  out / not for me: "true for millions. just not yours right now."). Faith paths get a lighter **"what spoke to you"**
  (no "not for me"; "the teaching stays the teaching"). (2026-11-15)
- No eight-homes map, no place-name homes (Marigold, Olive Grove, Pomegranate, Date Palm, Lotus, Nectar, Angelus,
  Wildwonder are internal keys only, never shown). No flight before a story. (2026-11-14)
- Streak: 7 days in a row with no rest day = **"lit"** ("your streak is lit"; Spanish "tu racha está encendida").
  Never "golden". Rest days + earn-back stay. (2026-11-15)
- Holy Land places (Israel, West Bank, Palestine, East Jerusalem, Golan) are labeled **"the Holy Land"** (Spanish
  "Tierra Santa"). (2026-11-15)

- The weekly drop is Friday at 7pm. Spanish name of "what i live by": "lo que me guía". Each lesson = a 60-second story film inside a ~5-6 minute episode (questions, reveal, card); say "a five-minute lesson a day" / "a 60-second film", never "a 60-second lesson".
- Any picture or film that shows the app must be a capture of the real app (never a redraw).

- The payoff (owner, 2026-11-15): "become the calm one in the room." (ES "conviértete en la calma del lugar."), then "the fight. the funeral. the 2am spiral. the dinner where religion comes up. everyone turns to someone. it's going to be you.", the day 1 / week 1 / month 1 / year 1 road, and "you'll know what you believe, and why. and you'll finally understand the person next to you." Homepage section #where; reuse in deck, films and ads.
- Homepage order (owner picked version A, 2026-11-15; EN site-new.html + ES site-es.html, same structure): top (#top, "religion, reimagined.") → how it works (#how) → research strip (#research: one line + 3 Pew stats) → the film (#film: "one feeling. every path has its own story." / ES "un sentimiento. cada camino tiene su propia historia.") → become the calm one (#where) → the road (#road: "you'll know what you believe, and why." + day 1 → year 1) → your guides (#guides) → you didn't leave the big questions (#kept) → pick your way in (#ways: cards #deep, #mine → /build-your-own, #need, #start) → scholars check every lesson + why not ChatGPT (#trust, #keepers, #faq) → what people are telling us (#reviews) → end card (#end). Only 3 shout headlines (top, #where, end); the rest medium. Word budget: about 35 body words per section, about 12 per card. The film section keeps today's film until the all-paths film replaces it.
