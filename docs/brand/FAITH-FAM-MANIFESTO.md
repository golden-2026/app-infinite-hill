# Faith Fam manifesto (draft for owner review, 2026-11-11)

FAITH FAM

we love our faith.
we love yours too.

some of us pray every morning.
some of us haven't been back since we were kids.
some of us are still figuring out what to call it.
all of us are here.

we go deep in our own story.
we get curious about our neighbor's.
the kid across the street, praying a different way? family.

we don't do hate.
not in our name. not in any faith's name.

we don't do boring either.
faith is the wildest story ever told.
it should feel like it.

one story. one teaching. one thing to try tonight.
step in to remember who you are.
step out to live it.

faithful and curious.
never faithful and closed.

this is the faith fam.
pull up a chair.
