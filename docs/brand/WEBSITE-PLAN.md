# Website plan: for your yes before I build

*November 13, 2026. Words only, no design yet. Nothing below is live.*

**What the website is for, in one sentence:** a visitor sees celebrities and a film in the first five seconds, finds the part written for them, and taps "start free".

**Where the words come from:**
- **[doors]**: the bold test site ("you didn't leave the big questions"). Most of the lines come from here.
- **[live]**: the current live site. The celebrity photos and the 8 real reviews come from here.
- **[you]**: your words from today.
- **[new]**: written by me. These lines are the ones that most need your eye.

---

## How we describe the product (used everywhere the same way)

| Word | What it means | We say |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesson** | The daily thing. About 5 minutes. | "one lesson. five minutes." |
| **Film** | How every lesson opens. About 60 seconds. | "every lesson opens with a film" |
| **Questions** | 2 or 3 quick taps after the film, covering what the faith actually teaches. | "a few quick questions" |
| **Carry it** | One thing to try today. Your streak grows. | "carry it" |
| **Picked for you** | Lessons are written ahead and checked by scholars (that part is the same for everyone). What's picked is *which* lesson you get today, based on what you're going through and where you are. | "picked for you" |

**The honest line between the two parts:**
- The lessons themselves are made and checked once, for everyone.
- The choice of which lesson you get is made for you.

So we never say the AI writes your lesson.

## Things I fixed by reading it as a first-time visitor

1. **Example mismatch.** The how-it-works example was a Buddha story, but the film right above it is a Jesus story. I took the example out so it doesn't confuse people.
2. **Faith buttons.** The hero's faith buttons left out "my own religion". It now sits as its own button.
3. **Repeated line.** "see what we share. treat each other better." was said three times. Now it's said once, plus the banner.
4. **Mismatched buttons.** The top said "start free" and the bottom said "go deep in mine". Both now say the same thing.
5. **Song lyric.** I dropped the line "because you gotta have faith." It's a famous song lyric, and the headline is stronger alone.
