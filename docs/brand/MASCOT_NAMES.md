What a good name needs:
- short and easy for kids to say;
- belongs to no single faith, so no Noor, Bodhi, Lumen or saint names;
- ties to the brand (the infinity eyes, the hill, the lantern);
- works in English and Spanish;
- sounds nothing like Duolingo's character names.
