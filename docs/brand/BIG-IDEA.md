## What Infinite Hill is

The best of a video game and AI. A beautiful world you step into every day, and a guide you already know who talks with
you, learns who you are and plans your days. A game treats everyone the same; Infinite Hill listens.

Life gets loud: you get scared, lost or stuck. You step into Infinite Hill to get grounded and guided. Then you step back
into your life, a little steadier, and come back whenever you need. No wall between the two.

Inside the company we say it plainly: Infinite Hill moves people from ego to awareness and back, and makes the trip easier
each time. In public we say it in everyday words (the line above), because every tradition names it differently
(Christians: "be still and know"; Hindus: the true self; Buddhists: awareness).

## The story (film order; the website follows it below its top section)

1. **The problem.** Every generation had a way through. We walked away and replaced it with a feed. Now we're divided,
   confused; our kids ask questions we can't answer; we say prayers we don't understand.
2. **The turn.** "But we can learn, together."
3. **The answer, in three parts.** People you already know (the celebrity hosts). Technology that knows you (you talk; it
   listens). A world you want to come back to (the mountain). Then: **"Introducing Infinite Hill."**
4. **What it's like to be there** (experiences, never the map first):
   - **Your guide listens.** Your guide is the celebrity guide of your path (Denzel Washington for Christianity). They
     tell every story and ask every question; they get to know you, plan each day, and stay with you. (Updated
     2026-11-14: Finn never talks; he celebrates.)
   - **A class you take part in.** A 60-second real story, then your guide's questions, your call. About five minutes.
   - **Ask anyone, out loud.** Sit with a pastor, priest, rabbi, imam or swamiji on the hill and ask anything, live, by
     voice (typing optional). Characters on the hill; their answers follow what the scholars checked.
   - **It comes into your real life.** The world says "breathe" and you breathe in your parked car while your screen is
     the waterfall. A small "try it" for your day. Your guide suggests a real church, temple or event near you, and checks
     in afterward.
   - Music, festivals, lanterns with your family.
5. **Your path, and the places the stories take you.** "This is Infinite Hill": after each story, the real place it is
   tied to lights up on your path (the reveal), and you claim its card. Places are named only after the story. You go
   deeper in one faith; you don't tour all eight. (Updated 2026-11-04 by the owner: the eight-homes map and the gondola
   are gone; exploring every faith lives only in "my own religion". Updated 2026-11-14: paths are named by faith, not by
   a home; trips earn cards, and stamps are only for real-life attendance.)
6. **Trust.** A scholar of each faith checks everything and can say no.
7. **The close.** "One human race. One hill." Then "Infinite Hill. Making religion yours again."

The film's spine is one person going through the loop (Christian-first: a mom in her car, guided by Denzel on the Christianity path):
lost in real life → steps in through her phone → grounded and guided → steps back out changed → walks into a real church
on Sunday → her family lights a lantern on the hill.

## Rules that come with it

- Voice first (Anurag's point): talking to your guide and to the people on the hill is the main way in; typing is optional.
- Prayer is always a choice. No AI speaks as God. No faces of Jesus, prophets or Sikh Gurus.
- Real-world suggestions only from verified places; off-app gatherings 18+; the guide never pressures anyone to go.
- Christian-first in marketing order; every faith shown warmly.
- Celebrity voices are AI versions, covered by their contracts; the animated likenesses are covered too.
- Lead with experiences. Never open a film or page with a map.
- One faith first: films, the site and the app show one person going deeper in one faith, never a tour of every faith
  or "8 of 8 faiths visited". Cross-faith exploring is opt-in, inside "my own religion".
- Light on text: one picture or clip and one sentence per idea.
