## The product in one line

A loud, fun daily app where you talk to a celebrity guide, and it brings you a short, cinematic episode from your
faith, picked for your day: approved films, real teaching, music, games and a cliffhanger.

- Eight doors: Christian, Catholic, Jewish, Muslim, Hindu, Buddhist, Sikh, and "my own religion" (build your own faith).
- **Decided:** one faith first. You go deep in your own faith; cross-faith exploring is opt-in, inside "my own religion".
- **Decided:** tagline "making religion yours again." Big idea: "step in to remember who you are. step out to live it."
- **Decided:** loud brand (Magna is the model: lime + black, Anton display type, shock through surprise, never mockery).
- **Decided:** celebrity guides and voices people know narrate. Keepers (scholars of each faith) check content.
- Mascot: Finn, the approved backwards-cap art only (name pending a trademark check).
- Not launching soon. No date-driven decisions.

## What's prototype vs. production-ready

Short version: **almost everything new is a working prototype that shows what "good" looks like. None of the new
app has accounts, a database or a real backend for drops, cards or circles.**

| piece | state |
|---|---|
| New app (`hill-app.html`, ~375 KB single file) | prototype. All state in the browser's `localStorage` (`ih-hill-app-demo`). No accounts, no sync. English only. |
| Chat-first app (`hill-chat.html`) | prototype. Same local state. Calls `/api/guide`, scripted fallback without it. |
| Drops, story cards, Finn's drip | prototype, computed on the device from a fixed weekly schedule. No server, nothing to buy. Drip has no approved art yet (`art/finn/drip/drip.js` lists none). |
| Circles, drop nights | prototype, local only. Sample members are made up on the page and labelled as demo. No network calls. |
| Episodes (226) and episode engine | working static pages, generated by `episodes/build.mjs`. Content has open scholar flags (`episodes/FLAGS.md`). |
| Curriculum player | working static page over 14,328 generated JSON files. Most days have no film yet (type-led). |
| Wave 1 films | 240 days mapped (first 30 days × 8 faiths): 180 reuse existing films, 60 new film plans; 58 of those have art. Picture notes in `films/wave1/REVIEW-NOTES.md`. |
| New website (`apps/app/public-next`) | static HTML, EN + ES, all footer pages. On the test link only. |
| Backend (`api/*.js` via `netlify/functions/`) | real code with tests, running in production for the old app: Guide, companion, encrypted state, waitlist, pulse (return counts), circles, friends, wellbeing, Stripe pieces. The new app only uses `/api/guide` and `/api/pulse`. |

## The hard rules (non-negotiable)

These come from the project's standing rules. The full list, with the reasons, is in `DECISIONS-AND-RULES.md`.

**Safety and privacy**
- The Guide and companion must fall back gracefully when no AI key is configured.
- Never put server credentials in `EXPO_PUBLIC_*`, `VITE_*` or any browser code.
- Crisis care: danger or self-harm words point to 988 (US) or local emergency help, in every language, before anything else.
- Don't log credentials, auth headers, decrypted snapshots, chosen faith, practice history, journal text or Guide
  questions. Treat recovery files like passwords.
- Don't claim a feature is connected until a live request proves it.
- Nothing deploys to production, changes production secrets or goes public without the owner's okay.

**Religious content**
- Religious content is public only after a Keeper (scholar) review. Lessons get repeated review passes; a year goes
  live only with the owner's okay.
- AI never speaks as God and never plays Jesus, prophets or Gurus. Holy figures' words stay fixed and checked.
- No faces of Jesus, prophets or Sikh Gurus. No people in Islamic story scenes.
- Use only facts already in an approved lesson. Never invent religious claims or quotes.
- Prayer is always a choice (watch, learn or join in). Never ask anyone to sing.
- Never mock a faith, its holy figures or its people. Jokes are about the situation or ourselves.

**Brand and honesty**
- Finn speaks warm first and says "i"; "we" is only the company. Approved backwards-cap art only.
- No "draft" or "review pending" labels in user-facing copy; never mention daily AI limits.
- Never invent testimonials or community numbers; show real counts only above a threshold.
- Never imply a celebrity or Keeper has agreed to or endorsed anything they haven't. Frame plans as plans.
- Say "celebrity guides," never "famous guides."
- Keep the Foundation "in formation"; never state a share of proceeds.
- Never say the hill never ends. Progress lines celebrate.

**Working with the owner**
- Shaan is a non-technical founder: plain, brief language, no jargon.
- Send phone-size screenshots before any design or copy change goes live. Ask before every deploy.
- Tell the owner the cost before any big job (AI batch runs, video generation, lesson checks).

## A suggested first sprint (an idea to steer at kickoff)

Xogito's proposal is a 5-week spec, research and prototype phase. One way to use the first week or two:

1. **Get it running on your side.** Receive the repo and media, serve the prototypes, run `npm run test:unit`,
   deploy a private preview of your own. Produce a short "what we found" note: what to keep, what's throwaway.
2. **Decide the base.** Native app (Expo again, or something else) vs. web-first, and how much of the old Expo app's
   backend (`api/*.js`, Supabase sync, encrypted state) carries over. We'd value Bogdan's view on what not to decide yet.
3. **Turn the contracts into a spec.** The episode contract, done ids, lesson JSON and app state (`DATA-CONTRACTS.md`)
   are the current shape; propose the real data model: accounts, progress, cards, drops, circles.
4. **The guide.** Chat-first, remembers you, curates only from the approved library, safety built in. Start from
   `api/guide.js`, `docs/GUIDE_PLAYBOOK.md` and the 51 cases in `tests/guide-cases/cases.json`. Estimate cost per
   active user at 10k and 100k.
5. **Test with real people.** Chat-first vs. menus (`hill-chat.html` vs. `hill-app.html`), drops, and what brings
   someone back on day 7. Keep testing throughout.
6. **Sketch the scholar review screen** for the film pipeline: a Keeper approves each film before it enters the library.
7. **Smallest community test** we could run in a month (see the three layers in `DECISIONS-AND-RULES.md`).

Deliverables we'd find most useful by the end of the phase: a recommended architecture, a data model, a cost model
for AI and video, a tested prototype of the chat-first guide, and a plan for community.
