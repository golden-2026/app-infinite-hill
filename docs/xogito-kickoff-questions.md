# Questions for the Xogito kickoff

For Shaan, for the kickoff the week of Nov 9, 2026 with Gabe (design), the PM and Bogdan Costea (CTO).
Phase one: 5 weeks, about $25K. The five questions to ask first are marked ★.

## 1. Scope: what ships in 5 weeks

1. ★ **"At the end of week 5, what exactly do I hold in my hand?"** Ask them to list the deliverables: a tested
   prototype, a spec, a technical plan, a build estimate, research findings.
   *Why it matters:* the proposal was written for a research-and-prototype phase. If "done" isn't written down, you
   can't tell on day 35 whether you got $25K of value.
2. **"How much of the 5 weeks is research with real people, and how much is building?"**
   *Why it matters:* your brief asked for validated personas and real families coming back on day 7. If it turns into
   five weeks of screens, you're still guessing what people want.
3. **"Which of these do you want to settle first: AI-guide-first (Gus's idea) or a reviewed film library the guide picks from (your lean)?"**
   *Why it matters:* this decides almost everything else. Settle it in week 1 so they don't build the wrong one.

## 2. Which app they build on

4. ★ **"Will you start from the new app (the hill app with episodes, drops and the guide) and treat the old app as reference only?"**
   *Why it matters:* you've decided the new app is the app. If they quietly start from the old app, you pay twice for
   work you've already moved past.
5. **"Native app, web app, or both, and when do you decide?"** Ask Bogdan what he'd decide now and what he'd wait on.
   *Why it matters:* it changes cost, App Store timing and who you hire next. A good CTO says what not to lock in yet.

## 3. Ownership, code and access

6. ★ **"Does the contract say Infinite Hill owns all the code, designs and research from day one, in our own GitHub and accounts?"**
   *Why it matters:* if they own the repo, the cloud accounts or the AI keys, leaving them later is slow and expensive.
   3 of the 4 CVs Gus sent were outside contractors, so ask that their work is assigned to you too.
7. **"How do you want to receive the prototypes and the 14 GB of art and film?"** (The prototypes aren't in git yet.)
   *Why it matters:* someone has to decide this before they can start, and it's your call, not theirs.

## 4. Team and who does what

8. **"Who are the actual people, with names, and how many hours a week each?"** Ask about the PM in particular, and
   whether anyone has shipped a consumer app or mobile game.
   *Why it matters:* agencies often sell the senior team and staff the junior one. You've said design and game feel are
   your biggest worries.
9. **"Who is my one day-to-day contact, and who can say no to me?"**
   *Why it matters:* you want a hands-on PM who turns your vision into a plan, and someone who pushes back on scope.
   Recommended split: Xogito builds the plumbing (accounts, data, app stores, speed, reliability); taste (look,
   voice, lessons) stays with you.

## 5. AI costs, keys and safety

10. ★ **"What will the AI guide cost per active user per month, at 10,000 and 100,000 users, and who pays the AI bills during the 5 weeks?"**
    *Why it matters:* AI and video can quietly cost more than the build. You want the number before you're committed.
11. **"Will you keep our safety rules as written?"** The guide still works when there's no AI key; danger or self-harm
    words point to 988 (or local help) first, in every language; nothing logs journal text, chosen faith, practice
    history or what people ask the guide; no keys in the app's code.
    *Why it matters:* these protect people in a hard moment and protect the company. Ask them to run the 51 guide test
    cases before any demo.
12. **"How do you keep the AI from making up religious claims or speaking as God, Jesus, a prophet or a Guru?"**
    *Why it matters:* one wrong quote in a sacred text can end trust with a faith community overnight.

## 6. App Store and religious content

13. **"What do Apple and Google need from us for an app with AI chat, faith content and teens?"** (age rating,
    reporting tools for community, AI content rules, privacy labels.)
    *Why it matters:* a rejection late in the build costs weeks. Better to design for it now.
14. **"Where does Keeper (scholar) review sit in the process, so nothing religious goes public unreviewed?"**
    *Why it matters:* it's one of your firmest rules. It needs a real step in the workflow, not a promise.

## 7. Analytics and the day-7 target

15. ★ **"How will we know if people come back on day 7, and what's the target before we spend more?"** (Your brief
    suggests about 4 in 10; Dan Nord said 30% before scaling.)
    Also ask what they'll measure and how they keep it private.
    *Why it matters:* this is the one number that says whether the app works, so agree on it before the build. And you
    want to learn what works without collecting people's faith or journal entries.

## 8. Testing and weekly demos

16. **"How do you test: with real people each week, and with automated checks on the code?"**
    *Why it matters:* "it works on our phones" isn't testing. You want real families trying it, and the existing tests
    kept passing.
17. **"Can we have a live demo every week on a test link, and nothing goes live without my okay?"**
    *Why it matters:* you see progress yourself instead of hearing about it, and you catch wrong turns in days, not weeks.

## 9. After week 5

18. **"What happens on day 36: the price and length of phase two, and can I take the work to another team with no penalty?"**
    *Why it matters:* you want a clean decision point, not an automatic rollover into a bigger contract.

---

## Don't agree to yet

- **A phase two, retainer or long contract.** Decide after you see the week-5 results.
- **Their repo, cloud accounts or AI keys holding your product.** Everything sits in Infinite Hill's accounts.
- **AI-first live video or AI-made sacred scenes.** Your lean is a reviewed film library the AI picks from.
- **Cross-faith features up front** (Gus's "here's the Sufi parallel"). One faith first; cross-faith lives only in "my own religion".
- **A game engine (Unity) or a 3D world build.** Gus himself says that waits until the direction is set.
- **Launch dates or App Store submission.** You're not launching soon.
- **Changes to the safety, privacy or scholar-review rules,** even "just for the prototype".
- **Price and terms you haven't seen in writing:** the discovery phase, the $5K credit and the fee weren't covered on the Nov 6 call.
