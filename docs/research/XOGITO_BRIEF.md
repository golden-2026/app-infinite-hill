# Brief for Xogito: the five-week research and prototype phase

Infinite Hill · October 2026 · from Shaan Sethi to Gus Spathis

## Who we're starting with
Families with one or two religions, live on the homepage as the first three "which one sounds like you?" cards:
1. "my partner and i grew up in different religions." Partners who want to understand each other.
2. "we have two religions at home. what do we teach our kids?" Parents unsure what to teach.
3. "our kids ask questions about our religion, and we don't have the answers." Parents who know the rituals, not the reasons.

Everyone else stays welcome (grief, doubt, curiosity, "my own religion"), but these families come first. All of it is a
hypothesis until the interviews say otherwise. Mission: every faith on one hill, more alike than we think; we honor the
real differences, never rank a religion, and never push conversion.

## What we want from the $25K (your proposal, focused)
1. **Validated personas.** Which of the three groups has the sharpest need, in their own words.
2. **The moment we show up.** When in a family's life they reach for this (a child's question, a holiday with the
   in-laws, a wedding, a new baby), mapped with the life-map exercise.
3. **Differentiators.** What only Infinite Hill gives them, versus Google, ChatGPT, a single-faith app or their house
   of worship.
4. **The top 1-5 features,** tested in the prototype (likely candidates below).
5. **How we reach them and keep them:** where these families gather, and what brings them back on day 7 and in week 6.
6. **A tested prototype, spec, technical plan and build estimate,** as in your proposal.

## What we bring
- **Interview guide** (60 minutes) and the **11×17 life map**, with a filled-in example.
- **Recruitment:** about 20 people, mostly from the three groups, at least two per religion, half 20-35 and half
  parents 30-50. Recruit through 2-3 houses of worship and youth groups, plus friends of friends.
- **The live app** (eight paths, five years of lessons, placement, streaks, the Guide) and the new recipe pilot that
  three blind judges preferred.
- **Feature candidates to test:** an "our family" sign-up (both religions, the kids' ages, how you're raising them);
  "when your kid asks" answers; shared lessons (one theme, both religions); a family holiday calendar with a short
  "be ready" lesson; one friend character per path whose story runs through the lessons.

## How we'd like to work
- Weekly 2-hour session; Hans Yang (advisor) joins where he can.
- Before any prototype, a short **vision board** we agree on together.
- A clear go / no-go at the end: we want to see families come back on their own (we suggest about 4 in 10 still
  opening on day 7), and hear "this is for us" in their words.
- Recruit beyond our own network; pay participants a fair thank-you.

## Questions for you

- How would you test "coming back on their own" inside five weeks?
- Who on your team leads research and design, and can we meet them before we start?
