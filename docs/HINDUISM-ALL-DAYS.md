# Hinduism: every day already written

All 1,791 days are written. Years 1 to 4 (days 1 to 1,426) passed the quality check. Year 5 (days 1,427 to 1,791) is written but held until you okay it.

Every day has the same parts: an opening line, 3 story cards, the teaching (about 8 short parts), how Hindus actually do it today, 2 to 3 chat games that test understanding, a short practice, the word of the day (with Sanskrit and how to say it), a closing line, and the sources (scripture references). Days 1 to 30, plus 40 and 60, also play a film.

Each row below: day, title, then what the day is about. Below that, *the closing line*.

- **385. The caterpillar's crossing** — a small creature at the end of a blade of grass never lets go until it has hold of the next one

### Days 391 to 420

- **391. The self made of food** — the Taittiriya's outermost layer of a person is made of food, and it calls food the eldest of beings  
  *the body is the first self, made of food*
- **392. The self made of breath** — the demons could pierce the mind but shattered on the breath, and the Taittiriya finds a self made of each  
  *inside the body, breath; inside breath, mind*
- **393. The self made of bliss** — the Taittiriya goes two layers deeper and finds the Hindu teaching that the self, at its center, is joy  
  *at the center of you is ananda, joy*
- **394. Where words turn back** — the Taittiriya says words and mind turn back from Brahman, which is why Hindus can give God a thousand names and call none of them the whole  
  *brahman is beyond words; names only point*
- **395. The ladder of joy** — the Taittiriya multiplies joy a hundredfold, rung after rung, then hands every rung to the one free of desire: the choice Nachiketa made at Death's door  
  *joy grows as wanting falls away*
- **396. Bhrigu and the five returns** — a son asks his father to teach him Brahman; the father gives him Hinduism's classic definition, then sends him away to find it  
  *brahman: what all is born from and returns to*
- **397. Food is Brahman** — right after Bhrigu reaches bliss, the Upanishad walks back into the kitchen: food is Brahman, so honor it, make plenty, and turn no guest away  
  *food is brahman: offer it, share it*
- **398. The song of the one who knows** — the Taittiriya ends with someone who finds one self in the eater and the eaten, and bursts into song: ha-vu, ha-vu, ha-vu

- **404. Prajnanam brahma: consciousness is Brahman** — the Aitareya ends by asking which one is the self, and answers with two words that became one of the great sayings  
  *consciousness is brahman*
- **405. Six students wait a year to ask** — six seekers arrive at a sage's door with their questions ready, and he tells them to wait a year

- **414. Fuel in hand** — Angiras sends the seeker to a guru with firewood in hand, and that's why students still bring an offering  
  *come to the guru with fuel in hand*
- **415. Sparks from one fire** — Angiras shows Shaunaka a blazing fire: every being is a spark of the one fire, and goes back to it
