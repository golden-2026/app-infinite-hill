## Gen Z is turning back toward faith, through tradition and learning
- **Stanford (2026):** 46 adults entered the Catholic Church at Easter, up from 14 the year before; for three decades the
  campus averaged about 4 adult baptisms a year. About 600 students attended a recent Mass of the Holy Spirit. Students
  describe being drawn by the intellectual tradition (reading the Church Fathers and Catholic philosophers) and by
  traditional worship (old hymns, Latin prayers). Primary source: [The Stanford Review](https://stanfordreview.org/catholic-conversions-surge-at-stanford-from-4-to-46-annually/);
  coverage: [NY Post, 2026-10-04](https://nypost.com/2026/10/04/lifestyle/stanford-university-sees-catholicism-surge-among-gen-z-students/),
  [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/stanford-students-turn-catholicism-big-120500278.html).
- **Campus-wide (as reported in that coverage):** participation in the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA)
  on college campuses up 115% on average from 2024–25 to 2025–26. *Verify the original survey before quoting.*
