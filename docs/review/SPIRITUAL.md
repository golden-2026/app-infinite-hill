## Day 1 · everything happens for a reason
Word: **meant to be** · Line: *not why, but what now*

- New lesson (2026-10-06), written from docs/learning/AUDIENCE_RESEARCH_SPIRITUAL.md (Top 20 #1, proposed day 1).
- Kelemen 1999: children chose between a physical explanation and a purpose explanation for pointy rocks (e.g. so animals could scratch on them); confirm the item wording and that 'many picked the animals' is fair to the result.
- Banerjee & Bloom 2014: believers and, to a lesser degree, atheists reported that significant life events happened for a reason; the tendency tracked mentalizing (reading intentions). The script says 'a surprising number of atheists' and glosses the mentalizing link plainly; confirm.
- The popular line is not in Aristotle's surviving works; 'nature does nothing in vain' is Politics 1.2 (1253a9), also De Anima 3.9 and elsewhere. Confirm no stronger attribution exists.
- Providence is described as a belief held by many religions, without saying it is false; the 'works either way' turn is meant to sit well with believers and non-believers alike. Keeper to confirm the tone.
- Story: Huainanzi 18 (塞翁失马). The second horse is 'a fine horse of the Hu' (nomads); the son's injury is to the leg/hip; most young men at the border died in the war. Told lightly; confirm.
