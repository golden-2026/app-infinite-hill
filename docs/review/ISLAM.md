# Islam: lessons to review

For the Keeper. 28 lessons were written or changed on 2026-10-06, from audience research (Reddit, YouTube, app reviews) and a five-year map. Each lists what the writer asks you to confirm. Nothing here is final until you sign off; mark any line you'd change.

How to read it: **day · title** — today's word / the line people carry. Then the writer's notes, then the sources.
