questions

We're an interfaith family. Which path?

both. the table (a planned family plan) holds different paths at one table. each person walks their own, and nobody has to pick a side.

Do I have to pick a religion?

no. pick a path, or my own religion. switch anytime.

Can I create my own religion?

yes. that's my own religion: walk it a little every day, take seven days on something on your mind, or wander through a different tradition each day. curious about a partner's faith? that lives in my own religion. your path stays yours.

Who checks the lessons?

a scholar of that one tradition, who knows it from the inside. how we check →

Do I have to start from the beginning?

no. start where you belong, not at zero. a couple of minutes of quick questions places you.

How long is it?

year one is about a lesson a day: a new place and a new story each time, and a story card for every one. years two to five go deeper: the texts underneath, the stories, the saints, the sources. five years deep, if you want it. year one is ready. the rest is being written and checked.

What happens if I miss a day?

life happens, and that's okay. a rest day covers it. your streak grows one lesson a day, with rest days built in, because every tradition knows rest. you start with two and earn one back every week. if a streak does break, two lessons in a day within three days earns it back, never for money. your days on the hill and your place on the path never reset.

Is it for kids?

everyone. under-13s join through a parent's table, on the parent's phone. no messages, no strangers, no ads.

Can my school use it?

soon. a teacher tries it free for a term; the school licenses it for less than the textbook it replaces; students never pay. launching after the pilot. infinite hill for schools →

Is infinite hill a nonprofit?

no. it's a company (Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc.), so it can pay its own way and keep the house free. part of what it earns goes to the infinite hill foundation, a separate nonprofit (in formation). and when giving opens, anything you give inside the app will go there in full.
