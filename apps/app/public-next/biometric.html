biometric consent.

this page is for the people whose voices we license. if you use the app, you never give us biometric data. we do not scan your face or your voice. ever.

a voice model is built from the recordings of the person whose voice we license. under laws like illinois’ biometric information privacy act, the data we derive from those recordings — a voiceprint — is biometric information. before we build a model, that person gives Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc. a written consent that says what we collect, what we use it for (narrating approved lessons and films, and nothing else), who processes it, how long we keep it (the length of their license), and that we permanently delete it within thirty days after.

we also obtain the specific consent california requires for a digital replica of a performer’s voice.
