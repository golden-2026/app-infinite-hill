why we don't write prayers.

three lines we never cross. they're the reason a Catholic Keeper and a Sikh Keeper will both let us in the door.

1. we never write a prayer

every prayer in infinite hill is that religion's own, from its own texts, in its own words. we give them to you as they are; we don't compose them, improve them, modernize them, or "make them relatable." a prayer somebody at a startup wrote is not a prayer. it's copy.

and praying is always your choice: watch, learn, or join in.

2. we never blend

one path at a time. a lesson in Hinduism is Hindu, start to finish. a lesson on my own religion uses wisdom stories from many places — and every one of them is tagged with where it came from, and none of them is folded into a teaching that pretends to be above all of them. eight paths, one hill. not one soup.

3. we never rank

we show what each faith says, in its own words, and never say who's right. you'll never see a chart on infinite hill ranking religions. we teach each path the way its own people teach it and let you walk where you want. the one thing we'll say out loud is the one rule they all landed on: love your enemy.

why it matters

the moment we write a prayer, blend a teaching, or pick a winner, it stops being theirs. we'd rather hand you the real thing.
