eight paths.

one program, eight paths. every path is built the same way and filled with its own words, its own stories, and its own texts. nothing is blended.

year one · one film a day

one film a day. a real story, what the faith actually teaches, what it means tonight. then quick games that test it, so it sticks. a few minutes and you're done. every day earns a story card. finish all seven in a week and your whole week lights up on your path.

years two to five · deeper

the texts underneath, the saints and teachers, the schools, the sources. five years deep, if you want it. year one is ready. the rest is being written and checked.

the eight paths, one line each

Christianity — year one: the Bible's big stories, from the first page of Genesis to the Gospels.
Catholicism — year one: the Gospels, Mary, the Mass, the rosary and the saints.
Judaism — year one: Torah stories, the sages, and the days the calendar keeps.
Islam — year one: the words every Muslim knows, starting with bismillah and al-Fatiha, and the stories behind them.
Hinduism — year one: Ganesha, the Ramayana, the Gita, and the festivals.
Buddhism — year one: the Buddha's life and the stories behind his teachings.
Sikhism — year one: Guru Nanak's journeys and the stories of the Gurus.
my own religion: build your own, one story at a time. after each one, call it: this is me, not sure yet, or not for me. what's true for you lands on your page, “what i live by.” every piece names its faith. never blended, never ranked.

already know some?

start where you belong, not at zero. a short check places you on the path. if it placed you too far, the path offers to walk you back, and nothing you've done is lost. your streak starts at day one, wherever you start.

the path rule

a path opens when it has a Keeper, a celebrity guide and its first films. more are coming. tell us which one you're hoping for.
