safety & hosts.

we want you to feel safe here, and your kids too. here's who gets to speak on infinite hill.

inside the app

there are no strangers on infinite hill. you can send a lantern — a link to a friend — or join a circle with a code from someone you know: a teacher, a temple, a church, a mosque, a synagogue, a gurdwara, a family. a circle sees how many are in it, how many walked today, and the nicknames of people who chose to show one. no feed, no followers, no messages from strangers.

your guide chat

the guide chat is AI, built on claude by anthropic, and it says so: every answer sits under “powered by AI”. it gets to know you, a little every day. it answers from your path's lessons (and from any tradition's own texts, if you ask about it), remembers only what you tap to keep, and never connects you with strangers.

kids

nothing is made for kids to use alone. younger kids come in with a parent, on the parent's phone. parents see everything they see. we don't sell a young person's faith to anyone — see Privacy.

off the app

nights aren't running yet. when they come, they'll be hosted only by institutions and organizers we've checked — a real place, a real record, a real person responsible for the room. every event will be 18+. reporting a host from the app is coming with them; a reported host will be paused until we've looked.

if you're struggling

if things are heavy right now, please talk to someone: a friend, a clergy member, a doctor. you don't have to carry it alone. infinite hill is a daily practice, not care. if you write something that sounds like you're in danger, the app shows you 988 (US), your local emergency number, and other ways to reach a person. the path will be right where you left it, whenever you're ready.

infinite hill is a company (Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc.), so it can pay its own way and keep infinite hill free. part of what it earns goes to the infinite hill foundation, a separate nonprofit (in formation).

If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.

infinite hill teaches from public-domain scripture, checked by scholars of each tradition. It is not a substitute for a congregation, a clergy member, or medical or mental-health care. Off-app gatherings, once they start, will be hosted only by verified institutions and organizers; all off-app events will be 18+.
