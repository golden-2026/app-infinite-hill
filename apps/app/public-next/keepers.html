the keeper desk.

the people who make sure we get it right.

every lesson in infinite hill is written by us and checked by a Keeper before it goes live. a Keeper is a scholar of that tradition: someone who has spent their career studying its texts, and whom people inside it and outside it respect. some are also ordained. what they share is that they know the sources, and they can say no. one desk per path, plus a science seat that checks every number we put on a page.

what a keeper does

reads every lesson. flags what's wrong, too early, too much, or not theirs. sends it back. we fix it or we cut it. the Keeper's job is "is it right," not "is it solemn" — if a lesson is accurate and a kid laughs, that's a pass.

what a keeper can do that we can't

veto. a Keeper can pull any lesson from their path, and we can't overrule it.

what a keeper never does

sell anything. nobody on a desk will ever be asked to promote anything. ever.

who's on it

the keepers are scholars from inside each tradition. each one checks their path's lessons before they go live.

want to help us get it right?

if you teach, lead, or study a faith and think we've got something wrong, thank you. we really want to hear it. see Contact.
