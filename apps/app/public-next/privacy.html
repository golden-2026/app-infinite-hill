privacy.

we don't sell your faith. that's the whole policy; the rest is how.

infinite hill is made by Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc. when this page says “we,” that’s who it means.

what "we don't sell your faith" means

which path you walk, what you watch, how often you come back, what you ask the Guide — none of it is sold, shared, rented or traded to anyone, ever. not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to religious institutions, not to your Keeper. this will be written into the legal policy with those words, and it can't be changed without telling you first.

no account. it lives on your phone.

there's no sign-up and no account. your path, your streak, your cards, your journal, what you live by and what your guide remembers about you all stay on your phone.

your guide

when you ask your guide something, your question and the last few lines of your chat go to our server and to claude (made by anthropic) to be answered. so the answer fits you, it can include which path you're on and what your guide remembers about you. it's never logged and never sold.

ads

the free plan may show ads: only between lessons, never during a film or a prayer, never targeted by your faith. the paid plans are ad-free.

what leaves your phone

only two things. your guide questions, as above. and an anonymous return count: the day you first opened infinite hill and which day you came back. no name, no id, no path, nothing you wrote.

reminders never leave your phone: "remind me" just adds a daily event to your own calendar. no location. no contacts. no microphone. no camera.

friends

inviting a friend shares a link straight from your phone. we don't see who you send it to, and nobody sees your path, your answers or your journal.

your data, your call

see everything your guide remembers about you in You, and forget one line or all of it, any time. because it all lives on your phone, deleting the app deletes it, for real, not "deactivated."

this is the plain-English version. the legal version is being written by an actual lawyer and will be here before launch. where the two differ, the legal one wins — and we'll fix this one.
