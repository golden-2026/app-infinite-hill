the infinite hill foundation.

if you give inside infinite hill, we don't keep a cent of it.

the infinite hill foundation is a separate nonprofit (in formation — papers first, promises second). every donation made anywhere in infinite hill will go to it, and from there to the places it's meant for: the institutions our Keepers come from, scholarships so a kid or a family who can't pay gets the year anyway, and the free student and youth-group plans.

the company gives too

infinite hill is a company, on purpose (Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc.). and part of what it earns goes to the foundation, not only what you give. once the paid plans open, we'll say exactly how much.

0%, forever

infinite hill the company takes nothing from a gift. no fee, no "processing," no float. the foundation is in formation. when giving opens, every cent of a gift goes there.

every gift gives twice

give someone a year, and a year goes to someone who can't pay. when giving opens, we'll publish the count.

why a company at all, then?

because a business that funds its own mission never has to ask anyone to keep the lights on. infinite hill is free, and will stay free because the company pays its own way. the foundation handles what should be given, and a share of what the company earns goes there too. paying our own way is how we keep that promise.
