terms.

the short version. the long version is being written by a lawyer and will be here before launch.

these terms are between you and Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc., the company that makes infinite hill. when they say “we” or “us,” that’s who they mean.

infinite hill is free. every path, every story, every lesson. it stays free.

paid plans are planned yearly or monthly subscriptions — not on sale during the pilot, when everything is free. once they launch: cancel in one tap; monthly stops at the end of the month, yearly runs out the year.

the lessons are written by infinite hill from public-domain texts, and checked by a Keeper of each religion before they go live. they're ours; the scriptures are everyone's. please don't scrape, resell or republish the lessons.

what infinite hill isn't: a congregation, a clergy member, medical or mental-health care, or a substitute for any of them. we're not affiliated with any religious institution.

off-app events aren't running yet. when they are, they'll be run by their hosts, not by us, and they'll be 18+. our part will be checking hosts and listing nights; the room is theirs.

be kind. treat each other better. in the app too.
