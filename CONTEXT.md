# Golden

Golden is a house for short daily religious and spiritual practice. This glossary keeps product language consistent across the universal App, the public Website, and the systems that support them.

## Language

**App**:
The universal Golden product built with Expo and React Native for iOS, Android, and installable web. The web build is a progressive web app and shares product behavior with the native builds.
_Avoid_: Mobile site, web app, native app when referring to the product as a whole

**Website**:
The separate public Golden marketing experience that explains the product and deep-links people into the App.
_Avoid_: App, landing page when referring to the complete public site

**Table**:
A private group of invited adult Golden members who can see agreed practice signals and encourage one another. Child profiles and guardian relationships are outside the first feature-complete release.
_Avoid_: Family account, household when the group may include unrelated adults

**Member**:
An adult with a recoverable Golden identity created through Apple, Google, or an email magic link. Anonymous local progress may be transferred into a Member account.
_Avoid_: User, account, subscriber when referring to the person

**Guest**:
An adult using Golden anonymously on one device before becoming a Member.
_Avoid_: Anonymous account

**Practice Signal**:
The limited status a Member may share with a Table: whether today's practice is complete and the shared streak contribution. It excludes lesson answers, detailed history, notes, and Guide conversations.
_Avoid_: Activity, progress data

**Sun**:
A lightweight encouragement one Table member sends to another without opening a conversation thread.
_Avoid_: Like, reaction

**The House**:
Golden's free plan: the complete core daily lesson and practice, personal progress, one Table, and limited Guide use. It is not supported by advertising in the first feature-complete release.
_Avoid_: Free tier

**Plus**:
Golden's paid individual plan with offline access, extended readings, unlimited Guide use, advanced review, and richer personal history.
_Avoid_: Premium

**The Table Plan**:
Golden's paid group plan providing Plus access and Table features for up to six adult Members.
_Avoid_: Family plan

**Guide**:
Golden's curriculum-grounded reference assistant. It answers from approved lessons and cited tradition sources, does not retain conversations by default, and does not act as clergy, therapy, or crisis care.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, companion, spiritual advisor

**Golden Studio**:
The private workspace where authorized writers, Keepers, rights reviewers, and publishers move lesson revisions through approval and release.
_Avoid_: CMS, admin panel

**Lesson Revision**:
An immutable version of one lesson's teaching, practice, sources, and production materials that receives its own review and release evidence.
_Avoid_: Draft version

**Keeper Approval**:
Recorded approval of one exact Lesson Revision by an authorized reviewer for that door. Approval does not automatically include voice or recording rights.
_Avoid_: Content approved

**Practice Reminder**:
An optional push or email prompt scheduled at a Member's fixed local time or device-calculated sunset, subject to their timezone and quiet-hour settings.
_Avoid_: Notification when referring specifically to the daily practice prompt

**Transactional Message**:
A service message required for identity, payment, security, or a Table invitation. It is controlled separately from Practice Reminders.
_Avoid_: Reminder

**Golden Hour Night**:
A Golden-listed, host-operated gathering for adults that has passed Golden's host and event review. Golden supports discovery and RSVP but does not operate the gathering.
_Avoid_: Golden event

**Host**:
An approved institution or organizer responsible for a Golden Hour Night, its venue, attendees, and conduct.
_Avoid_: Partner, venue

**Entitlement**:
The current access a Member has to The House, Plus, or The Table Plan after Supabase verifies the relevant Stripe lifecycle event.
_Avoid_: Subscription status, paid flag

**Gift Claim**:
A redeemable grant of Golden access purchased for another adult without creating an account or revealing the recipient's activity to the giver.
_Avoid_: Gift account, gift subscription
