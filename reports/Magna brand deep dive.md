# Magna wins loud with one color

Magna is a magnesium drink mix started in July 2024 by Everlane's founder. It looks loud, but the system behind it is tight and simple. Almost everything is black, white and one acid lime-yellow (**#F1FF67**). The headlines are big, blocky and confident. Every new flavor is run like a sneaker drop: a tease, a reveal, a real-life moment at a cool café or run club, then behind-the-scenes posts. It also keeps three separate voices. Instagram is playful culture with almost no health claims. The website is proof and benefits. Paid ads push hard to sell. Its loudest move so far is a set of LA billboards for its creatine product. Each one is an all-caps, first-person line in the style of a meme or affirmation ("STRENGTH FINDS ME IN EVERY TIMELINE"), set over a strange, confident fashion photo. For Infinite Hill this matters because Magna proves a small brand can feel like the most confident thing in the room without a celebrity budget or a comedy mascot. The shock comes from confidence, surprise and scene, not from making fun of anyone. Magna is also smaller than it looks: about **70.7K Instagram followers**, no press coverage of its billboards, and only modest out-of-home spending. Its playbook is cheap enough to copy.

## The 10 things that matter most

1. **One neon color, used sparingly, carries the whole brand.** Black (#000000) and white make up the base. Lime #F1FF67 is saved for buttons, badges, the scrolling top bar and billboard headlines ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)).
2. **Big blocky type does the shouting, not jokes.** The main font is Lineto's Riforma, a heavy, plain font, with a "Bull" display font and a hand-marker font for scribbled notes ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)).
3. **Every launch is a four-beat drop.** Tease, then reveal, then a real-life moment, then behind-the-scenes and user posts. Shirley Temple ran the whole arc in about three weeks (Oct 12 to Nov 4, 2026) ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)).
4. **Fill-in-the-blank teasers get people guessing before launch.** "YOUR BODY IS A __ __ Give us your best guess. Coming this week." ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397)).
5. **Billboards are first-person meme-style lines at full volume.** All-caps lime headlines over confident, absurd fashion photos, with the product tucked in a corner ([Instagram @drinkmagna](https://www.instagram.com/drinkmagna/)).
6. **Instagram sells the mood, not the claims.** Social is puns, nostalgia and the LA scene. Science lives on the site, and hard selling lives in the ads ([Runneth ad library](https://runneth.com/library/drink-magna)).
7. **Coolness is borrowed from real scenes.** Silver Lake run clubs, independent cafés, a design hotel, an Olympic marathoner. The partners bring the venue and the crowd ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)).
8. **Fashion-level photo shoots, with full credit rolls.** Posts name the photographer, models, stylist and crew like film credits, which signals "this is culture" ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/magna/shirley-bts.-team-work-makes-the-dream-work-9501397-f2c422c9)).
9. **The scarcity is real.** A "SOLD OUT" badge, a café special that ends on a date, subscribers getting in two hours early. No fake countdowns ([try.drinkmagna.com](https://try.drinkmagna.com/products/magna)).
10. **Old idea, new object.** Hydration is old ("Gatorade has been doing this successfully for decades"). Magna won by looking like a fashion object, even borrowing from cigarette packs, instead of looking like its category ([Dieline](https://thedieline.com/some-days-magna-design-is-a-very-modern-take-on-sports-drinks/); [Taste Radio](https://www.tasteradio.com/episodes/how-everlanes-founder-is-fashioning-success-in-cpg)).

## A Nike-trained studio built a black, white and lime system

Some Days, the studio behind the identity, was founded in Berkeley in 2017 by Steve Reinmuth. Its long-time client is **Nike**, along with Converse and Jordan ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/some-days-graphic-design-discover-050623)). Its own credit line (checked by the coordinator in Google Images results) covers "brand identity, visual system, art direction, and packaging." Dieline called the result "a very modern take on sports drinks," aimed at younger millennials and Gen Z ([Dieline](https://thedieline.com/some-days-magna-design-is-a-very-modern-take-on-sports-drinks/)). The founder, Michael Preysman, has said the pack takes cues from a cigarette brand. The point is that you carry it and show it like a status object, not like medicine ([Taste Radio](https://www.tasteradio.com/episodes/how-everlanes-founder-is-fashioning-success-in-cpg)).

The color counts in the website's code show the discipline. **Black is used 64 times, white 52 and lime #F1FF67 21 times**, with stone greys (#cdcfce) for section backgrounds. Single flavor accents appear only rarely: pink #ff5381, amber #ffb81c, sky blue #86bee2 and red #ff2a14 ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)). Buttons are black or lime, and on hover they flip to black with lime text. Badges ("NEW", "BEST SELLER", "SOLD OUT") are lime pills with black text. The logo is a blocky lowercase "magna" wordmark in black, and product names follow the same lowercase pattern ("magna +creatine"). The recurring devices are easy to borrow: a scrolling announcement bar, huge-number stat blocks ("75% Americans don't get enough magnesium"), a bar chart with Magna's own bar highlighted, checkmark trust lines, and a row of certification icons ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)).

On shelf the box flips the system. The packs are **bright yellow-lime with a red-and-black wordmark and big fruit photos**, sold as single-serve stick packs. At Target a 10-count is **$16.99** and a 3-count Shirley Temple trial pack is **$5**. Online, 28 servings cost **$39 a month** on subscription ([Target](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-94931978); [try.drinkmagna.com](https://try.drinkmagna.com/products/magna)). Flavor names moved from fruit descriptions in 2024 (Lemon Lime, Peach Mango) to drinks you grew up with in 2025-26 (Apple Juice, Island Punch, Blue Ice, Shirley Temple). The retro feeling lives in the names, while the pack stays modern ([Wellworthy](https://wellworthy.com/magna-magnesium-powered-hydration/)). One caution: the Target listing shows Lemon Lime on clearance, and one weak affiliate page questions how widely Target stocks it. The size of the "every Target" rollout is unconfirmed ([Target](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-94931978)).

## The website is a single long scroll that ends every block in a button

The homepage runs top to bottom like this. First, a lime scrolling bar ("Introducing Shirley Temple", "magna +creatine Now Available"). Then a video hero with the headline "**Triple-Magnesium Electrolytes that hit harder, taste better, and work smarter.**" and the line "The nostalgic icon, reimagined for what your body needs now." Then come flavor cards ("Tastes Great. Works Greater."), a Target block ("Find Us In Every Target."), four benefit cards ("Not Just Hydration. A Full Body Upgrade."), big science numbers, a competitor bar chart ("3x More Magnesium Than Our Competitors", 210mg vs 60/50/26/0mg), a reviews wall ("3,000+ FIVE STAR REVIEWS", looping customer videos), a quality line ("Trusted by doctors, demanded by athletes."), a community strip of real events, and an FAQ ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)). Every block ends in a short button: "Shop Now", "Find Your Nearest Store", "Get Hydrated →". The page is built from swappable blocks, so the hero changes with each drop.

The voice is confident and benefit-first. It uses flips ("Tastes Great. Works Greater."), contrasts ("Not just X. A full Y."), clean negatives ("no spikes, just clean power", "without the crash") and warm reassurance ("There's no weird aftertaste, we promise.") ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)). The product page is subscription-first. A "Starter Kit" drops from $55 to $39 with a free bottle and a free "surprise flavor 2-pack". There is a 60-day money-back guarantee and "Update or cancel anytime" ([try.drinkmagna.com](https://try.drinkmagna.com/products/magna)). The surprise 2-pack is a quiet sampling engine for each new drop.

## Instagram runs a repeatable tease, reveal, real-life, credits loop

The profile is short and current: "Magnesium Powered Hydration / Play the Long Game™ / Shirley Temple out Now 🍒". The story highlights are **COMMUNITY, TARGET, STATS, THE LONG GAME** ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/drinkmagna/)). Posting comes in bursts. It was close to daily through the two Shirley weeks (Oct 12 to 23), then dropped to about two posts in the following fortnight. The same pattern showed up around the July iced-tea drop and the March Blue Ice drop ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/company/magna)).

Each launch follows the same order. The **tease** is a fill-in-the-blank ("YOUR BODY IS A __ __"), a dinner-party hint ("Table's set / Guest of honor arrives soon / Don't be late"), or a time-boxed drop ("Something special drops tomorrow at 11am PST, subscribers get early access at 9am PST. Sorry for the Teas."). The **reveal** is a reel with a one-line twist: "Your body is a Shirley Temple / The drink you grew up on, the version you grew into / Out now 🍒". **Real life** follows: "SHIRLEY TEMPLE MATCHA at @sua_superette 🍒 Available now until 10/25," with a free sample pack with every drink, and "SHIRLEY UNDER THE STARS @starpartysilverlake x @renegade_running ⭐️". Last come **credits and user posts**: "Shirley BTS. Team work makes the dream work 🍒" with a full film-style crew list, and "The Classic Shirley… Show us what you got 🍒" ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/data/companies/magna/assets/9501397); [Particl](https://www.particl.com/magna/shirley-bts.-team-work-makes-the-dream-work-9501397-f2c422c9)). The look of the grid, judged from image descriptions, is cherry red, cocktail-bar props and bold text over editorial models. It reads as a night out, not a gym.

The other two voices handle the selling. Meta ads ran **86 active ads with about 61 new versions a week**. The hooks are blunt: "WARNING THIS ELECTROLYTE MAY CAUSE EXCESSIVE PRODUCTIVITY", "Brain Fog? No More.", "Buy one get one FREE right now at @target" ([Runneth](https://runneth.com/library/drink-magna)). Keeping claims off the feed is what lets Instagram feel like a magazine. Compared with Liquid Death's comedy persona (about 7.2M Instagram followers), Magna plays smaller, more local and more real-life ([Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2026/01/12/liquid-death-social-marketing-strategy)). Shirley Temple itself was a crowded trend, since Poppi, 7UP and Slice all launched one ([Beverage Industry](https://www.bevindustry.com/articles/97928-slice-soda-shirley-temple)). Magna's edge was the headline, not the flavor.

## Real life is borrowed scenes plus one confident billboard push

Magna's physical world is small and built on partners. The community strip on its homepage lists **Renegade Running x Adidas (LA 2026), Softbar x Track Club (Brooklyn 2026), Cafe Tondo (LA), Session Training x PNTB Movement (NYC 2025) and The LINE Hotel (LA)** ([drinkmagna.com](https://www.drinkmagna.com)). For LA Marathon weekend in March 2026 it ran a shakeout run led by Olympian Deena Kastor with Asics and Silver Lake Track Club, a Café Tondo party with Magna cocktails ("DRINK MAGNA AND LIVE FOREVER"), and a recap post ("LA showed up… #playthelonggame") ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/magna/lam26-shakeout-run-announcement-9501397-49319267); [Particl](https://www.particl.com/magna/la-marathon-weekend-recap-and-new-flavor-launch-9501397-811e1f11)). A flavor preview at Café Tondo was announced with "This post will self destruct in 24 hours" ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/magna/new-flavor-announcement-9501397-8b28e0c4)). Wholesale is a simple form with a six-box minimum for cafés and studios ([Magna wholesale](https://www.drinkmagna.com/pages/wholesale)). Sampling runs through $4 two-packs handed out at sponsored events found through the onbrand marketplace ([onbrand](https://www.onbrand.com/magna)). Merch is thin: a $9 water bottle is the only item confirmed.

The billboards are the exception, and they are the boldest thing Magna has done. Magna's own Instagram announced them: "Speak it into existence. Magna Creatine is officially out in the wild. LA, tag us when you spot one." A credit post names photographer David Brandon Geeting, a stylist, and hair, makeup and nails artists ([Instagram @drinkmagna](https://www.instagram.com/drinkmagna/), coordinator-verified 2026-11-10). They ran on Outfront structures in LA. The headlines seen were "**STRENGTH FINDS ME IN EVERY TIMELINE**", "**PLEASE BE PATIENT! CREATINE ISN'T FINISHED WITH ME YET**", "**I AM PURE OF HEART AND I DESERVE TO BE BEAUTIFUL**", "**THE CREATINE YOU FEAR TO DRINK…**", "**I CAN HAVE THE POWER**" and "**CREATINE … EFFORTLESSLY**". The visual is acid lime condensed all-caps over absurd, confident editorial photos: a swimmer in a black one-piece hoisted overhead by a man, a shirtless man lounging among dumbbells, a woman on a motorbike. The stick pack sits small in a corner. The idea links to the product page line "Manifest your future in 30 days or less," because creatine builds up slowly ([drinkmagna.com/products/creatine](https://www.drinkmagna.com/products/creatine)). What is unknown: the agency, dates, number of boards and public reaction. No trade press covered the billboards. In the same LA season, Thorne ran a creatine campaign with Ciara ([MM+M](https://www.mmm-online.com/news/ciara-and-thorne-creatine-marketing/)). Magna's version stood out through attitude, not celebrity.

## How Infinite Hill models each move

The rule for every line below: the surprise comes from **confidence and the unexpected**, never from mocking a faith, a believer or a named rival, and never from romance. Finn speaks as "i". Lines stay warm first. Prayer is always offered as a choice. Each piece stays inside one faith. Cross-faith content lives only in "my own religion." Scarcity is only ever real.

| Magna move | Infinite Hill move |
|---|---|
| **One neon color, black base** (#F1FF67 only on buttons, badges, top bar, headlines) | **Website and app:** lock IH lime and black as the only loud pair. Lime goes only on the main button, badges, the scrolling bar and headlines; everything else is black, white and one grey. Each faith gets one small accent color, used the way Magna uses flavor colors. |
| **Heavy plain font plus a marker accent** | **Everywhere:** one heavy display font for all-caps headlines, plus one handwritten face only for Finn's scribbled notes ("i got you"). No third font. |
| **Scrolling top bar announcing drops** | **Website and app home:** a lime scrolling bar: "NEW EPISODE: THE NIGHT OUT · CELEBRITY GUIDE DROPS FRIDAY · NEW STAMP UNLOCKED IN [real city]". |
| **Long single-scroll homepage, a button after every block** | **Website:** hero film, then "what you get" (want first: calm after a blowup, a fresh start), then one episode preview, celebrity guides, real stamps, community, FAQ. Every block ends in one button ("Start day one →"). Keep it short, in line with the Magna-style short homepage rule. |
| **"Tastes Great. Works Greater." flips; "no spikes, no crash" negatives** | **Copy system:** "Feels like a show. Works like a practice." "No lecture. No guilt. Just five minutes that stay with you." Roast *stale faith apps* in general, never a faith or a named rival. |
| **Three voices** (feed = mood, site = proof, ads = sell) | **Social:** Instagram and TikTok are pure culture: episodes, Finn, scenes, music. **Site:** proof (real scholar Keepers, the science page, real reviews above a threshold). **Ads:** direct ("Had a blowup? Five minutes. Start here."). |
| **Four-beat drop** (tease → reveal → real life → credits) | **Drops:** every new episode, celebrity guide or stamp runs the arc. Tease: "THIS FRIDAY, FINN BRINGS SOMEONE YOU KNOW". Reveal: a reel. Real life: a partner venue night. Credits: a behind-the-scenes post with the full crew. |
| **Fill-in-the-blank teaser** ("YOUR BODY IS A __ __") | **Social:** "BEFORE YOU REPLY TO THAT TEXT, ______. Guess. Episode drops Thursday." Don't copy the "your body is a temple" pun style: never pun on sacred phrases. |
| **Subscribers get in two hours early** | **App:** streak-holders or members open new episodes first ("you're in at 9, everyone else at 11"). It's real, because the episode really is released in stages. |
| **Real SOLD OUT, café special "until 10/25"** | **IRL and drops:** physical passport stamps exist only at real events, so "stamp available at the [city] night only, Nov 21" is true. Never fake countdowns or invented member numbers. |
| **Café collab drink + free sample pack** | **IRL:** partner with one beloved independent café per city for a limited "Finn order", like a special drink for one week. The cup sleeve carries a QR code to day one and a Finn sticker. Christian-first cities and venues lead. |
| **Run club and night events** ("SHIRLEY UNDER THE STARS") | **IRL:** host "the night out" events, music-led with a live crowd and big visuals in the teamLab spirit, tied to one faith's episode. Partners bring the venue and crowd. No sing-alongs and no mic in anyone's hand; the prayer moment is clearly optional. |
| **Fashion shoots with full credit rolls** | **Social and site:** shoot Finn and real everyday people with editorial ambition. Post film-style credits. It signals culture, not "faith app." |
| **Meme/affirmation billboards in all-caps lime over absurd confident photos** | **Billboards:** first-person, all-caps lime lines in Finn's confidence, over surprising editorial photos (a sharp-dressed person calmly reading on a moving bus roof, Finn in the corner): "PATIENCE IS STILL WORKING ON ME." "I CAME FOR FIVE MINUTES. I LEFT CALMER." "MY STREAK IS 40 DAYS AND SO IS MY PEACE." "MAKING RELIGION YOURS AGAIN." App icon in the corner, never a crowd number. |
| **"Speak it into existence… tag us when you spot one"** | **Social plus billboards:** "Spot Finn in the wild. Tag us." Reposting real sightings turns billboards into user posts. |
| **"Play the Long Game" attitude line + highlight** | **Brand line and app:** pair "making religion yours again" with a streak attitude line in highlights ("ONE DAY AT A TIME, ON PURPOSE"). Progress lines always celebrate, and never say the hill never ends. |
| **Nostalgic names, modern pack** ("the drink you grew up on, the version you grew into") | **Episodes:** "The story you heard as a kid, the version you're ready for now." It fits returning Christians and second-generation users without mocking the tradition. |
| **Stick pack in yellow box, $5 trial** | **App and IRL:** a free "trial night" pass (a 3-episode starter) printed on a lime card handed out at events, plus a passport booklet as the physical object people carry and show. |

The biggest risk to watch is drift. Magna's 2026 copy is more mass-market and claim-heavy than its 2024 launch ([Particl](https://www.particl.com/company/magna)). Infinite Hill should hold its edge as it grows, and must not carry Magna's ad bluntness ("may cause excessive productivity") into spiritual promises it can't prove.

## Conclusion

Magna's lesson isn't "be loud." It is "be loud in one color, one font and one rhythm, then let real places and real people do the rest." The brand gets its swagger from restraint in the system and confidence in the headline. Its billboards say outrageous things in the first person, calmly, as if they were simply true. That is exactly the kind of shock Infinite Hill can use without mocking anything: Finn stating a bold inner truth at billboard size, over a picture you didn't expect.

The second insight is about cost. Magna's coolness comes mostly from borrowed scenes (a café's regulars, a run club's crowd) and from one well-shot campaign at a time, not from heavy media spending. For Infinite Hill, whose top unsolved problem is community, the drop arc plus partner venues plus real-only stamps is a ready-made, low-cost way to turn launches into gatherings. The open questions are worth checking before copying the billboard play: who made Magna's boards, how many ran, and whether they actually moved sales.
