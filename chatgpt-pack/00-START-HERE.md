## The idea in one breath
- Big idea: **"Step in to remember who you are. Step out to live it."**
- Tagline: **"making religion yours again."**
- A mobile app that feels like a calm, beautiful world (Duolingo meets Animal Crossing, for religion). When life gets loud, you step in, get grounded and guided, then step back into your life a little steadier.
- **You are the mascot**: a round yellow face, infinity-sign glasses, backwards black cap. Only this approved mascot, anywhere.
- **Your guide is a celebrity you already know** (e.g. Denzel Washington for Christianity) who talks with you (voice first), learns who you are and suggests your day. Never write that any celebrity or scholar is "signed" in anything shared outside the company.
- **Eight homes on one mountain**: Christianity, Catholicism, Judaism, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism, and "my own religion." Lead with the religion's name ("the home of Christianity"); the place names (Olive Grove, Marigold…) are small.
- A **scholar of each faith** (the Keepers) reviews every class.

## One day (5–8 minutes)
1. Arrive in the painted village. Your guide asks how you're arriving today (tap, type or talk).
2. Your guide suggests your day. It's always your choice.
3. A 5-minute class: your life → a real story from the religion → what the religion actually teaches → a question to answer.
4. A quiet moment at a place on the map (the waterfall, the gospel steps, festivals). Pray, breathe or just sit. Prayer is always optional.
5. Step out: one thing to carry into your real day; sometimes a pointer to a real church or temple nearby.

## Who it's for first
Adults first, especially parents. First 1,000: interfaith families who want to understand each other's faith and know what to teach their kids. Segment two: adults who drifted from religion but are curious. Marketing leads with Christian viewers.

## Rules that always apply
- Warm, kind, never preachy. Plain language.
- No AI ever speaks as God. AI teachers are clearly labeled, never presented as real or ordained clergy.
- No faces of Jesus, the prophets or the Sikh Gurus.
- Prayer is always a choice ("watch, learn or join in").
- Never invent testimonials, user numbers or statistics.
- Crisis care: danger or self-harm always points to 988 (US) or local emergency help.
- Keep the Infinite Hill Foundation "in formation"; never state a share of proceeds.
