INFINITE HILL FILM v14 — punchy script (about 3:10)

Please make a new cut of the Infinite Hill film from this script. Reuse the v12 shots wherever listed (by number) and only generate the new shots marked NEW. Same art style, same mascot (black cap worn backwards). Our main viewer is a Christian family in America: lead with familiar Christian moments, and show the other faiths warmly alongside. Miriam narrates, warm but with more energy and pace than before: short lines, a smile in her voice. Music: one continuous, upbeat, joyful track, steady level, dipping a little under her voice. Every change of scene is a quick dissolve or a camera move, never a hard jump. No shot longer than 4 seconds unless she is still talking over it.

1. COLD OPEN (0:00-0:06) Fast, joyful montage, 1 second each: gondola (13), the mascot in a gondola cabin (14), rowing past floating lamps (19), hiking with friends (22), the summit circle (56).
   Miriam: "What if learning about faith felt like this?"

   Miriam: "Infinite Hill is a world you visit every day, to learn the world's religions, one story at a time."
   Card: "infinite hill. a world for every faith."

   NEW: 2 seconds each: a grandmother and grandchild saying grace at a candlelit dinner table; a church congregation singing together; a family lighting Shabbat candles; a family lighting a lamp at home (01).
   Miriam: "For thousands of years, every culture built the same thing: a way to find meaning, get through hard days, and be okay, together."
   The teen alone on his phone (04).
   Miriam: "Then we traded it for a feed. And the world got louder, angrier, more divided."
   Quick, 2 seconds each: the couple from two faiths at the kitchen table (05), the child asking at dinner (06), the young man praying (08).
   Miriam: "Now we have two religions at home and don't know what to teach our kids. They ask questions we can't answer. We say prayers we don't understand."
   THE HEART OF THE FILM: hold here. The young man looks up from his prayer; dissolve to the two kids from different lands meeting on the trail and smiling (28). Music lifts.
   Miriam (slower, every word landing): "We're divided. We're confused. But we can learn, together."
   Card: "we can learn, together." (hold 3 seconds)

4. EIGHT HOMES, SIDE BY SIDE (0:42-1:04) The labeled map, the camera gliding from badge to badge in this order as each is named: Olive Grove, Angelus, Pomegranate, Date Palm, Marigold, Lotus Pond, Nectar Lake, Wildwonder.
   Miriam: "So we built Infinite Hill. One mountain, with every faith side by side. Olive Grove, for Christianity. Angelus, Catholicism. Pomegranate, Judaism. Date Palm, Islam. Marigold, Hinduism. Lotus Pond, Buddhism. Nectar Lake, Sikhism. And Wildwonder, if you're building your own path. You don't need a religion to come in."

5. MORE ALIKE THAN WE THINK (1:04-1:14) Three kindness moments, 3 seconds each: sharing food (24), holding a door for a grandmother (26), two kids from different lands laughing on the trail (28).
   Miriam: "Because every faith on the mountain landed on the same rule: treat people the way you want to be treated."
   Card: "more alike than we think."

   breathe (39 waterfall): "Breathe at the waterfall."
   meditate (40 grove): "Sit in the grove."
   pray, if you want (NEW: the mascot at the open doors of a warm stone church at dusk, candles inside; then 41 temple): "Step into a church, a synagogue, a mosque, a temple."
   listen (NEW: a joyful gospel choir in robes singing on an outdoor stage; then 47 Torah rapper, 48 Sufi orchestra): "Catch a gospel choir, a Torah rapper and a Sufi orchestra, all in one night."
   share (43 storyteller's fire): "Hear stories by the fire."
   play (44 tea house): "Play the daily puzzle."
   celebrate (NEW: a festival street at night: a Christmas tree and Christmas lights first, then a Hanukkah menorah in a window, Eid lanterns and Diwali lamps along the same street, families laughing together): "Christmas, Hanukkah, Eid, Diwali. A festival almost every month."
   climb (NEW: the mascot and friends climbing a long zig-zag trail lit by lanterns at dawn toward a small stone chapel on a peak): "Hike by lantern light to a mountaintop chapel."
   visit (50 gondola, 51 blue door): "And ride the gondola to a friend's faith."
   Card at the start: "then the mountain is yours."

   Miriam: "Your whole family is on the mountain. See where everyone is, and light a lantern for grandma."
