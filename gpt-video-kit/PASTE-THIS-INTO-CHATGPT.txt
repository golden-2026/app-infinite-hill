PART 1: WHAT INFINITE HILL IS
=====================================================
Infinite Hill is a calm, beautiful iPad game where you learn the world's religions and practice them, a few minutes a day. You land in a magical world on a hill, a guide gets to know you, and every day you do three small things: read a sign on the street, go to class for a short lesson, and be still in a quiet place (meditation, a sound bath). Then you come home to your family. It's like a spiritual person's day, aimed at lasting peace, love and happiness, not school all day.

Tagline: "every faith on one hill, more alike than we think."
Eight paths (called "doors"): Hinduism, Buddhism, Christianity, Catholicism, Judaism, Islam, Sikhism, and "my own religion" (for people with no religion, who build their own from the best of every tradition).

What's behind it (real, already built):
- Five years of daily lessons on all eight paths (about 14,300 lessons). Every lesson tells a real story from the tradition, names a big teaching, then ties it to modern life.
- A friend character on each path who texts you a real-life problem that tests what you learned.
- Family features: everyone at home walks their own path and sees each other's light. Answers parents can give when their kids ask big questions.
- "Same idea, another door": once a week, you see how another religion says the same idea, with one honest difference.
- A guide you can ask anything, by voice.
