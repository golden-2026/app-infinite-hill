RUNWAY: HOW TO MAKE THE INFINITE HILL GAME VIDEO
=================================================
Runway makes video clips, not the playable game. Use it to turn our pictures into a moving demo / trailer.

SETUP
- Tool: Image to Video (Gen-4 or newest). Landscape 16:9, 10 seconds per clip.
- If your plan has "References", add folder 1-mascot/mascot-wave.webp as a character reference in every shot so the mascot stays the same.
- Upload the picture listed for each shot (folder 2-game-screens unless noted) and paste its prompt.
- Describe only motion. If faces, text or the game buttons warp, re-run, or add: "keep faces, text and on-screen interface stable."
- Do not generate real celebrities. The celebrity guide is an original stand-in character.

SHOT 1 · ARRIVAL · upload 2-game-screens/1-arrival.webp
Game camera behind the mascot. He takes a few steps up the stone path out of the glowing ring of light. Lanterns flicker, petals drift, the old lantern-keeper up the path raises his lantern and waves. Gentle forward camera follow. Keep the on-screen interface stable.

SHOT 2 · THE CELEBRITY GUIDE · upload 2-game-screens/1-arrival.webp
A warm, elegant woman in her 30s, an original character in modern-meets-traditional clothes, appears in a soft ring of golden light beside the mascot, smiles and gestures up the hill as if saying "i'll guide you". Soft sparkles, golden-hour light. Slow camera push in.

SHOT 3 · MEETING THE LANTERN-KEEPER · upload 2-game-screens/2-guide.webp
The lantern-keeper lifts his glowing lantern and smiles at the mascot; the lantern flame flickers, his shawl moves in a light breeze, the mascot tilts his head. Very slight camera drift. Keep the dialogue box and buttons stable.

SHOT 4 · THE CAMPFIRE · upload 2-game-screens/3-campfire.webp
Sunset by the campfire. Sparks rise slowly, the lantern-keeper gestures as he talks, the "path of your life" scroll glows softly on the right. Slow camera orbit. Keep the scroll and text stable.

SHOT 5 · THE MAP · upload 2-game-screens/4-map.webp
The map comes alive: soft glows pulse on each place, clouds drift past the summit, and the lime dotted path draws itself from the village square to the quiet grove. Slow overhead glide.

SHOT 6 · THE STREET OF SIGNS · upload 2-game-screens/5-street.webp
Game camera follows the mascot walking down the lantern-lit street at dusk. Lanterns sway, petals drift, the sparkles over the signs twinkle, he slows and looks up at the "what you do, you become" sign. Steady follow. Keep sign text stable.

SHOT 7 · CLASS, THE STORY · upload 2-game-screens/7-school.webp
The teacher tells a story and the glowing painting above the class comes alive like a moving illustration: the robber on the forest road walks toward the sage. The mascot and Priya lean in. Sunlight flickers through leaves. Slow pan. Keep the quiz panel stable.

SHOT 8 · CLASS, THE TURN · upload 2-game-screens/7-school.webp
In the glowing painting above the teacher, the robber sits still while an anthill slowly grows around him, then he becomes a poet writing under the tree. The teacher smiles; the "action" answer button glows lime. Slow camera push in.

SHOT 9 · THE QUIET GROVE · upload 2-game-screens/6-grove.webp
The mascot sits still with eyes closed among glowing singing bowls. The lime breathing ring in the center slowly grows and shrinks, soft rings of light ripple from the bowls, bamboo sways, fireflies drift. Very slow push in. Calm.

SHOT 10 · HOME · upload 2-game-screens/8-home.webp
Evening at the family table. The lanterns on the "our lights this week" board light up one by one, grandma laughs, the kids lean in, fire crackles. Slow pull back.

SHOT 11 · FESTIVAL GLIMPSE · upload 4-scenes/hindu-scene-diwali.webp
Diwali night: lamps flicker into light one after another, colors glow, sparklers shimmer, a family smiles. Slow camera drift.

SHOT 12 · CLOSING · upload 2-game-screens/4-map.webp
Slow aerial rise above the whole hill as day turns to night, the eight houses of worship and every path lighting up with lanterns, stars appearing. Majestic and calm.
