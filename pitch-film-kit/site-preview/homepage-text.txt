=== faq ===
infinite hill curious?
FAQs
Is it free?
yes. everything is free during the pilot, and the daily class and the mountain stay free. later, a paid plan will add extras, and we'll show the price before anyone pays a cent.
We're an interfaith family. Which door?
both. the table (a planned family plan) holds different doors at one table. the kids can walk two. nobody has to pick.
Do I have to pick a religion?
no. pick a door, or my own religion — no tradition attached. change anytime.
Can my school use it?
soon. a teacher tries it free for a term; the school licenses it for less than the textbook it replaces; students never pay. a faith school teaches its own door; any other school teaches religious literacy across all eight. classroom codes, assignments, a progress view, a certificate at every lookout. launching after the pilot.
Can I create my own religion?
yes. that's my own religion: walk it a little every day, take seven days on something on your mind, or wander through a different tradition each day. and on any door, you can visit another for a session — a friend's, a partner's, your mother-in-law's. every visit is one door, taught properly, by its own texts and people. the sessions never blend.
Is infinite hill a nonprofit?
no. it's a company (Infinite Hill Ventures, Inc.), so it can pay its own way and keep the house free. part of what it earns goes to the infinite hill foundation, a separate nonprofit (in formation). and when giving opens, anything you give inside the app will go there in full.
Who checks the lessons?
a keeper of that one tradition, a scholar who knows it from the inside. there's more about the keepers above.
Do I have to start from the beginning?
no. a couple of minutes of quick questions puts you where you belong, anywhere in the first four years. nobody starts from zero twice.
How long is it?
year one is twelve months of classes, one part of your life each month: fresh starts, worry, anger, gratitude, joy and more. years two to five go deeper: the texts, the history, the saints and the sources. the mountain is open the whole time.
Is it a video game?
not really. it's a five-minute class and a few quiet practices a day, set in a world that's a joy to walk around. you move between places with a tap. no fighting, no grinding, no ads.
Do I have to pray?
no. prayer is always your choice. in the chapels, synagogues, mosques and temples on the hill, you can watch, learn what it means, or join in.
What happens if I miss a day?
life happens, and that's okay. a rest day covers it. your streak grows one lesson a day, with rest days built in, because every tradition knows rest. you start with two and earn one back every week. if a streak does break, two lessons in a day within three days earns it back, never for money. your days on the hill and your place on the path never reset.
Is it for kids?
everyone. under-13s join through a parent's table, on the parent's phone. no messages, no strangers, no ads.
