WHAT THIS VERSION MUST GET ACROSS, IN ORDER
1. Why religion: it's the oldest technology on earth for being okay, together, and we traded it for a feed. Infinite Hill is making religion yours again.
2. One human race, one hill: every faith on one massive mountain, each on its own face, with a gondola between them, and at the summit, the whole human race together in peace, and they're more alike than we think. Every religion landed on the golden rule.
3. The voices are famous people who truly follow these faiths. It matters to them.
4. What you actually do: every day starts in class (a story and a few questions). Then the hill is yours: breathe, meditate, pray if you want, read, music, stories, games, family. Everything after class is the viewer's choice; nothing is required. Five minutes a day on your phone. It is NOT a game where you wander around doing nothing.
5. The surprises on the hill: music from people you'd never expect (devotion doesn't check your passport).
6. Your whole family is on the hill with you, and you can see where everyone is.
7. The offer, and leave the world a little kinder.
