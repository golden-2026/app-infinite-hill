Infinite Hill · investor film · production pack

The film: making religion yours again.

A 3½-minute film for investors. It tells the story first (the problem, why now), then shows the world: you're known, you choose where to go, you learn, you practice, you feel better, you grow, you do it together. It ends with what's already built and the brand.

How this gets made (what each tool is good at)

  • Runway makes the moving footage: the world, the mascot, the guide, the stories. One shot at a time, 5–10 seconds each, from a reference picture plus a prompt (below). It can't read our files or make a pitch deck, and words inside AI video come out garbled, so no text is generated in Runway.
  • The words (stats, taglines, logo) are finished brand cards I made: folder B-cards (full-screen) and C-supers (lower-third lines with transparent backgrounds).
  • Voice: a narrator (ElevenLabs or a human voice actor), plus Denzel's lines once his voice is recorded or licensed. Until then, a placeholder narrator reads his lines.
  • Music: one warm cinematic track that builds: piano and strings, a hint of tabla and choir as the faiths come together.
  • The edit happens in Runway's editor (or CapCut / Premiere): footage, cards, supers, voice, music.

Runway settings for every shot

  • Image to Video (Gen-4 or the newest model), 16:9, 10 seconds. Where a shot has no reference picture, first make a still with Runway's image tool using the same prompt, then animate it.
  • References: add the listed pictures from folder A-references. Always include model-turnaround.webp when the mascot appears, so he stays on model.
  • Paste the style line at the start of every prompt:
Stylized 3D animated film, the warmth of Pixar with the calm wonder of Sky: Children of the Light. Golden-hour cinematic lighting, soft volumetric light, painterly textures, gentle camera, shallow depth of field. Peaceful and awe-inspiring. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

Our mascot: round sunny-yellow face, infinity-symbol glasses, black cap worn backwards, cream "infinite hill" hoodie, black joggers, white sneakers with yellow stripes, white cartoon gloves.

Before this goes to an investor

  • Denzel: his face and voice appear only if his written approval covers an AI-made likeness and voice in a pitch film. Runway may also block a public figure's face; if it does, keep his real photo on a card and his real recorded voice.
  • No other celebrity names, faces or voices until each one is signed.
  • Sacred figures: no faces for prophets or Sikh Gurus (many Muslims and Sikhs consider that disrespectful). The shots below use scenes, light and practice instead.
  • Numbers on the cards are real and sourced; the faith-app figures are reported by those companies and press, as labeled.
  • Label it "concept film" in the corner or credits, since it isn't gameplay yet.
