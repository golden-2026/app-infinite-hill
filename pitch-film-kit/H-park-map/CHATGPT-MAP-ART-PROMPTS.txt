STYLE FOR EVERYTHING
Bright, warm, hand-painted theme-park map style, like the classic Disneyland park map, with the soft golden-hour light of our existing Infinite Hill images. Packed with tiny joyful details: little people walking, lanterns, flags, musicians playing. No words, letters, signs or labels anywhere (I'll add the names myself). No faces of Jesus, prophets or Sikh Gurus. Deities only as temple statues.

PROMPT 1: THE WHOLE MOUNTAIN (square, 1:1)
Seen from high above, looking almost straight down at one big mountain with one snowy summit in the center. The mountain is divided into eight colorful lands around it like slices, each covering one face, separated by rivers, ridges and trees. A winding trail circles the whole mountain, and a gondola line with glowing cabins crosses over the summit from land to land. At the very top, above a ring of clouds, a round stone terrace with golden lanterns where a crowd of people sit together in sunrise light.
Going clockwise from the top:
- Olive Grove (blue tones): olive trees, a lakeside fishing village with boats, a white chapel with a choir on its steps.
- Angelus (purple): a stone cathedral with bell towers, a candlelit chapel, a winding pilgrim trail with scallop shells.
- Pomegranate (teal): stone lanes and a busy market, a synagogue, pomegranate trees, a path through a parted sea with walls of water.
- Date Palm (green): date palms, a mosque with a dome and minaret, a garden of tiled fountains, a lantern-lit market street.
- Marigold (orange): marigold garlands everywhere, river steps with floating lamps, a carved temple, a giant banyan tree, an open-air stage.
- Lotus Pond (gold): a big pond full of pink lotuses, a temple with a stupa, prayer flags strung across a pass, a raked-sand garden.
- Nectar Lake (red): a shining golden gurdwara in the middle of a still lake, a big open dining hall, musicians in a pavilion.
- Wildwonder (light green): a wildflower meadow, a domed observatory, a stone labyrinth, a treehouse, giant redwood trees.
Leave clear open space near the outer edge of each land for a name label.
