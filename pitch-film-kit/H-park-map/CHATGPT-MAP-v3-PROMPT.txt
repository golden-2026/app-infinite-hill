CHANGES

1. MAKE IT VAST. Zoom in so lands, trails and rivers run off all four edges, like a real ski-resort map. No floating island, no ring of clouds around the edges, no empty green ovals.

2. THE SUMMIT. At the very top, a glowing round stone terrace ringed with lanterns where people of every color and age sit together in sunrise light. Gondola lines with glowing cabins climb to it from every land.

3. IN EVERY LAND, three things that are easy to spot:
- a small open-air schoolhouse with a bell, where the main path enters the land
- a small open-air amphitheater with musicians playing
- a gondola station with a cabin leaving toward the summit

- Buddhist: a steep stone stairway lit by lanterns up a pointed peak, a tiny shrine at the top.

- Jewish: a flat-topped desert mesa with a winding snake path and ancient fortress ruins on top at sunrise.
- Christian: a trail up a rocky peak to a small stone chapel at sunrise.

- My own path: a forest trail through giant redwoods up to a lookout under the northern lights.
