PROMPT 1: THE WHOLE MOUNTAIN (trail map)

Make a beautiful illustrated resort trail map, in the style of a hand-painted Whistler or Swiss ski-resort map, warm golden-hour light, cozy and magical, same art style as my attached Infinite Hill images.

One single massive mountain seen from a high three-quarter angle, with one snowy summit. The mountain has several faces, and each face is home to one faith, with its own village and its own house of worship built into the slope: a white chapel, a stone cathedral, a synagogue, a mosque with a dome, a carved Hindu mandir, a Buddhist temple with a stupa, a white gurdwara with golden domes, and an open wildflower meadow. Each face has the same kinds of places: a little open-air schoolhouse at the foot of the face, a waterfall, a grove of trees, a storyteller's fire, a library, an open-air amphitheater with lanterns, a festival square, a tea house, cozy homes with lanterns.

A scenic gondola line with small glowing cabins crosses from face to face over the summit. A winding high trail circles the whole mountain, with tiny people walking and cross-country skiing between the faces. At the very top, above the clouds, the most majestic place on the mountain: a wide circle of golden sunrise light where people from every face sit together, every age and color, peaceful and joyful, with lanterns rising from every face below and gathering in the sky above them.

Make it feel like one mountain you could wander for a lifetime. No text, no labels, no words anywhere. No faces of Jesus, prophets or Sikh Gurus.
