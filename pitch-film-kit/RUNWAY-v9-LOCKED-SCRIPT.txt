Please rebuild the Infinite Hill film from this locked script. Keep the art style, mascot, music and Miriam's voice.

THE ONE RULE: picture and voice are locked together. Each numbered beat is ONE continuous shot (or a few cuts of the same scene), and the picture must show exactly what the narrator is saying at that moment. Cut on the narrator's pauses, never in the middle of a sentence. Use slow camera moves that carry into the next shot (a dissolve or a push through a doorway), not hard jumps.

ONE LOOK FOR THE SUMMIT, used every time it appears: the snowy peak rises above a sea of clouds, and at the very top is a wide round stone terrace ringed with lanterns, glowing gold. In the opening it is seen far away, glowing at the top of the mountain. At the end the mascot arrives on that same terrace. Same terrace, same lanterns, same peak.

ONE LOOK FOR THE MASCOT: our approved mascot, black cap worn backwards, round glasses, white hoodie. Never a forward cap.

Text on screen: only the cards listed below, always laid over moving footage, never on black. Around 3 minutes 15 seconds.

1. THE OLD WAY (0:00-0:16)
   Picture: grandma lighting the lamp with her granddaughter; slow dissolve to a family praying together; dissolve to a village gathering at dusk.
   Voice: "for five thousand years, every people on earth built the same thing: a way to sit down, get quiet, and remember what matters."

   Voice: "then we walked out, and replaced it with a feed." (pause) "was that the plan?"
