Infinite Hill · the journey

From the website to a guide who knows you

Step in to remember who you are. Step out to live it.

Five stops, with your guide beside you the whole way. Each one shows what it is, where it stands today, and what comes next.

  1. 1
    On the website

    Learn what it is, and meet your guide

    The film, the celebrity guides, the map of the eight homes. The guide starts asking what brings you here, so it can point you to the right door.

    LiveThe website and film are live. The guide on the site is still a promise; it doesn't talk with you yet.
    Next: a short guide chat on the site that hands off into the app.
  2. 2
    On your phone

    Download the app

    One app for iPhone and Android, made from the same code as today's web app.

    PartlyA web app works today. The store apps come after the first real-people test; no rebuild needed.
    Next: Apple and Google developer accounts when the test is done.
  3. 3
    Inside the world

    A small world you can walk around

    Your celebrity guide welcomes you. You walk your mascot between a set number of places: the class, the waterfall, the story fire, home, the festival square. Just you and your guide.

    Not yetOnly pictures and mockups so far. Needs a game developer.
    Next: a cheap ad test, then one indie developer builds the first home with 3–4 places.
  4. 4
    In class

    The story plays like a short film, with questions

    One to two minutes of moving scenes and a narrator. It pauses two or three times: what would you do, what happens next, what did it mean. Then a quick quiz so it sticks.

    StartedThe first moving scenes are done (the broken tusk). No full film with questions yet.
    Next: the son who came home as the first full story film. In progress now.
  5. 5
    Every day after

    Your guide keeps getting to know you

    It remembers what matters to you, plans your next day, suggests a place to go on the hill and a small step in real life, and walks you along your path.

    PartlyThe text Guide works in today's app. The celebrity voice comes later.
    Next: connect the guide to the world, so it can send you to a place.
The loop

Back to real life, and back again

After class you step out to try one small thing in your own life: a message, a walk, a quiet minute. Tomorrow you step back in, and your guide remembers how it went.

What we are building

  • A small, beautiful world with set places
  • Story films with questions and quizzes
  • Celebrity guides and a guide that learns you
  • Single player, with family touches later

What we are not building, for now

  • A big go-anywhere 3D game
  • Live multiplayer with strangers
  • Painted stills as the final stories
