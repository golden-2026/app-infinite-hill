# Wave 1 style bible

The owner: "We can't have the same look for every film" and "Need to have diversity." This file sets 25 art directions. Each spec's image prompts already end with the style's suffix, the faith's image rule and the base rule, so a prompt can be pasted into ChatGPT as it is.

## Rules every image keeps

- Vertical 9:16, with no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks in the picture. Captions go on in the edit.
- Nothing gory. Violence is shown only as a raised silhouette or a flash of light, never as harm.
- No faces of Jesus, the prophets or the Sikh Gurus. Show them from behind, as hands, as light or as a silhouette.
- Muslim (islam) story shots have no people at all: landscapes, objects, light, architecture and nature only, and no Arabic or scripture text. The "today" shot shows hands only.
- The style is bold; the subject stays reverent. A loud look never mocks a holy figure.
- One faith per film. Never compare faiths inside a film.

## How styles were given out

- No two back-to-back days in one faith share a style.
- On any one day number, no two faiths share a style (day 29 and day 30 each use eight different looks).
- Christian (14 new films) and Hindu (23 new films) each use at least 10 styles. The other faiths have only 2 to 6 new films; every one of those gets its own style, and their other days keep the look of the existing filmed story, so each month still mixes many looks.
- The style fits the culture: Kerala mural, Kalighat and miniature for Hindu stories; phulkari and miniature for Sikh history; retablo, mosaic and stained glass for Catholic prayers; Jewish papercut (cut-out) and woodblock for Torah stories; landscape-only watercolour and risograph for Muslim stories; Roman fresco for the Stoics.
- Two styles (`zellige`, `sumi`) are held for later waves.
