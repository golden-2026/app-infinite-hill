# Wave 1 summary: the first 30 days of all 8 faiths

This is a plan only. No images were made. `map.json` says, for each faith and day, whether an existing filmed story fits; `specs/<door>-<day>.json` holds a six-shot film plan for every day without one. Styles are in `STYLE-BIBLE.md`.

Reuse was strict: a film counts only if it tells the same story or teaches the same idea, it lives in the same faith home, and no film appears twice in a month. Most existing films were clearly made for these exact days, which is why Islam, Buddhism and the Catholic, Jewish and "my own religion" months need so few new ones. The Christian month has 14 gaps (mostly teaching days), and the Hindu month has 23, because the Hindu library was filmed before the current lesson plan.

## Days to make type-led, not filmed

- **Spiritual day 29 (Cato's last night):** the lesson tells a death. A spec is written with no death shown, but this day is better as a type-led day. If it's filmed, the end card must show 988 (US) or local emergency help.
- **Sikh day 9 (Ik Onkar):** the day is about one symbol, and ChatGPT draws Gurmukhi badly. The spec never shows the glyph. Better: type-led, with the real symbol set as type.
- **Worth thinking about:** Christian day 5 (church words) and Jewish day 3 (Yiddish words) are vocabulary days. Their specs work, but kinetic type alone might hit harder.

## Notes for the painter

- Paste each shot's `prompt` as it is: it already carries the style suffix and the faith's image rules.
- `caption` is the spoken or narration line, taken from the lesson's own story and teaching, with no new religious claims.
- `move` is the camera move for the edit.
- Shot 6 is always "today", modern life tied to the lesson. For Muslim films it shows hands only.
