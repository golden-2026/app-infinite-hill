concept c

the group

the idea

A small-group leader (church group, campus group, friends who already meet) drops the week's episode to their group with one tap. Everyone watches and picks on their own phone; Finn turns the group's split into a ready 45-minute night for the leader. At the meeting, a code from the leader's phone taps everyone in and stamps the real IRL stamp into the group passport.

why people come back

Because there's a room on Wednesday and people you know will be in it. The app gives the leader the hardest part (a great opener and a plan) for free, so the leader keeps dropping, and every drop is 5 to 12 people opening the app. The stamps are earned only by showing up, so they mean something, and a six-week season gives the group a finish line together.

biggest risk

Leaders and pastors are picky about theology and slow to adopt; if one episode feels off, the group drops us. RightNow Media, Alpha and YouVersion already serve small groups with video. We win only if our episodes and Finn's kit feel far more alive than theirs, and the content passes real scholar review.
