your people

three ways to make Infinite Hill something you do with people, not alone. each one is a clickable phone you can tap through. all inside one faith (Christian examples), no leaderboards, real stamps only for real rooms.

my bet

C, with B on top

The group is the only one that starts with people who already meet. One leader brings a whole room, the IRL stamp is earned honestly, and every week has a real reason to open the app. Drop night then becomes the loud weekly moment those groups (and crews) all share. The crew is the fallback for people with no group.
