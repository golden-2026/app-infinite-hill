concept b

drop night

the idea

Once a week, every Christian on Infinite Hill opens the same new episode at the same minute, like a sneaker drop or a premiere. You react live with stickers, then everyone answers the story's big question in 10 seconds and sees how the room split. Watching live earns a drop card and Finn fit; hosted watch parties in real rooms earn the real stamp.

why people come back

Appointment TV. A fixed time, a countdown, a teaser, and the feeling that everybody you know is opening the same thing right now. The live vote is the hook ("wait, most people said that?"), the drop card is the souvenir, and the IRL drop nights turn the app into a reason to leave the house on a Sunday night.

biggest risk

Live only feels big with a big crowd. Early on the room is small and the split can't be shown honestly (we never fake it), so the event can feel empty. It also needs a strong new episode every single week, which is a heavy content load. Best as a layer on top of a crew or group, not alone.
