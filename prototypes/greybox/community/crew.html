concept a

the crew

the idea

A crew of 3 to 6 friends (or one household) watches the same daily story on their own phones. Every story has a choice, and the crew sees each other's picks and one-line why, then reacts. The crew keeps one shared light; a full week lit unlocks a Finn fit for everyone at once.

why people come back

Because your friends' answers are waiting behind your own. You can't see what Sam picked until you pick, so curiosity pulls you in, and nobody wants to be the one who lets the crew's light go out (rest days and crew saves keep it kind, not guilt). The split itself is the hook: Alex admitting "i'm the older brother" is a better reason to open the app than any streak number.

biggest risk

It needs 3 friends who all want this. Most people won't have a ready crew in one faith, so the crew dies at 1 to 2 people. It is also close to what YouVersion "plans with friends" and BeReal-style group loops already do; the story choices and Finn fits have to feel far better to win.
