# The son who came home: story film plan

About 100 seconds of moving scenes (8-second Runway clips trimmed to about 6–7 s), a narrator, and three pauses where the viewer answers. Jesus is never shown; the narrator says "Jesus told a story". Text follows Luke 15:11-24 (WEB, public domain). Scholar review is still needed before anything is released.

## After the film: three quick questions

1. What did the son plan to ask his father for? (to be a hired worker ✓ / his money back / a feast)
2. What did the father do before the son could finish his speech? (called for a robe, ring and feast ✓ / asked him where the money went / told him to wait outside)
3. Christians read the father as a picture of whom? (God ✓ / a king / the older brother)

Then the lesson goes on as it does now: your life, the takeaway, the small step.
